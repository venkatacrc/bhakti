Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 13

अध्यायः १३ · क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञविभागयोगः — The Yoga of Distinguishing the Field and the Knower

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 35 verses.

Arjuna asks about prakṛti, puruṣa, the field and its knower, knowledge and the knowable. Krishna explains the body as the field (kṣetra), the Self as its knower (kṣetrajña), the virtues that constitute true knowledge, the all-pervading Brahman to be known, and how seeing the one Self in all beings leads to the supreme.

The Thirty-Five Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव क्षेत्रं क्षेत्रज्ञमेव च ।
एतद्वेदितुमिच्छामि ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं च केशव ॥ १॥
ప్రకృతిం పురుషం చైవ క్షేత్రం క్షేత్ర-జ్ఞమేవ చ ।
ఏతద్-వేదితుమిచ్ఛామి జ్ఞానం జ్ఞేయం చ కేశవ ॥ 1 ॥
prakṛtiṁ puruṣaṁ chaiva kṣetraṁ kṣetra-jñam eva cha |
etad veditum ichchhāmi jñānaṁ jñeyaṁ cha keśava || 1 ||
Words: prakṛtim (material nature), puruṣam (the enjoyer), cha (and), eva (indeed), kṣetram (the field of activities), kṣetra-jñam (the knower of the field), eva (even), cha (also), etat (this), veditum (to know), ichchhāmi (I wish), jñānam (knowledge), jñeyam (the goal of knowledge), cha (and), keśava (Krishna, the killer of the demon named Keshi)
Meaning: I wish to learn about Nature and the Spirit, the field and the knower of the field, knowledge and that which ought to be known, O Keśava.
Verse 2
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते ।
एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः ॥ २॥
ఇదం శరీరం కౌంతేయ క్షేత్రమిత్యభిధీయతే ।
ఏతద్-యో వేత్తి తం ప్రాహుః క్షేత్ర-జ్ఞ ఇతి తద్-విదః ॥ 2 ॥
idaṁ śarīraṁ kaunteya kṣetram ity abhidhīyate |
etad yo vetti taṁ prāhuḥ kṣetra-jña iti tad-vidaḥ || 2 ||
Words: idam (this), śarīram (body), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), kṣetram (the field of activities), iti (thus), abhidhīyate (is termed as), etat (this), yaḥ (one who), vetti (knows), tam (that person), prāhuḥ (is called), kṣetra-jñaḥ (the knower of the field), iti (thus), tat-vidaḥ (those who discern the truth)
Meaning: O son of Kunti, this body is referred to as the 'field'. Those who are versed in this call him who is conscious of it the 'knower of the field'."
Verse 3
क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम ॥ ३॥
క్షేత్ర-జ్ఞం చాపి మాం విద్ధి సర్వ-క్షేత్రేషు భారత ।
క్షేత్ర-క్షేత్ర-జ్ఞయోర్-జ్ఞానం యత్-తజ్-జ్ఞానం మతం మమ ॥ 3 ॥
kṣetra-jñaṁ chāpi māṁ viddhi sarva-kṣetreṣu bhārata |
kṣetra-kṣetrajñayor jñānaṁ yat taj jñānaṁ mataṁ mama || 3 ||
Words: kṣetra-jñam (the knower of the field), cha (also), api (only), mām (me), viddhi (know), sarva (all), kṣetreṣu (in individual fields of activities), bhārata (scion of Bharat), kṣetra (the field of activities), kṣetra-jñayoḥ (of the knower of the field), jñānam (understanding of), yat (which), tat (that), jñānam (knowledge), matam (opinion), mama (my)
Meaning: And, O scion of the Bharata dynasty, understand Me to be the 'Knower of the field' in all the fields. In My opinion, that is Knowledge which is the knowledge of the field and the knower of the field.
Verse 4
तत्क्षेत्रं यच्च यादृक् च यद्विकारि यतश्च यत् ।

స చ యో యత్-ప్రభావశ్-చ తత్-సమాసేన మే శృణు ॥ 4 ॥
tat kṣetraṁ yach cha yādṛk cha yad-vikāri yataś cha yat |

Words: tat (that), kṣetram (field of activities), yat (what), cha (and), yādṛk (its nature), cha (and), yat-vikāri (how change takes place in it), yataḥ (from what), cha (also), yat (what), saḥ (he), cha (also), yaḥ (who), yat-prabhāvaḥ (what his powers are), cha (and), tat (that), samāsena (in summary), me (from me), śṛṇu (listen)
Meaning: Hear from Me in brief about all that, what that field is and how it is; what its changes are, and what causes what effect; and who He is, and what His powers are.
Verse 5
ऋषिभिर्बहुधा गीतं छन्दोभिर्विविधैः पृथक् ।

బ్రహ్మ-సూత్ర-పదైశ్-చైవ హేతుమద్భిర్-వినిశ్చితైః ॥ 5 ॥

Meaning: It has been sung of in various ways by the Rsis, separately by the different branches of Vedic texts, and also by the rational and convincing sentences themselves which are indicative of and lead to Brahman.
Verse 6
महाभूतान्यहङ्कारो बुद्धिरव्यक्तमेव च ।

ఇంద్రియాణి దశైకం చ పంచ చేంద్రియ-గోచరాః ॥ 6 ॥
mahā-bhūtāny ahankāro buddhir avyaktam eva cha |

Words: mahā-bhūtāni (the (five) great elements), ahankāraḥ (the ego), buddhiḥ (the intellect), avyaktam (the unmanifested primordial matter), eva (indeed), cha (and), indriyāṇi (the senses), daśa-ekam (eleven), cha (and), pañcha (five), cha (and), indriya-go-charāḥ (the (five) objects of the senses)
Meaning: The great elements, egoism, intellect, and the Unmanifest itself; the ten organs and the one, and the five objects of the senses;
Verse 7
इच्छा द्वेषः सुखं दुःखं सङ्घातश्चेतनाधृतिः ।

ఇచ్ఛా ద్వేషః సుఖం దుఃఖం సంఘాతశ్-చేతనా-ధృతిః ।
ఏతత్-క్షేత్రం సమాసేన స-వికారముదాహృతమ్ ॥ 7 ॥
ichchhā dveṣaḥ sukhaṁ duḥkhaṁ saṅghātaś chetanā dhṛtiḥ |
etat kṣetraṁ samāsena sa-vikāram udāhṛtam || 7 ||
Words: ichchhā (desire), dveṣaḥ (aversion), sukham (happiness), duḥkham (misery), saṅghātaḥ (the aggregate), chetanā (the consciousness), dhṛtiḥ (the will), etat (all these), kṣetram (the field of activities), samāsena (comprise of), sa-vikāram (with modifications), udāhṛtam (are said)
Meaning: Desire, repulsion, happiness, sorrow, the aggregate (of body and organs), sentience, fortitude—this field, together with its modifications, has been spoken of briefly.
Verse 8
अमानित्वमदम्भित्वमहिंसा क्षान्तिरार्जवम् ।

Meaning: Humility, unpretentiousness, non-injury, forbearance, sincerity, service to the teacher, cleanliness, steadiness, and control of the body and its organs;
Verse 9
इन्द्रियार्थेषु वैराग्यमनहङ्कार एव च ।

Words: indriya-artheṣu (toward objects of the senses), vairāgyam (dispassion), anahankāraḥ (absence of egotism), eva cha (and also), janma (of birth), mṛtyu (death), jarā (old age), vyādhi (disease), duḥkha (evils), doṣa (faults), anudarśanam (perception)
Meaning: Non-attachment to objects of the senses, and also the absence of egotism; seeing the evil in birth, death, aging, diseases, and miseries;
Verse 10

Meaning: Non-attachment and absence of fondness with regard to sons, wives, homes, etc., and constant equanimity of the mind with regard to the attainment of both the desirable and the undesirable;

Meaning: And unwavering devotion to Me with single-minded concentration; inclination to retreat to a clean place; lack of delight in a crowd of people;
Verse 12
अध्यात्मज्ञाननित्यत्वं तत्त्वज्ञानार्थदर्शनम् ।

Meaning: Steadfastness in the knowledge of the Self, contemplation on the Goal of the knowledge of Reality—this is spoken of as Knowledge. Ignorance is that which is other than this.
Verse 13
ज्ञेयं यत्तत्प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वाऽमृतमश्नुते ।

Meaning: I shall speak of that which is to be known, by realizing which one attains immortality. The supreme Brahman is without any beginning; that is called neither being nor non-being.
Verse 14

Verse 17

భూత-భర్తృ చ తజ్-జ్ఞేయం గ్రసిష్ణు ప్రభవిష్ణు చ ॥ 17 ॥
avibhaktaṁ cha bhūteṣu vibhaktam iva cha sthitam |
bhūta-bhartṛ cha taj jñeyaṁ grasiṣṇu prabhaviṣṇu cha || 17 ||
Words: avibhaktam (indivisible), cha (although), bhūteṣu (amongst living beings), vibhaktam (divided), iva (apparently), cha (yet), sthitam (situated), bhūta-bhartṛ (the sustainer of all beings), cha (also), tat (that), jñeyam (to be known), grasiṣṇu (the annihilator), prabhaviṣṇu (the creator), cha (and)
Meaning: The Knowable, though undivided, appears to exist as divided in all beings, and It is the sustainer, devourer, and originator of all beings.
Verse 18
ज्योतिषामपि तज्ज्योतिस्तमसः परमुच्यते ।
ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं ज्ञानगम्यं हृदि सर्वस्य विष्ठितम् ॥ १८॥

జ్ఞానం జ్ఞేయం జ్ఞాన-గమ్యం హృది సర్వస్య విష్ఠితమ్ ॥ 18 ॥

Words: jyotiṣām (in all luminarie), api (and), tat (that), jyotiḥ (the source of light), tamasaḥ (the darkness), param (beyond), uchyate (is said (to be)), jñānam (knowledge), jñeyam (the object of knowledge), jñāna-gamyam (the goal of knowledge), hṛdi (within the heart), sarvasya (of all living beings), viṣṭhitam (dwells)
Meaning: That is the Light of all lights; It is spoken of as beyond darkness. It is Knowledge, the Knowable, and the Known. It is specially situated in the hearts of all.
Verse 19

మద్-భక్త ఏతద్-విజ్ఞాయ మద్-భావాయోపపద్యతే ॥ 19 ॥

Meaning: Thus, in brief, the field, Knowledge, and the Knowable have been spoken of. By understanding this, My devotee becomes qualified for My state.
Verse 20
प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव विद्ध्यनादी उभावपि ।

వికారాంశ్-చ గుణాంశ్-చైవ విద్ధి ప్రకృతి-సంభవాన్ ॥ 20 ॥

vikārānś cha guṇānś chaiva viddhi prakṛti-sambhavān || 20 ||
Words: prakṛtim (material nature), puruṣam (the individual souls), cha (and), eva (indeed), viddhi (know), anādī (beginningless), ubhau (both), api (and), vikārān (transformations (of the body)), cha (also), guṇān (the three modes of nature), cha (and), eva (indeed), viddhi (know), prakṛti (material energy), sambhavān (produced by)
Meaning: Know both Nature and the individual soul to be verily without beginning; know the modifications as well as the qualities to be born of Nature.
Verse 21
कार्यकारणकर्तृत्वे हेतुः प्रकृतिरुच्यते ।
पुरुषः सुखदुःखानां भोक्तृत्वे हेतुरुच्यते ॥ २१॥

పురుషః సుఖ-దుఃఖానాం భోక్తృత్వే హేతురుచ్యతే ॥ 21 ॥
kārya-kāraṇa-kartṛtve hetuḥ prakṛtir uchyate |

Words: kārya (effect), kāraṇa (cause), kartṛtve (in the matter of creation), hetuḥ (the medium), prakṛtiḥ (the material energy), uchyate (is said to be), puruṣaḥ (the individual soul), sukha-duḥkhānām (of happiness and distress), bhoktṛtve (in experiencing), hetuḥ (is responsible), uchyate (is said to be)
Meaning: With regard to the source of the body and its organs, Nature is said to be the cause; the soul is the cause so far as the enjoyment of happiness and sorrow is concerned.
Verse 22
पुरुषः प्रकृतिस्थो हि भुङ्क्ते प्रकृतिजान्गुणान् ।
कारणं गुणसङ्गोऽस्य सदसद्योनिजन्मसु ॥ २२॥
పురుషః ప్రకృతి-స్థో హి భుంక్తే ప్రకృతి-జాన్-గుణాన్ ।
కారణం గుణ-సంగోఽస్య సదసద్-యోని-జన్మసు ॥ 22 ॥
puruṣaḥ prakṛti-stho hi bhuṅkte prakṛti-jān guṇān |
kāraṇaṁ guṇa-saṅgo ’sya sad-asad-yoni-janmasu || 22 ||
Words: puruṣaḥ (the individual soul), prakṛti-sthaḥ (seated in the material energy), hi (indeed), bhuṅkte (desires to enjoy), prakṛti-jān (produced by the material energy), guṇān (the three modes of nature), kāraṇam (the cause), guṇa-saṅgaḥ (the attachment (to three guṇas)), asya (of its), sat-asat-yoni (in superior and inferior wombs), janmasu (of birth)
Meaning: Since the soul is seated in Nature, therefore it experiences the qualities born of Nature. Contact with the qualities is the cause of its births in good and evil wombs.
Verse 23

परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः ॥ २३॥

పరమాత్మేతి చాప్యుక్తో దేహేఽస్మిన్-పురుషః పరః ॥ 23 ॥
upadraṣṭānumantā cha bhartā bhoktā maheśvaraḥ |
paramātmeti chāpy ukto dehe ’smin puruṣaḥ paraḥ || 23 ||
Words: upadraṣṭā (the witness), anumantā (the permitter), cha (and), bhartā (the supporter), bhoktā (the transcendental enjoyer), mahā-īśvaraḥ (the ultimate controller), parama-ātmā (Superme Soul), iti (that), cha api (and also), uktaḥ (is said), dehe (within the body), asmin (this), puruṣaḥ paraḥ (the Supreme Lord)
Meaning: He who is the Witness, the Permitter, the Sustainer, the Experiencer, the great Lord, and who is also spoken of as the transcendental Self, is the supreme Person in this body.
Verse 24

Meaning: He who knows thus the Person, Nature, and Qualities will not be born again, no matter how he may live.
Verse 25

అన్యే సాంఖ్యేన యోగేన కర్మ-యోగేన చాపరే ॥ 25 ॥
dhyānenātmani paśyanti kechid ātmānam ātmanā |
anye sānkhyena yogena karma-yogena chāpare || 25 ||

Meaning: Some realize the Self in their intellect through meditation, others through Sankhya-Yoga, and still others through Karma-Yoga, with the help of the internal organ.
Verse 26
अन्ये त्वेवमजानन्तः श्रुत्वाऽन्येभ्य उपासते ।
तेऽपि चातितरन्त्येव मृत्युं श्रुतिपरायणाः ॥ २६॥

తేఽపి చాతితరంత్యేవ మృత్యుం శ్రుతి-పరాయణాః ॥ 26 ॥
anye tv evam ajānantaḥ śrutvānyebhya upāsate |
te ’pi chātitaranty eva mṛtyuṁ śruti-parāyaṇāḥ || 26 ||
Words: anye (others), tu (still), evam (thus), ajānantaḥ (those who are unaware (of spiritual paths)), śrutvā (by hearing), anyebhyaḥ (from others), upāsate (begin to worship), te (they), api (also), cha (and), atitaranti (cross over), eva (even), mṛtyum (death), śruti-parāyaṇāḥ (devotion to hearing (from saints))
Meaning: Others, again, who do not know thus, take to thinking after hearing from others; they too, who are devoted to hearing, certainly overcome death.
Verse 27
यावत्सञ्जायते किञ्चित्सत्त्वं स्थावरजङ्गमम् ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञसंयोगात्तद्विद्धि भरतर्षभ ॥ २७॥
యావత్-సంజాయతే కించిత్-సత్త్వం స్థావర-జంగమమ్ ।
క్షేత్ర-క్షేత్రజ్ఞ-సంయోగాత్-తద్-విద్ధి భరతర్షభ ॥ 27 ॥

Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, whatever object, moving or non-moving, comes into existence, know that to be from the combination of the field and the Knower of the field!
Verse 28
समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु तिष्ठन्तं परमेश्वरम् ।

వినశ్యత్స్వవినశ్యంతం యః పశ్యతి స పశ్యతి ॥ 28 ॥

Meaning: He who sees the Supreme Lord existing truly in all beings, and the Imperishable among the perishable, sees indeed.
Verse 29
समं पश्यन्हि सर्वत्र समवस्थितमीश्वरम् ।

Meaning: Since, by truly seeing God, who is present everywhere alike, he does not injure the Self by the Self, he therefore attains the supreme Goal.
Verse 30
प्रकृत्यैव च कर्माणि क्रियमाणानि सर्वशः ।

Verse 31

yadā bhūta-pṛthag-bhāvam eka-stham anupaśyati |

Words: yadā (when), bhūta (living entities), pṛthak-bhāvam (diverse variety), eka-stham (situated in the same place), anupaśyati (see), tataḥ (thereafter), eva (indeed), cha (and), vistāram (born from), brahma (Brahman), sampadyate ((they) attain), tadā (then)
Meaning: When one realizes that the state of diversity of living things is rooted in the One, and that their manifestation also comes from That, then one becomes identified with Brahman.
Verse 32
अनादित्वान्निर्गुणत्वात्परमात्मायमव्ययः ।

శరీర-స్థోఽపి కౌంతేయ న కరోతి న లిప్యతే ॥ 32 ॥
anāditvān nirguṇatvāt paramātmāyam avyayaḥ |
śarīra-stho ’pi kaunteya na karoti na lipyate || 32 ||
Words: anāditvāt (being without beginning), nirguṇatvāt (being devoid of any material qualities), parama (the Supreme), ātmā (soul), ayam (this), avyayaḥ (imperishable), śarīra-sthaḥ (dwelling in the body), api (although), kaunteya (Arjun, the the son of Kunti), na (neither), karoti (acts), na (nor), lipyate (is tainted)
Meaning: Being without beginning and without end, O son of Kunti, this immutable, supreme Self does not act, nor is It affected, although It exists in the body.
Verse 33
यथा सर्वगतं सौक्ष्म्यादाकाशं नोपलिप्यते ।

సర్వత్రావస్థితో దేహే తథాఽఽత్మా నోపలిప్యతే ॥ 33 ॥

Words: yathā (as), sarva-gatam (all-pervading), saukṣmyāt (due to subtlety), ākāśam (the space), na (not), upalipyate (is contaminated), sarvatra (everywhere), avasthitaḥ (situated), dehe (the body), tathā (similarly), ātmā (the soul), na (not), upalipyate (is contaminated)
Meaning: As the all-pervading space is not defiled, due to its subtlety, similarly the Self, present everywhere in bodies, is not defiled.
Verse 34
यथा प्रकाशयत्येकः कृत्स्नं लोकमिमं रविः ।

క్షేత్రం క్షేత్రీ తథా కృత్స్నం ప్రకాశయతి భారత ॥ 34 ॥
yathā prakāśayaty ekaḥ kṛtsnaṁ lokam imaṁ raviḥ |
kṣetraṁ kṣetrī tathā kṛtsnaṁ prakāśayati bhārata || 34 ||
Words: yathā (as), prakāśayati (illumines), ekaḥ (one), kṛtsnam (entire), lokam (solar system), imam (this), raviḥ (sun), kṣetram (the body), kṣetrī (the soul), tathā (so), kṛtsnam (entire), prakāśayati (illumine), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat)
Meaning: As the single sun illuminates this entire world, similarly, O descendant of the Bharata dynasty, the Knower of the field illuminates the entire field.
Verse 35
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा ।
भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम् ॥ ३५॥
క్షేత్ర-క్షేత్ర-జ్ఞయోరేవమంతరం జ్ఞాన-చక్షుషా ।
భూత-ప్రకృతి-మోక్షం చ యే విదుర్-యాంతి తే పరమ్ ॥ 35 ॥
kṣetra-kṣetrajñayor evam antaraṁ jñāna-chakṣuṣā |
bhūta-prakṛti-mokṣaṁ cha ye vidur yānti te param || 35 ||
Words: kṣetra (the body), kṣetra-jñayoḥ (of the knower of the body), evam (thus), antaram (the difference), jñāna-chakṣuṣā (with the eyes of knowledge), bhūta (the living entity), prakṛti-mokṣam (release from material nature), cha (and), ye (who), viduḥ (know), yānti (approach), te (they), param (the Supreme)
Meaning: Those who, through the eye of wisdom, know the distinction between the field and the Knower of the field, and the annihilation of the Matrix of beings, reach the Supreme.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञविभागयोगो नाम त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the thirteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Kṣetra Kṣetrajña Vibhāga Yoga — the Yoga of Distinguishing the Field and the Knower.
