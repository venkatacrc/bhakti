Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 15

अध्यायः १५ · पुरुषोत्तमयोगः — The Yoga of the Supreme Person

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 20 verses.

Krishna describes the eternal aśvattha tree of saṃsāra — roots above, branches below — to be cut with the axe of non-attachment. He reveals Himself as the light in sun and moon, the fire of digestion, and the knower of the Vedas; then teaches the perishable, the imperishable, and the Supreme Person (Puruṣottama) beyond both.

chhandānsi yasya parṇāni yas taṁ veda sa veda-vit || 1 ||
Words: ūrdhva-mūlam (with roots above), adhaḥ (downward), śākham (branches), aśvattham (the sacred fig tree), prāhuḥ (they speak), avyayam (eternal), chhandānsi (Vedic mantras), yasya (of which), parṇāni (leaves), yaḥ (who), tam (that), veda (knows), saḥ (he), veda-vit (the knower of the Vedas)
Meaning: They say that the peepul tree, which has its roots upward and its branches downward, and of which the Vedas are the leaves, is imperishable. He who realizes it is a knower of the Vedas."

Verse 16
द्वाविमौ पुरुषौ लोके क्षरश्चाक्षर एव च ।
क्षरः सर्वाणि भूतानि कूटस्थोऽक्षर उच्यते ॥ १६॥
ద్వౌ ఇమౌ పురుషౌ లోకే క్షరశ్చాక్షర ఏవ చ ।
క్షరః సర్వాణి భూతాని కూటస్థోఽక్షర ఉచ్యతే ॥ ౧౬॥
dvāv imau puruṣau loke kṣaraś chākṣara eva cha |
kṣaraḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni kūṭa-stho ’kṣara uchyate || 16 ||
Words: dvau (two), imau (these), puruṣau (beings), loke (in creation), kṣaraḥ (the perishable), cha (and), akṣaraḥ (the imperishable), eva (even), cha (and), kṣaraḥ (the perishable), sarvāṇi (all), bhūtāni (beings), kūṭa-sthaḥ (the liberated), akṣaraḥ (the imperishable), uchyate (is said)
Meaning: There are two kinds of people in the world: the mutable and the immutable. The mutable consists of all things; the one existing as Maya is referred to as the immutable.
Verse 17
उत्तमः पुरुषस्त्वन्यः परमात्मेत्युदाहृतः ।
यो लोकत्रयमाविश्य बिभर्त्यव्यय ईश्वरः ॥ १७॥
ఉత్తమః పురుషః తు అన్యః పరమ-ఆత్మా ఇతి ఉదాహృతః ।
యో లోకత్రయమావిశ్య బిభర్త్యవ్యయ ఈశ్వరః ॥ ౧౭॥
uttamaḥ puruṣas tv anyaḥ paramātmety udāhṛtaḥ |
yo loka-trayam āviśya bibharty avyaya īśvaraḥ || 17 ||
Words: uttamaḥ (the Supreme), puruṣaḥ (Divine Personality), tu (but), anyaḥ (besides), parama-ātmā (the Supreme Soul), iti (thus), udāhṛtaḥ (is said), yaḥ (who), loka trayam (the three worlds), āviśya (enters), bibharti (supports), avyayaḥ (indestructible), īśvaraḥ (the controller)
Meaning: But the Supreme Person is different; He is spoken of as the transcendental Self, permeating the three worlds and upholding them, and He is the imperishable God.
Verse 18
यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः ।
अतोऽस्मि लोके वेदे च प्रथितः पुरुषोत्तमः ॥ १८॥
యస్మాత్క్షరమతీతోఽహమక్షరాదపి చోత్తమః ।
అతోఽస్మి లోకే వేదే చ ప్రథితః పురుష-ఉత్తమః ॥ ౧౮॥
yasmāt kṣaram atīto ’ham akṣarād api chottamaḥ |
ato ’smi loke vede cha prathitaḥ puruṣottamaḥ || 18 ||
Words: yasmāt (hence), kṣaram (to the perishable), atītaḥ (transcendental), aham (I), akṣarāt (to the imperishable), api (even), cha (and), uttamaḥ (transcendental), ataḥ (therefore), asmi (I am), loke (in the world), vede (in the Vedas), cha (and), prathitaḥ (celebrated), puruṣa-uttamaḥ (as the Supreme Divine Personality)
Meaning: Since I am transcendental to the mutable and above even the immutable, therefore I am well known in the world and in the Vedas as the supreme Person.
Verse 19
यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम् ।
स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत ॥ १९॥
యో మామేవమసమ్మూఢో జానాతి పురుష-ఉత్తమమ్ ।
స సర్వవిద్భజతి మాం సర్వభావేన భారత ॥ ౧౯॥
yo mām evam asammūḍho jānāti puruṣottamam |
sa sarva-vid bhajati māṁ sarva-bhāvena bhārata || 19 ||
Words: yaḥ (who), mām (me), evam (thus), asammūḍhaḥ (without a doubt), jānāti (know), puruṣa-uttamam (the Supreme Divine Personality), saḥ (they), sarva-vit (those with complete knowledge), bhajati (worship), mām (me), sarva-bhāvena (with one’s whole being), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat)
Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, he who, being free from delusion, knows Me, the supreme Person, thus, is all-knowing and adores Me with his whole being.
Verse 20
इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रमिदमुक्तं मयाऽनघ ।
एतद्बुद्ध्वा बुद्धिमान्स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत ॥ २०॥
ఇతి గుహ్యతమం శాస్త్రమిదముక్తం మయాఽనఘ ।
ఏతద్బుద్ధ్వా బుద్ధిమాన్స్యాత్కృతకృత్యశ్చ భారత ॥ ౨౦॥
iti guhyatamaṁ śāstram idam uktaṁ mayānagha |
etad buddhvā buddhimān syāt kṛta-kṛtyaś cha bhārata || 20 ||
Words: iti (these), guhya-tamam (most secret), śāstram (Vedic scriptures), idam (this), uktam (spoken), mayā (by me), anagha (Arjun, the sinless one), etat (this), buddhvā (understanding), buddhi-mān (enlightened), syāt (one becomes), kṛta-kṛtyaḥ (who fulfills all that is to be accomplished), cha (and), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat)
Meaning: O sinless one, I have thus uttered this most secret scripture. Understanding this, one becomes wise and fulfills their duties, O scion of the Bharata dynasty.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे पुरुषोत्तमयोगो नाम पञ्चदशोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the fifteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Puruṣottama Yoga — the Yoga of the Supreme Person.
