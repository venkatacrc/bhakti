Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 16

अध्यायः १६ · दैवासुरसम्पद्विभागयोगः — The Yoga of Distinguishing Divine and Demonic Destinies

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 24 verses.

Krishna contrasts divine and demonic natures — fearlessness, purity, and self-restraint versus hypocrisy, arrogance, and craving. He warns that desire, anger, and greed are the triple gateway to hell, and urges Arjuna to follow scripture as the guide of what to do and avoid.

The Twenty-Four Verses

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिः ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।
दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम् ॥ १॥
అభయం సత్త్వ-సంశుద్ధిః జ్ఞాన-యోగ-వ్యవస్థితిః ।
దానం దమః చ యజ్ఞశ్చ స్వాధ్యాయస్తప ఆర్జవమ్ ॥ ౧॥
abhayaṁ sattva-sanśuddhir jñāna-yoga-vyavasthitiḥ |
dānaṁ damaś cha yajñaś cha svādhyāyas tapa ārjavam || 1 ||
Words: abhayam (fearlessness), sattva-sanśuddhiḥ (purity of mind), jñāna (knowledge), yoga (spiritual), vyavasthitiḥ (steadfastness), dānam (charity), damaḥ (control of the senses), cha (and), yajñaḥ (performance of sacrifice), cha (and), svādhyāyaḥ (study of sacred books), tapaḥ (austerity), ārjavam (straightforwardness)
Meaning: Fearlessness, purity of mind, persistence in knowledge and yoga, charity, control of the external organs, sacrifice, scriptural study, austerity, and rectitude.

Meaning: Non-injury, truthfulness, absence of anger, renunciation, control of the inner self, absence of vilification, kindness to creatures, non-covetousness, gentleness, modesty, and freedom from restlessness;

Meaning: The divine nature is Liberation, while the demoniacal is considered to be an inevitable bondage. Do not grieve, O son of Pandu! You are destined to possess the divine nature.

Meaning: In this world, there are two kinds of creation of beings: the divine and the demoniacal. The divine has been spoken of elaborately. Hear about the demoniacal from Me, O son of Prtha.

ātma-sambhāvitāḥ stabdhā dhana-māna-madānvitāḥ |
yajante nāma-yajñais te dambhenāvidhi-pūrvakam || 17 ||
Words: ātma-sambhāvitāḥ (self-conceited), stabdhāḥ (stubborn), dhana (wealth), māna (pride), mada (arrogance), anvitāḥ (full of), yajante (perform sacrifice), nāma (in name only), yajñaiḥ (sacrifices), te (they), dambhena (ostentatiously), avidhi-pūrvakam (with no regards to the rules of the scriptures)
Meaning: Self-conceited, haughty, filled with pride and intoxicated by wealth, they perform sacrifices that are only in name, ostentatiously and without regard for the injunctions.
Verse 18
अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः ।
मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः ॥ १८॥
అహఙ్కారం బలం దర్పం కామం క్రోధం చ సంశ్రితాః ।
మామాత్మపరదేహేషు ప్రద్విషన్తోఽభ్యసూయకాః ॥ ౧౮॥
ahankāraṁ balaṁ darpaṁ kāmaṁ krodhaṁ cha sanśritāḥ |
mām ātma-para-deheṣu pradviṣanto ’bhyasūyakāḥ || 18 ||
Words: ahankāram (egotism), balam (strength), darpam (arrogance), kāmam (desire), krodham (anger), cha (and), sanśritāḥ (covered by), mām (me), ātma-para-deheṣu (within one’s own and bodies of others), pradviṣantaḥ (abuse), abhyasūyakāḥ (the demoniac)
Meaning: Resorting to egotism, power, arrogance, passion, and anger, hating Me in their own and others' bodies, they become envious by nature.
Verse 19
तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् ।
क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु ॥ १९॥
తానహం ద్విషతః క్రూరాన్సంసారేషు నరాధమాన్ ।
క్షిపామ్యజస్రమశుభానాసురీష్వేవ యోనిషు ॥ ౧౯॥
tān ahaṁ dviṣataḥ krūrān sansāreṣu narādhamān |
kṣipāmy ajasram aśubhān āsurīṣv eva yoniṣu || 19 ||
Words: tān (these), aham (I), dviṣataḥ (hateful), krūrān (cruel), sansāreṣu (in the material world), nara-adhamān (the vile and vicious of humankind), kṣipāmi (I hurl), ajasram (again and again), aśubhān (inauspicious), āsurīṣu (demoniac), eva (indeed), yoniṣu (in to the wombs)
Meaning: I cast forever those hateful, cruel, evil-doers in the worlds, the vilest of human beings, verily into the demonic classes.
Verse 20
असुरीं योनिमापन्ना मूढा जन्मनि जन्मनि ।
मामप्राप्यैव कौन्तेय ततो यान्त्यधमां गतिम् ॥ २०॥
అసురీం యోనిమాపన్నా మూఢా జన్మని జన్మని ।
మామప్రాప్యైవ కౌన్తేయ తతో యాన్త్యధమాం గతిమ్ ॥ ౨౦॥
āsurīṁ yonim āpannā mūḍhā janmani janmani |
mām aprāpyaiva kaunteya tato yānty adhamāṁ gatim || 20 ||
Words: āsurīm (demoniac), yonim (wombs), āpannāḥ (gaining), mūḍhāḥ (the ignorant), janmani janmani (in birth after birth), mām (me), aprāpya (failing to reach), eva (even), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), tataḥ (thereafter), yānti (go), adhamām (abominable), gatim (destination)
Meaning: Being born among the demoniacal species in birth after birth, the souls, without ever reaching Me, O son of Kunti, attain conditions lower than that.
Verse 21
त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः ।
कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत् ॥ २१॥
త్రివిధం నరకస్య ఇదం ద్వారం నాశనమ్ ఆత్మనః ।
కామః క్రోధస్తథా లోభస్తస్మాదేతత్త్రయం త్యజేత్ ॥ ౨౧॥
tri-vidhaṁ narakasyedaṁ dvāraṁ nāśanam ātmanaḥ |
kāmaḥ krodhas tathā lobhas tasmād etat trayaṁ tyajet || 21 ||
Words: tri-vidham (three types of), narakasya (to the hell), idam (this), dvāram (gates), nāśanam (destruction), ātmanaḥ (self), kāmaḥ (lust), krodhaḥ (anger), tathā (and), lobhaḥ (greed), tasmāt (therefore), etat (these), trayam (three), tyajet (should abandon)
Meaning: This door to hell, which destroys the soul, is of three kinds—passion, anger, and greed. Therefore, one should forsake these three.
Verse 22
एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः ।
आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ २२॥
ఏతైర్విముక్తః కౌన్తేయ తమోద్వారైస్త్రిభిర్నరః ।
ఆచరత్యాత్మనః శ్రేయస్తతో యాతి పరాం గతిమ్ ॥ ౨౨॥
etair vimuktaḥ kaunteya tamo-dvārais tribhir naraḥ |
ācaraty ātmanaḥ śreyas tato yāti parāṁ gatim || 22 ||
Words: etaiḥ (from this), vimuktaḥ (freed), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), tamaḥ-dvāraiḥ (gates to darkness), tribhiḥ (three), naraḥ (a person), ācharati (endeavor), ātmanaḥ (soul), śreyaḥ (welfare), tataḥ (thereby), yāti (attain), parām (supreme), gatim (goal)
Meaning: O son of Kunti, a person who is free from these three doors to darkness strives for the good of the soul; thereby, they attain the highest Goal.
Verse 23
यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः ।
न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम् ॥ २३॥
యః శాస్త్రవిధిముత్సృజ్య వర్తతే కామ-కారతః ।
న స సిద్ధిమవాప్నోతి న సుఖం న పరాం గతిమ్ ॥ ౨౩॥
yaḥ śāstra-vidhim utsṛjya vartate kāma-kārataḥ |
na sa siddhim avāpnoti na sukhaṁ na parāṁ gatim || 23 ||
Words: yaḥ (who), śāstra-vidhim (scriptural injunctions), utsṛjya (discarding), vartate (act), kāma-kārataḥ (under the impulse of desire), na (neither), saḥ (they), siddhim (perfection), avāpnoti (attain), na (nor), sukham (happiness), na (nor), parām (the supreme), gatim (goal)
Meaning: Ignoring the precepts of the scriptures, he who acts under the impulse of passion does not attain perfection, nor happiness, nor the supreme goal.
Verse 24
तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।
ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि ॥ २४॥
తస్మాచ్ఛాస్త్రం ప్రమాణం తే కార్యాకార్యవ్యవస్థితౌ ।
జ్ఞాత్వా శాస్త్రవిధానోక్తం కర్మ కర్తుమిహార్హసి ॥ ౨౪॥
tasmāch chhāstraṁ pramāṇaṁ te kāryākārya-vyavasthitau |
jñātvā śāstra-vidhānoktaṁ karma kartum ihārhasi || 24 ||
Words: tasmāt (therefore), śāstram (scriptures), pramāṇam (authority), te (your), kārya (duty), akārya (forbidden action), vyavasthitau (in determining), jñātvā (having understood), śāstra (scriptures), vidhāna (injunctions), uktam (as revealed), karma (actions), kartum (perform), iha (in this world), arhasi (you should)
Meaning: Therefore, the scripture is your authority as regards determining what should be done and what should not be done. After understanding your duty as presented by scriptural injunction, you ought to perform it here.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे दैवासुरसम्पद्विभागयोगो नाम षोडशोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the sixteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Daivāsura Sampad Vibhāga Yoga — the Yoga of Distinguishing Divine and Demonic Destinies.
