Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 9

अध्यायः ९ · राजविद्याराजगुह्ययोगः — The Yoga of the Royal Knowledge and the Royal Secret

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 34 verses.

Krishna discloses the most secret knowledge — He pervades all yet remains unattached; the deluded overlook His human form, while the great souls worship Him with love. Even a leaf, flower, fruit, or water offered with devotion He accepts; He is equal to all, and whoever takes refuge in Him reaches the supreme goal.

The Thirty-Four Verses

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥ १॥
ఇదం తు తే గుహ్యతమం ప్రవక్ష్యామ్యనసూయవే ।
జ్ఞానం విజ్ఞాన-సహితం యజ్-జ్ఞాత్వా మోక్ష్యసేఽశుభాత్ ॥ 1 ॥
idaṁ tu te guhyatamaṁ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave |
jñānaṁ vijñāna-sahitaṁ yaj jñātvā mokṣyase ’śubhāt || 1 ||
Words: idam (this), tu (but), te (to you), guhya-tamam (the most confidential), pravakṣyāmi (I shall impart), anasūyave (nonenvious), jñānam (knowledge), vijñāna (realized knowledge), sahitam (with), yat (which), jñātvā (knowing), mokṣyase (you will be released), aśubhāt (miseries of material existence)
Meaning: However, to you who are not given to cavilling, I shall speak of this highest secret itself, which is knowledge combined with experience, by realizing which you shall be free from evil.
Verse 2
राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् ।
प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम् ॥ २॥
రాజ-విద్యా రాజ-గుహ్యం పవిత్రమిదముత్తమమ్ ।
ప్రత్యక్షావగమం ధర్మ్యం సు-సుఖం కర్తుమవ్యయమ్ ॥ 2 ॥
rāja-vidyā rāja-guhyaṁ pavitram idam uttamam |
pratyakṣāvagamaṁ dharmyaṁ su-sukhaṁ kartum avyayam || 2 ||
Words: rāja-vidyā (the king of sciences), rāja-guhyam (the most profound secret), pavitram (pure), idam (this), uttamam (highest), pratyakṣa (directly perceptible), avagamam (directly realizable), dharmyam (virtuous), su-sukham (easy), kartum (to practice), avyayam (everlasting)
Meaning: This is the sovereign knowledge, the sovereign profundity, the best sanctifier; directly realizable, righteous, very easy to practice, and imperishable.
Verse 3
अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परन्तप ।
अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि ॥ ३॥
అశ్రద్దధానాః పురుషా ధర్మస్యాస్య పరంతప ।
అప్రాప్య మాం నివర్తంతే మృత్యు-సంసార-వర్త్మని ॥ 3 ॥
aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya parantapa |
aprāpya māṁ nivartante mṛtyu-samsāra-vartmani || 3 ||
Words: aśraddadhānāḥ (people without faith), puruṣāḥ ((such) persons), dharmasya (of dharma), asya (this), parantapa (Arjun, conqueror the enemies), aprāpya (without attaining), mām (me), nivartante (come back), mṛtyu (death), samsāra (material existence), vartmani (in the path)
Meaning: O destroyer of foes, those who are disregarding this Dharma (knowledge of the Self) certainly go round and round, without reaching Me, along the path of transmigration which is fraught with death.
Verse 4
मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना ।
मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः ॥ ४॥
మయా తతమిదం సర్వం జగదవ్యక్త-మూర్తినా ।
మత్-స్థాని సర్వ-భూతాని న చాహం తేష్వవస్థితః ॥ 4 ॥
mayā tatam idaṁ sarvaṁ jagad avyakta-mūrtinā |
mat-sthāni sarva-bhūtāni na chāhaṁ teṣvavasthitaḥ || 4 ||
Words: mayā (by me), tatam (pervaded), idam (this), sarvam (entire), jagat (cosmic manifestation), avyakta-mūrtinā (the unmanifested form), mat-sthāni (in me), sarva-bhūtāni (all living beings), na (not), cha (and), aham (I), teṣu (in them), avasthitaḥ (dwell)
Meaning: This whole world is pervaded by Me in My unmanifest form. All beings exist in Me, but I am not contained within them!
Verse 5
न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ।
भूतभृन्न च भूतस्थो ममात्मा भूतभावनः ॥ ५॥
న చ మత్స్థాని భూతాని పశ్య మే యోగమైశ్వరమ్ ।
భూతభృన్న చ భూతస్థో మమాత్మా భూతభావనః ॥ 5 ॥
na cha mat-sthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogam aiśwaram |
bhūta-bhṛn na cha bhūta-stho mamātmā bhūta-bhāvanaḥ || 5 ||
Words: na (never), cha (and), mat-sthāni (abide in me), bhūtāni (all living beings), paśya (behold), me (my), yogam aiśwaram (divine energy), bhūta-bhṛt (the sustainer of all living beings), na (never), cha (yet), bhūta-sthaḥ (dwelling in), mama (my), ātmā (self), bhūta-bhāvanaḥ (the creator of all beings)
Meaning: Neither do the beings dwell in Me. Behold My divine Yoga! I am the sustainer and originator of beings, but My Self is not contained within the beings.
Verse 6
यथाऽऽकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् ।
तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय ॥ ६॥
యథాఽఽకాశ-స్థితో నిత్యం వాయుః సర్వత్ర-గో మహాన్ ।
తథా సర్వాణి భూతాని మత్-స్థానీత్యుపధారయ ॥ 6 ॥
yathākāśa-sthito nityaṁ vāyuḥ sarvatra-go mahān |
tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni mat-sthānītyupadhāraya || 6 ||
Words: yathā (as), ākāśa-sthitaḥ (rests in the sky), nityam (always), vāyuḥ (the wind), sarvatra-gaḥ (blowing everywhere), mahān (mighty), tathā (likewise), sarvāṇi bhūtāni (all living beings), mat-sthāni (rest in me), iti (thus), upadhāraya (know)
Meaning: Understand thus that just as the voluminous wind, moving everywhere, is ever present in space, similarly all beings abide in Me.
Verse 7
सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम् ।
कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम् ॥ ७॥
సర్వ-భూతాని కౌంతేయ ప్రకృతిం యాంతి మామికామ్ ।
కల్ప-క్షయే పునస్-తాని కల్పాదౌ విసృజామ్యహమ్ ॥ 7 ॥
sarva-bhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṁ yānti māmikām |
kalpa-kṣaye punas tāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham || 7 ||
Words: sarva-bhūtāni (all living beings), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), prakṛtim (primordial material energy), yānti (merge), māmikām (my), kalpa-kṣaye (at the end of a kalpa), punaḥ (again), tāni (them), kalpa-ādau (at the beginning of a kalpa), visṛjāmi (manifest), aham (I)
Meaning: O son of Kunti, all the beings at the end of a cycle go back to My Prakrti. At the beginning of a cycle, I project them forth again.
Verse 8
प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः ।
भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात् ॥ ८॥
ప్రకృతిం స్వామవష్టభ్య విసృజామి పునః పునః ।
భూత-గ్రామమిమం కృత్స్నమవశం ప్రకృతేర్-వశాత్ ॥ 8 ॥
prakṛtiṁ svām avaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ |
bhūta-grāmam imaṁ kṛtsnam avaśaṁ prakṛter vaśāt || 8 ||
Words: prakṛtim (the material energy), svām (my own), avaṣṭabhya (presiding over), visṛjāmi (generate), punaḥ punaḥ (again and again), bhūta-grāmam (myriad forms), imam (these), kṛtsnam (all), avaśam (beyond their control), prakṛteḥ (nature), vaśāt (force)
Meaning: Keeping My own prakṛti under control, I project forth again and again this multitude of beings, which are powerless due to the influence of their own nature.
Verse 9
न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय ।
उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु ॥ ९॥
న చ మాం తాని కర్మాణి నిబధ్నంతి ధనంజయ ।
ఉదాసీన-వదాసీనమసక్తం తేషు కర్మసు ॥ 9 ॥
na cha māṁ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya |
udāsīna-vad āsīnam asaktaṁ teṣu karmasu || 9 ||
Words: na (none), cha (as), mām (me), tāni (those), karmāṇi (actions), nibadhnanti (bind), dhanañjaya (Arjun, conqueror of wealth), udāsīna-vat (as neutral), āsīnam (situated), asaktam (detached), teṣu (those), karmasu (actions)
Meaning: O Dhananjaya, those actions do not bind Me, for I remain unconcerned and unattached to them.
Verse 10
मयाऽध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् ।
हेतुनाऽनेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते ॥ १०॥
మయాఽధ్యక్షేణ ప్రకృతిః సూయతే స-చరాచరమ్ ।
హేతునాఽనేన కౌంతేయ జగద్-విపరివర్తతే ॥ 10 ॥
mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sa-charācaram |
hetunānena kaunteya jagad viparivartate || 10 ||
Words: mayā (by me), adhyakṣeṇa (direction), prakṛtiḥ (material energy), sūyate (brings into being), sa (both), chara-acharam (the animate and the inanimate), hetunā (reason), anena (this), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), jagat (the material world), viparivartate (undergoes the changes)
Meaning: Under Me as the supervisor, the Prakrti produces the world of the moving and the non-moving things. Owing to this, O son of Kunti, the world revolves.
Verse 11
अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् ।

అవజానంతి మాం మూఢా మానుషీం తనుమాశ్రితమ్ ।
పరం భావమజానంతో మమ భూత-మహేశ్వరమ్ ॥ 11 ॥
avajānanti māṁ mūḍhā mānuṣīṁ tanum āśritam |
paraṁ bhāvam ajānanto mama bhūta-maheśvaram || 11 ||
Words: avajānanti (disregard), mām (me), mūḍhāḥ (dim-witted), mānuṣīm (human), tanum (form), āśritam (take on), param (divine), bhāvam (personality), ajānantaḥ (not knowing), mama (my), bhūta (all beings), mahā-īśvaram (the Supreme Lord)
Meaning: Not knowing My supreme nature as the Lord of all beings, foolish people disregard Me, who have taken a human form.
Verse 12
मोघाशा मोघकर्माणो मोघज्ञाना विचेतसः ।
राक्षसीमासुरीं चैव प्रकृतिं मोहिनीं श्रिताः ॥ १२॥
మోఘాశా మోఘ-కర్మాణో మోఘ-జ్ఞానా విచేతసః ।
రాక్షసీమాసురీం చైవ ప్రకృతిం మోహినీం శ్రితాః ॥ 12 ॥
moghāśā mogha-karmāṇo mogha-jñānā vichetasaḥ |

Words: mogha-āśāḥ (of vain hopes), mogha-karmāṇaḥ (of vain actions), mogha-jñānāḥ (of baffled knowledge), vichetasaḥ (deluded), rākṣasīm (demoniac), āsurīm (atheistic), cha (and), eva (certainly), prakṛtim (material energy), mohinīm (bewildered), śritāḥ (take shelter)
Meaning: Possessed of vain hopes, vain actions, vain knowledge, and senselessness, they become verily possessed of the deceptive disposition of fiends and demons.
Verse 13
महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः ।
भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम् ॥ १३॥
మహాత్మానస్-తు మాం పార్థ దైవీం ప్రకృతిమాశ్రితాః ।
భజంత్యనన్య-మనసో జ్ఞాత్వా భూతాదిమవ్యయమ్ ॥ 13 ॥
mahātmānas tu māṁ pārtha daivīṁ prakṛtim āśritāḥ |
bhajantyananya-manaso jñātvā bhūtādim avyayam || 13 ||
Words: mahā-ātmānaḥ (the great souls), tu (but), mām (me), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), daivīm prakṛtim (divine energy), āśritāḥ (take shelter of), bhajanti (engage in devotion), ananya-manasaḥ (with mind fixed exclusively), jñātvā (knowing), bhūta (all creation), ādim (the origin), avyayam (imperishable)
Meaning: O son of Prtha, the noble ones, being possessed of divine nature, surely adore Me with single-mindedness, knowing Me to be the immutable source of all objects.
Verse 14
सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः ।
नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ॥ १४॥
సతతం కీర్తయంతో మాం యతంతశ్-చ దృఢ-వ్రతాః ।
నమస్యంతశ్-చ మాం భక్త్యా నిత్య-యుక్తా ఉపాసతే ॥ 14 ॥
satataṁ kīrtayanto māṁ yatantaś cha dṛḍha-vratāḥ |

Words: satatam (always), kīrtayantaḥ (singing divine glories), mām (me), yatantaḥ (striving), cha (and), dṛḍha-vratāḥ (with great determination), namasyantaḥ (humbly bowing down), cha (and), mām (me), bhaktyā (loving devotion), nitya-yuktāḥ (constantly united), upāsate (worship)
Meaning: Always glorifying Me and striving, the men of firm vows worship Me by paying obeisance to Me and being ever endowed with devotion.
Verse 15
ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामुपासते ।
एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम् ॥ १५॥
జ్ఞాన-యజ్ఞేన చాప్యన్యే యజంతో మాముపాసతే ।
ఏకత్వేన పృథక్త్వేన బహుధా విశ్వతో-ముఖమ్ ॥ 15 ॥
jñāna-yajñena chāpyanye yajanto mām upāsate |

Words: jñāna-yajñena (yajña of cultivating knowledge), cha (and), api (also), anye (others), yajantaḥ (worship), mām (me), upāsate (worship), ekatvena (undifferentiated oneness), pṛthaktvena (separately), bahudhā (various), viśwataḥ-mukham (the cosmic form)
Meaning: Others indeed worship Me by exclusively adoring through the sacrifice of the knowledge of oneness; (others worship Me) in many ways, and (others) as the multiform existing in various forms.
Verse 16

Meaning: I am the ritual, I am the sacrifice, I am the oblation, I am the medicinal herb, I am the mantra, I am the clarified butter, I am the fire, and I am the act of offering.
Verse 17

Words: pitā (Father), aham (I), asya (of this), jagataḥ (universe), mātā (Mother), dhātā (Sustainer), pitāmahaḥ (Grandsire), vedyam (the goal of knowledge), pavitram (the purifier), om-kāra (the sacred syllable Om), ṛk (the Rig Veda), sāma (the Sama Veda), yajuḥ (the Yajur Veda), eva (also), cha (and)
Meaning: Of this world, I am the father, mother, ordainer, and grandfather; I am the knowable, the sanctifier, the syllable Om, as well as the Rk, Sama, and Yajus.

मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः ॥ ३४॥
మన్-మనా భవ మద్-భక్తో మద్-యాజీ మాం నమస్కురు ।
మామేవైష్యసి యుక్త్వైవమాత్మానం మత్-పరాయణః ॥ 34 ॥
man-manā bhava mad-bhakto mad-yājī māṁ namaskuru |
mām evaiṣyasi yuktvaivam ātmānaṁ mat-parāyaṇaḥ || 34 ||
Words: mat-manāḥ (always think of me), bhava (be), mat (my), bhaktaḥ (devotee), mat (my), yājī (worshipper), mām (to me), namaskuru (offer obeisances), mām (to me), eva (certainly), eṣyasi (you will come), yuktvā (united with me), evam (thus), ātmānam (your mind and body), mat-parāyaṇaḥ (having dedicated to me)
Meaning: Having your mind fixed on Me, be devoted to Me, sacrifice to Me, and bow down to Me. By concentrating your mind and accepting Me as the supreme goal, you shall surely attain Me, who am thus the Self.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे राजविद्याराजगुह्ययोगो नाम नवमोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the ninth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Rāja Vidyā Rāja Guhya Yoga — the Yoga of the Royal Knowledge and the Royal Secret.
