Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 4

अध्यायः ४ · ज्ञानकर्मसंन्यासयोगः — The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation of Action

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 42 verses.

Krishna reveals the ancient lineage of this yoga and His divine births for the protection of dharma. He teaches action in knowledge — seeing inaction in action — the many forms of sacrifice, and the supremacy of jñāna that burns all karma like fire.

The Forty-Two Verses

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् ।
विवस्वान् मनवे प्राह मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत् ॥ १॥
ఇమం వివస్వతే యోగం ప్రోక్తవానహమవ్యయమ్ ।
వివస్వాన్ మనవే ప్రాహ మనురిక్ష్వాకవేఽబ్రవీత్ ॥ ౧॥
imaṁ vivasvate yogaṁ proktavān aham avyayam |
vivasvān manave prāha manur ikṣvākave ’bravīt || 1 ||
Words: imam (this), vivasvate (to the Sun-god), yogam (the science of Yog), proktavān (taught), aham (I), avyayam (eternal), vivasvān (Sun-god), manave (to Manu, the original progenitor of humankind), prāha (told), manuḥ (Manu), ikṣvākave (to Ikshvaku, first king of the Solar dynasty), abravīt (instructed)
Meaning: I imparted this imperishable Yoga to Vivasvan, who then taught it to Manu, who in turn transmitted it to Iksavaku.
Verse 2
एवं परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं राजर्षयो विदुः ।
स कालेनेह महता योगो नष्टः परन्तप ॥ २॥
ఏవం పరమ్పరా-ప్రాప్తమ్ ఇమం రాజర్షయో విదుః ।
స కాలేనేహ మహతా యోగో నష్టః పరన్తప ॥ ౨॥
evaṁ paramparā-prāptam imaṁ rājarṣayo viduḥ |
sa kāleneha mahatā yogo naṣṭaḥ parantapa || 2 ||
Words: evam (thus), paramparā (in a continuous tradition), prāptam (received), imam (this (science)), rāja-ṛṣayaḥ (the saintly kings), viduḥ (understood), saḥ (that), kālena (with the long passage of time), iha (in this world), mahatā (great), yogaḥ (the science of Yog), naṣṭaḥ (lost), parantapa (Arjun, the scorcher of foes)
Meaning: The king-sages knew this yoga, which was received in regular succession. That yoga, O destroyer of foes, is now lost due to a long lapse of time.
Verse 3
स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः ।
भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम् ॥ ३॥
స ఏవాయం మయా తేఽద్య యోగః ప్రోక్తః పురాతనః ।
భక్తోఽసి మే సఖా చేతి రహస్యం హ్యేతదుత్తమమ్ ॥ ౩॥
sa evāyaṁ mayā te ’dya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ |
bhakto ’si me sakhā cheti rahasyaṁ hyetad uttamam || 3 ||
Words: saḥ (that), eva (certainly), ayam (this), mayā (by me), te (unto you), adya (today), yogaḥ (the science of Yog), proktaḥ (reveal), purātanaḥ (ancient), bhaktaḥ (devotee), asi (you are), me (my), sakhā (friend), cha (and), iti (therefore), rahasyam (secret), hi (certainly), etat (this), uttamam (supreme)
Meaning: That ancient Yoga itself, which is this, has been taught to you by Me today, considering you to be My devotee and friend, for this Yoga is a profound secret.
Verse 4
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वतः ।
कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति ॥ ४॥
అపరం భవతో జన్మ పరం జన్మ వివస్వతః ।
కథమేతద్విజానీయాం త్వమాదౌ ప్రోక్తవానితి ॥ ౪॥
aparaṁ bhavato janma paraṁ janma vivasvataḥ |
katham etad vijānīyāṁ tvam ādau proktavān iti || 4 ||
Words: aparam (later), bhavataḥ (your), janma (birth), param (prior), janma (birth), vivasvataḥ (Vivasvan, the sun-god), katham (how), etat (this), vijānīyām (am I to understand), tvam (you), ādau (in the beginning), proktavān (taught), iti (thus)
Meaning: Your birth was later, whereas Vivasvan's birth was earlier. How am I to understand that You instructed him in the beginning?"
Verse 5
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन ।
तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप ॥ ५॥
బహూని మే వ్యతీతాని జన్మాని తవ చార్జున ।
తాన్యహం వేద సర్వాణి న త్వం వేత్థ పరన్తప ॥ ౫॥
bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava chārjuna |
tānyahaṁ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṁ vettha parantapa || 5 ||
Words: bahūni (many), me (of mine), vyatītāni (have passed), janmāni (births), tava (of yours), cha (and), arjuna (Arjun), tāni (them), aham (I), veda (know), sarvāṇi (all), na (not), tvam (you), vettha (know), parantapa (Arjun, the scorcher of foes)
Meaning: O Arjuna, many lives of Mine have passed, and so have yours. I know them all, but you do not know, O scorcher of enemies!"
Verse 6
अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् ।
प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय संभवाम्यात्ममायया ॥ ६॥
అజోఽపి సన్నవ్యయాత్మా భూతానామీశ్వరోఽపి సన్ ।
ప్రకృతిం స్వామధిష్ఠాయ సంభవామ్యాత్మమాయయా ॥ ౬॥
ajo ’pi sannavyayātmā bhūtānām īśvaro ’pi san |
prakṛtiṁ svām adhiṣṭhāya sambhavāmyātma-māyayā || 6 ||
Words: ajaḥ (unborn), api (although), san (being so), avyaya ātmā (Imperishable nature), bhūtānām (of (all) beings), īśvaraḥ (the Lord), api (although), san (being), prakṛtim (nature), svām (of myself), adhiṣṭhāya (situated), sambhavāmi (I manifest), ātma-māyayā (by my Yogmaya power)
Meaning: Though I am birthless and undecaying by nature, and the Lord of beings, yet by subjugating My Prakriti, I take birth through My own Maya.
Verse 7
यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदाऽऽत्मानं सृजाम्यहम् ॥ ७॥
యదా యదా హి ధర్మస్య గ్లానిర్భవతి భారత ।
అభ్యుత్థానమధర్మస్య తదాఽఽత్మానం సృజామ్యహమ్ ॥ ౭॥
yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānir bhavati bhārata |
abhyutthānam adharmasya tadātmānaṁ sṛjāmyaham || 7 ||
Words: yadā yadā (whenever), hi (certainly), dharmasya (of righteousness), glāniḥ (decline), bhavati (is), bhārata (Arjun, descendant of Bharat), abhyutthānam (increase), adharmasya (of unrighteousness), tadā (at that time), ātmānam (self), sṛjāmi (manifest), aham (I)
Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, whenever there is a decline in virtue and an increase in vice, then I manifest Myself.
Verse 8
परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् ।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय संभवामि युगे युगे ॥ ८॥
పరిత్రాణాయ సాధూనాం వినాశాయ చ దుష్కృతామ్ ।
ధర్మ-సంస్థాపన-అర్థాయ సంభవామి యుగే యుగే ॥ ౮॥
paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṁ vināśāya cha duṣkṛtām |
dharma-sansthāpanārthāya sambhavāmi yuge yuge || 8 ||
Words: paritrāṇāya (to protect), sādhūnām (the righteous), vināśāya (to annihilate), cha (and), duṣkṛtām (the wicked), dharma (the eternal religion), sansthāpana-arthāya (to reestablish), sambhavāmi (I appear), yuge yuge (age after age)
Meaning: For the protection of the pious, the destruction of the evil-doers, and the establishment of virtue, I manifest myself in every age.
Verse 9
जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन ॥ ९॥
జన్మ కర్మ చ మే దివ్యమేవం యో వేత్తి తత్త్వతః ।
త్యక్త్వా దేహం పునర్జన్మ నైతి మామేతి సోఽర్జున ॥ ౯॥
janma karma cha me divyam evaṁ yo vetti tattvataḥ |
tyaktvā dehaṁ punar janma naiti mām eti so ’rjuna || 9 ||
Words: janma (birth), karma (activities), cha (and), me (of mine), divyam (divine), evam (thus), yaḥ (who), vetti (know), tattvataḥ (in truth), tyaktvā (having abandoned), deham (the body), punaḥ (again), janma (birth), na (never), eti (takes), mām (to me), eti (comes), saḥ (he), arjuna (Arjun)
Meaning: He who thus knows truly the divine birth and actions of Mine does not get rebirth after casting off the body. He attains Me, O Arjuna.
Verse 10
वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः ।
बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः ॥ १०॥
వీతరాగభయక్రోధా మన్మయా మాముపాశ్రితాః ।
బహవో జ్ఞానతపసా పూతా మద్భావమాగతాః ॥ ౧౦॥
vīta-rāga-bhaya-krodhā man-mayā mām upāśritāḥ |
bahavo jñāna-tapasā pūtā mad-bhāvam āgatāḥ || 10 ||
Words: vīta (freed from), rāga (attachment), bhaya (fear), krodhāḥ (and anger), mat-mayā (completely absorbed in me), mām (in me), upāśritāḥ (taking refuge (of)), bahavaḥ (many (persons)), jñāna (of knowledge), tapasā (by the fire of knowledge), pūtāḥ (purified), mat-bhāvam (my divine love), āgatāḥ (attained)
Meaning: Many who were devoid of attachment, fear, and anger, who were absorbed in Me, who had taken refuge in Me, and were purified by the austerity of knowledge, have attained My state.
Verse 11
ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः ॥ ११॥
యే యథా మాం ప్రపద్యన్తే తాంస్తథైవ భజామ్యహమ్ ।
మమ వర్త్మానువర్తన్తే మనుష్యాః పార్థ సర్వశః ॥ ౧౧॥
ye yathā māṁ prapadyante tāns tathaiva bhajāmyaham |
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ || 11 ||
Words: ye (who), yathā (in whatever way), mām (unto me), prapadyante (surrender), tān (them), tathā (so), eva (certainly), bhajāmi (reciprocate), aham (I), mama (my), vartma (path), anuvartante (follow), manuṣyāḥ (men), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), sarvaśaḥ (in all respects)
Meaning: According to the manner in which they approach Me, I favor them in that same manner. O son of Partha, humans follow My path in every way.
Verse 12
काङ्क्षन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः ।
क्षिप्रं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्भवति कर्मजा ॥ १२॥
కాఙ్క్షన్తః కర్మణాం సిద్ధిం యజన్త ఇహ దేవతాః ।
క్షిప్రం హి మానుషే లోకే సిద్ధిర్భవతి కర్మజా ॥ ౧౨॥
kāṅkṣantaḥ karmaṇāṁ siddhiṁ yajanta iha devatāḥ |
kṣipraṁ hi mānuṣe loke siddhir bhavati karmajā || 12 ||
Words: kāṅkṣantaḥ (desiring), karmaṇām (material activities), siddhim (success), yajante (worship), iha (in this world), devatāḥ (the celestial gods), kṣipram (quickly), hi (certainly), mānuṣe (in human society), loke (within this world), siddhiḥ (rewarding), bhavati (manifest), karma-jā (from material activities)
Meaning: Longing for the fruition of their actions (of their rites and duties), they worship the gods here. For, in the human world, success from action comes quickly.
Verse 13
चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः ।
तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम् ॥ १३॥
చాతుర్వర్ణ్యం మయా సృష్టం గుణకర్మవిభాగశః ।
తస్య కర్తారమపి మాం విద్ధ్యకర్తారమవ్యయమ్ ॥ ౧౩॥
chātur-varṇyaṁ mayā sṛṣṭaṁ guṇa-karma-vibhāgaśaḥ |
tasya kartāram api māṁ viddhyakartāram avyayam || 13 ||
Words: chātuḥ-varṇyam (the four categories of occupations), mayā (by me), sṛṣṭam (were created), guṇa (of quality), karma (and activities), vibhāgaśaḥ (according to divisions), tasya (of that), kartāram (the creator), api (although), mām (me), viddhi (know), akartāram (non-doer), avyayam (unchangeable)
Meaning: The four castes have been created by Me through a classification of the gunas and duties. Even though I am the agent of that (act of classification), still know Me to be non-agentive and changeless.
Verse 14
न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पृहा ।
इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मभिर्न स बध्यते ॥ १४॥
న మాం కర్మాణి లిమ్పన్తి న మే కర్మ-ఫలే స్పృహా ।
ఇతి మాం యోఽభిజానాతి కర్మభిర్న స బధ్యతే ॥ ౧౪॥
na māṁ karmāṇi limpanti na me karma-phale spṛhā |

Words: na (not), mām (me), karmāṇi (activities), limpanti (taint), na (nor), me (my), karma-phale (the fruits of action), spṛhā (desire), iti (thus), mām (me), yaḥ (who), abhijānāti (knows), karmabhiḥ (result of action), na (never), saḥ (that person), badhyate (is bound)
Meaning: Actions do not taint Me; for I have no hankering for the results of actions. One who knows Me thus, does not become bound by their actions.
Verse 15
एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः ।
कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम् ॥ १५॥
ఏవం జ్ఞాత్వా కృతం కర్మ పూర్వైరపి ముముక్షుభిః ।
కురు కర్మైవ తస్మాత్త్వం పూర్వైః పూర్వతరం కృతమ్ ॥ ౧౫॥
evaṁ jñātvā kṛtaṁ karma pūrvair api mumukṣubhiḥ |
kuru karmaiva tasmāttvaṁ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraṁ kṛtam || 15 ||
Words: evam (thus), jñātvā (knowing), kṛtam (performed), karma (actions), pūrvaiḥ (of ancient times), api (indeed), mumukṣubhiḥ (seekers of liberation), kuru (should perform), karma (duty), eva (certainly), tasmāt (therefore), tvam (you), pūrvaiḥ (of those ancient sages), pūrva-taram (in ancient times), kṛtam (performed)
Meaning: Thus having known, even the ancient seekers of liberation performed their duties. Therefore, you should undertake action as was performed earlier by the ancients.
Verse 16
किं कर्म किमकर्मेति कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः ।
तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात् ॥ १६॥

తత్తే కర్మ ప్రవక్ష్యామి యజ్జ్ఞాత్వా మోక్ష్యసేఽశుభాత్ ॥ ౧౬॥

tat te karma pravakṣyāmi yaj jñātvā mokṣyase ’śubhāt || 16 ||
Words: kim (what), karma (action), kim (what), akarma (inaction), iti (thus), kavayaḥ (the wise), api (even), atra (in this), mohitāḥ (are confused), tat (that), te (to you), karma (action), pravakṣyāmi (I shall explain), yat (which), jñātvā (knowing), mokṣyase (you may free yourself), aśubhāt (from inauspiciousness)
Meaning: Even the intelligent are confounded as to what is action and what is inaction. I shall tell you of that action, knowing which you will become free from evil.
Verse 17
कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः ।
अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः ॥ १७॥
కర్మణో హ్యపి బోద్ధవ్యం బోద్ధవ్యం చ వికర్మణః ।
అకర్మణశ్చ బోద్ధవ్యం గహనా కర్మణో గతిః ॥ ౧౭॥

akarmaṇaś cha boddhavyaṁ gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ || 17 ||
Words: karmaṇaḥ (recommended action), hi (certainly), api (also), boddhavyam (should be known), boddhavyam (must understand), cha (and), vikarmaṇaḥ (forbidden action), akarmaṇaḥ (inaction), cha (and), boddhavyam (must understand), gahanā (profound), karmaṇaḥ (of action), gatiḥ (the true path)
Meaning: For there is something to be known even about action, and something to be known about prohibited action; and something needs to be known about inaction. The true nature of action is inscrutable.
Verse 18
कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः ।
स बुद्धिमान् मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥ १८॥
కర్మణ్యకర్మ యః పశ్యేదకర్మణి చ కర్మ యః ।
స బుద్ధిమాన్ మనుష్యేషు స యుక్తః కృత్స్నకర్మకృత్ ॥ ౧౮॥
karmaṇyakarma yaḥ paśyed akarmaṇi cha karma yaḥ |
sa buddhimān manuṣyeṣu sa yuktaḥ kṛtsna-karma-kṛt || 18 ||
Words: karmaṇi (action), akarma (in inaction), yaḥ (who), paśyet (see), akarmaṇi (inaction), cha (also), karma (action), yaḥ (who), saḥ (they), buddhi-mān (wise), manuṣyeṣu (amongst humans), saḥ (they), yuktaḥ (yogis), kṛtsna-karma-kṛt (performers all kinds of actions)
Meaning: He who finds inaction in action, and action in inaction, is the wise one among men; they are engaged in yoga and are performers of all actions!
Verse 19
यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसङ्कल्पवर्जिताः ।
ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाहुः पण्डितं बुधाः ॥ १९॥
యస్య సర్వే సమారమ్భాః కామసఙ్కల్పవర్జితాః ।
జ్ఞానాగ్నిదగ్ధకర్మాణం తమాహుః పణ్డితం బుధాః ॥ ౧౯॥
yasya sarve samārambhāḥ kāma-saṅkalpa-varjitāḥ |
jñānāgni-dagdha-karmāṇaṁ tam āhuḥ paṇḍitaṁ budhāḥ || 19 ||
Words: yasya (whose), sarve (every), samārambhāḥ (undertakings), kāma (desire for material pleasures), saṅkalpa (resolve), varjitāḥ (devoid of), jñāna (divine knowledge), agni (in the fire), dagdha (burnt), karmāṇam (actions), tam (him), āhuḥ (address), paṇḍitam (a sage), budhāḥ (the wise)
Meaning: The wise call him learned whose actions are all devoid of desires and their thoughts, and whose actions have been burned away by the fire of wisdom.
Verse 20
त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यतृप्तो निराश्रयः ।
कर्मण्यभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किञ्चित्करोति सः ॥ २०॥
త్యక్త్వా కర్మ-ఫలాసఙ్గం నిత్యతృప్తో నిరాశ్రయః ।
కర్మణ్యభిప్రవృత్తోఽపి నైవ కిఞ్చిత్కరోతి సః ॥ ౨౦॥
tyaktvā karma-phalāsaṅgaṁ nitya-tṛpto nirāśrayaḥ |
karmaṇyabhipravṛtto ’pi naiva kiñchit karoti saḥ || 20 ||
Words: tyaktvā (having given up), karma-phala-āsaṅgam (attachment to the fruits of action), nitya (always), tṛptaḥ (satisfied), nirāśrayaḥ (without dependence), karmaṇi (in activities), abhipravṛttaḥ (engaged), api (despite), na (not), eva (certainly), kiñchit (anything), karoti (do), saḥ (that person)
Meaning: Having given up attachment to the results of action, he who is ever-content, dependent on nothing, does not really do anything even though he is engaged in action.
Verse 21
निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा त्यक्तसर्वपरिग्रहः ।
शारीरं केवलं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम् ॥ २१॥
నిరాశీః యత-చిత్త-ఆత్మా త్యక్తసర్వపరిగ్రహః ।
శారీరం కేవలం కర్మ కుర్వన్నాప్నోతి కిల్బిషమ్ ॥ ౨౧॥
nirāśīr yata-chittātmā tyakta-sarva-parigrahaḥ |
śārīraṁ kevalaṁ karma kurvan nāpnoti kilbiṣam || 21 ||
Words: nirāśīḥ (free from expectations), yata (controlled), chitta-ātmā (mind and intellect), tyakta (having abandoned), sarva (all), parigrahaḥ (the sense of ownership), śārīram (bodily), kevalam (only), karma (actions), kurvan (performing), na (never), āpnoti (incurs), kilbiṣam (sin)
Meaning: One who is without solicitation, who has the mind and senses under control, and is totally without possessions, incurs no sin by performing actions merely for the maintenance of the body.
Verse 22
यदृच्छालाभसन्तुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः ।
समः सिद्धावसिद्धौ च कृत्वापि न निबध्यते ॥ २२॥
యదృచ్ఛా-లాభ-సన్తుష్టో ద్వన్ద్వ-అతీతో విమత్సరః ।
సమః సిద్ధావసిద్ధౌ చ కృత్వాపి న నిబధ్యతే ॥ ౨౨॥
yadṛchchhā-lābha-santuṣṭo dvandvātīto vimatsaraḥ |
samaḥ siddhāvasiddhau cha kṛtvāpi na nibadhyate || 22 ||
Words: yadṛchchhā (which comes of its own accord), lābha (gain), santuṣṭaḥ (contented), dvandva (duality), atītaḥ (surpassed), vimatsaraḥ (free from envy), samaḥ (equipoised), siddhau (in success), asiddhau (failure), cha (and), kṛtvā (performing), api (even), na (never), nibadhyate (is bound)
Meaning: Remaining satisfied with what comes unasked for, having transcended the dualities, being free from spite, and balanced under success and failure, he is not bound even by performing actions.
Verse 23
गतसङ्गस्य मुक्तस्य ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः ।
यज्ञायाचरतः कर्म समग्रं प्रविलीयते ॥ २३॥
గతసఙ్గస్య ముక్తస్య జ్ఞానావస్థితచేతసః ।
యజ్ఞాయాచరతః కర్మ సమగ్రం ప్రవిలీయతే ॥ ౨౩॥
gata-saṅgasya muktasya jñānāvasthita-chetasaḥ |
yajñāyācarataḥ karma samagraṁ pravilīyate || 23 ||
Words: gata-saṅgasya (free from material attachments), muktasya (of the liberated), jñāna-avasthita (established in divine knowledge), chetasaḥ (whose intellect), yajñāya (as a sacrifice (to God)), ācharataḥ (performing), karma (action), samagram (completely), pravilīyate (are freed)
Meaning: The liberated person, who has rid themselves of attachment and whose mind is fixed in knowledge, has their actions undertaken for a sacrifice completely destroyed.
Verse 24
ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्महविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम् ।
ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना ॥ २४॥
బ్రహ్మ-అర్పణం బ్రహ్మహవిర్బ్రహ్మ-అగ్నౌ బ్రహ్మణా హుతమ్ ।
బ్రహ్మైవ తేన గన్తవ్యం బ్రహ్మ-కర్మసమాధినా ॥ ౨౪॥
brahmārpaṇaṁ brahma havir brahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam |
brahmaiva tena gantavyaṁ brahma-karma-samādhinā || 24 ||
Words: brahma (Brahman), arpaṇam (the ladle and other offerings), brahma (Brahman), haviḥ (the oblation), brahma (Brahman), agnau (in the sacrificial fire), brahmaṇā (by that person), hutam (offered), brahma (Brahman), eva (certainly), tena (by that), gantavyam (to be attained), brahma (Brahman), karma (offering), samādhinā (those completely absorbed in God-consciousness)
Meaning: The ladle is Brahman, the oblations are Brahman, and the offering is poured by Brahman into the fire of Brahman. He who has concentration on Brahman as the objective can only reach Brahman.
Verse 25
दैवमेवापरे यज्ञं योगिनः पर्युपासते ।
ब्रह्माग्नावपरे यज्ञं यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति ॥ २५॥
దైవమేవాపరే యజ్ఞం యోగినః పర్యుపాసతే ।
బ్రహ్మాగ్నావపరే యజ్ఞం యజ్ఞేనైవోపజుహ్వతి ॥ ౨౫॥
daivam evāpare yajñaṁ yoginaḥ paryupāsate |
brahmāgnāvapare yajñaṁ yajñenaivopajuhvati || 25 ||
Words: daivam (the celestial gods), eva (indeed), apare (others), yajñam (sacrifice), yoginaḥ (spiritual practioners), paryupāsate (worship), brahma (of the Supreme Truth), agnau (in the fire), apare (others), yajñam (sacrifice), yajñena (by sacrifice), eva (indeed), upajuhvati (offer)
Meaning: Other yogis undertake sacrifice to gods alone, while others offer the Self as a sacrifice by themselves, in the fire of Brahman.
Verse 26
श्रोत्रादीनीन्द्रियाण्यन्ये संयमाग्निषु जुह्वति ।
शब्दादीन्विषयानन्य इन्द्रियाग्निषु जुह्वति ॥ २६॥
శ్రోత్రాదీనీన్ద్రియాణ్యన్యే సంయమాగ్నిషు జుహ్వతి ।
శబ్దాదీన్విషయానన్య ఇన్ద్రియాగ్నిషు జుహ్వతి ॥ ౨౬॥
śrotrādīnīndriyāṇyanye sanyamāgniṣu juhvati |
śabdādīn viṣayānanya indriyāgniṣu juhvati || 26 ||
Words: śrotra-ādīni (such as the hearing process), indriyāṇi (senses), anye (others), sanyama (restraint), agniṣu (in the sacrficial fire), juhvati (sacrifice), śabda-ādīn (sound vibration, etc), viṣayān (objects of sense-gratification), anye (others), indriya (of the senses), agniṣu (in the fire), juhvati (sacrifice)
Meaning: Others offer their organs, such as their ears, etc., in the fires of self-control. Others offer the objects, such as sound, etc., in the fires of their organs.
Verse 27
सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे ।
आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते ॥ २७॥
సర్వాణీన్ద్రియకర్మాణి ప్రాణకర్మాణి చాపరే ।
ఆత్మ-సంయమ-యోగ-అగ్నౌ జుహ్వతి జ్ఞానదీపితే ॥ ౨౭॥
sarvāṇīndriya-karmāṇi prāṇa-karmāṇi chāpare |
ātma-sanyama-yogāgnau juhvati jñāna-dīpite || 27 ||
Words: sarvāṇi (all), indriya (the senses), karmāṇi (functions), prāṇa-karmāṇi (functions of the life breath), cha (and), apare (others), ātma-sanyama yogāgnau (in the fire of the controlled mind), juhvati (sacrifice), jñāna-dīpite (kindled by knowledge)
Meaning: Others offer all the activities of the organs and the activities of the vital force into the fire of the yoga of self-control, lit by Knowledge.
Verse 28
द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपोयज्ञा योगयज्ञास्तथापरे ।
स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितव्रताः ॥ २८॥
ద్రవ్యయజ్ఞాస్తపోయజ్ఞా యోగయజ్ఞాస్తథాపరే ।
స్వాధ్యాయజ్ఞానయజ్ఞాశ్చ యతయః సంశితవ్రతాః ॥ ౨౮॥
dravya-yajñās tapo-yajñā yoga-yajñās tathāpare |
swādhyāya-jñāna-yajñāś cha yatayaḥ sanśita-vratāḥ || 28 ||
Words: dravya-yajñāḥ (offering one’s own wealth as sacrifice), tapaḥ-yajñāḥ (offering severe austerities as sacrifice), yoga-yajñāḥ (performance of eight-fold path of yogic practices as sacrifice), tathā (thus), apare (others), swādhyāya (cultivating knowledge by studying the scriptures), jñāna-yajñāḥ (those offer cultivation of transcendental knowledge as sacrifice), cha (also), yatayaḥ (these ascetics), sanśita-vratāḥ (observing strict vows)
Meaning: Similarly, others are performers of sacrifices through wealth, through austerity, through yoga, and through study and knowledge; others are ascetics with severe vows.
Verse 29
अपाने जुह्वति प्राण प्राणेऽपानं तथाऽपरे ।
प्राणापानगती रुद्ध्वा प्राणायामपरायणाः ॥ २९॥
అపానే జుహ్వతి ప్రాణ ప్రాణేఽపానం తథాఽపరే ।
ప్రాణాపానగతీ రుద్ధ్వా ప్రాణాయామపరాయణాః ॥ ౨౯॥
apāne juhvati prāṇaṁ prāṇe ’pānaṁ tathāpare |
prāṇāpāna-gatī ruddhvā prāṇāyāma-parāyaṇāḥ apare niyatāhārāḥ prāṇān prāṇeṣu juhvati sarve ’pyete yajña-vido yajña-kṣapita-kalmaṣāḥ || 29 ||
Words: apāne (the incoming breath), juhvati (offer), prāṇam (the outgoing breath), prāṇe (in the outgoing breath), apānam (incoming breath), tathā (also), apare (others), prāṇa (of the outgoing breath), apāna (and the incoming breath), gatī (movement), ruddhvā (blocking), prāṇa-āyāma (control of breath), parāyaṇāḥ (wholly devoted), apare (others), niyata (having controlled), āhārāḥ (food intake), prāṇān (life-breaths), prāṇeṣu (life-energy), juhvati (sacrifice), sarve (all), api (also), ete (these), yajña-vidaḥ (knowers of sacrifices), yajña-kṣapita (being cleansed by performances of sacrifices), kalmaṣāḥ (of impurities)
Meaning: Constantly practicing control of the vital forces by stopping the movements of the outgoing and incoming breaths, some offer as a sacrifice the outgoing breath into the incoming breath; while still others, the incoming breath into the outgoing breath.
Verse 30
अपरे नियताहाराः प्राणान्प्राणेषु जुह्वति ।
सर्वेऽप्येते यज्ञविदो यज्ञक्षपितकल्मषाः ॥ ३०॥
అపరే నియతాహారాః ప్రాణాన్ప్రాణేషు జుహ్వతి ।
సర్వేఽప్యేతే యజ్ఞవిదో యజ్ఞక్షపితకల్మషాః ॥ ౩౦॥
apare niyatāhārāḥ prāṇān prāṇeṣu juhvati |
sarve py 'ete yajña-vido yajña-kṣapita-kalmaṣāḥ || 30 ||
Words: apare (others), niyata (controlled), āhārāḥ (eating), prāṇān (outgoing air), prāṇeṣu (in the outgoing air), sarve (all), api (although apparently different), ete (all these), yajñavidaḥ (conversant with the purpose of performing), yajña (sacrifices), kṣapita (being cleansed of the result of such performances), kalmaṣāḥ (sinful reactions), juhvati (sacrifices.)
Meaning: Others, having their food regulated, offer the vital forces in the vital forces. All of them are knowers of the sacrifice and have their sins destroyed by the sacrifice.
Verse 31
यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।
नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतो़ऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम ॥ ३१॥
యజ్ఞశిష్టామృతభుజో యాన్తి బ్రహ్మ సనాతనమ్ ।
నాయం లోకోఽస్త్యయజ్ఞస్య కుతో఼ఽన్యః కురుసత్తమ ॥ ౩౧॥
yajña-śiṣṭāmṛta-bhujo yānti brahma sanātanam |
nāyaṁ loko ’styayajñasya kuto ’nyaḥ kuru-sattama || 31 ||
Words: yajña-śiṣṭa amṛta-bhujaḥ (they partake of the nectarean remnants of sacrifice), yānti (go), brahma (the Absolute Truth), sanātanam (eternal), na (never), ayam (this), lokaḥ (planet), asti (is), ayajñasya (for one who performs no sacrifice), kutaḥ (how), anyaḥ (other (world)), kuru-sat-tama (best of the Kurus, Arjun)
Meaning: Those who partake of the nectar left over after a sacrifice reach the eternal Brahman. This world ceases to exist for one who does not perform sacrifices; what, then, to speak of the other world, O best among the Kurus (Arjuna)!
Verse 32
एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे ।
कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोक्ष्यसे ॥ ३२॥
ఏవం బహువిధా యజ్ఞా వితతా బ్రహ్మణో ముఖే ।
కర్మజాన్విద్ధి తాన్సర్వానేవం జ్ఞాత్వా విమోక్ష్యసే ॥ ౩౨॥
evaṁ bahu-vidhā yajñā vitatā brahmaṇo mukhe |
karma-jān viddhi tān sarvān evaṁ jñātvā vimokṣyase || 32 ||
Words: evam (thus), bahu-vidhāḥ (various kinds of), yajñāḥ (sacrifices), vitatāḥ (have been described), brahmaṇaḥ (of the Vedas), mukhe (through the mouth), karma-jān (originating from works), viddhi (know), tān (them), sarvān (all), evam (thus), jñātvā (having known), vimokṣyase (you shall be liberated)
Meaning: Thus, various kinds of sacrifices are spread at the mouth of the Vedas. Know them all to be born of action. Knowing this, you will become liberated.
Verse 33
श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः परन्तप ।
सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते ॥ ३३॥
శ్రేయాన్ద్రవ్యమయాద్యజ్ఞాజ్జ్ఞానయజ్ఞః పరన్తప ।
సర్వం కర్మాఖిలం పార్థ జ్ఞానే పరిసమాప్యతే ॥ ౩౩॥
śreyān dravya-mayād yajñāj jñāna-yajñaḥ parantapa |
sarvaṁ karmākhilaṁ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate || 33 ||
Words: śreyān (superior), dravya-mayāt (of material possessions), yajñāt (than the sacrifice), jñāna-yajñaḥ (sacrifice performed in knowledge), parantapa (subduer of enemies, Arjun), sarvam (all), karma (works), akhilam (all), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), jñāne (in knowledge), parisamāpyate (culminate)
Meaning: O destroyer of enemies, knowledge considered as a sacrifice is greater than sacrifices requiring materials. O son of Prtha, all actions in their totality culminate in knowledge.
Verse 34
तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ॥ ३४॥
తద్విద్ధి ప్రణిపాతేన పరిప్రశ్నేన సేవయా ।
ఉపదేక్ష్యన్తి తే జ్ఞానం జ్ఞానినస్తత్త్వదర్శినః ॥ ౩౪॥
tad viddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā |
upadekṣyanti te jñānaṁ jñāninas tattva-darśinaḥ || 34 ||
Words: tat (the Truth), viddhi (try to learn), praṇipātena (by approaching a spiritual master), paripraśnena (by humble inquiries), sevayā (by rendering service), upadekṣyanti (can impart), te (unto you), jñānam (knowledge), jñāninaḥ (the enlightened), tattva-darśinaḥ (those who have realized the Truth)
Meaning: Know that the wise ones who have realized the Truth will impart the Knowledge to you through prostration, inquiry, and service.
Verse 35
यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव ।
येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि ॥ ३५॥
యజ్జ్ఞాత్వా న పునర్మోహమేవం యాస్యసి పాణ్డవ ।
యేన భూతాన్యశేషేణ ద్రక్ష్యస్యాత్మన్యథో మయి ॥ ౩౫॥
yaj jñātvā na punar moham evaṁ yāsyasi pāṇḍava |
yena bhūtānyaśeṣeṇa drakṣyasyātmanyatho mayi || 35 ||
Words: yat (which), jñātvā (having known), na (never), punaḥ (again), moham (delusion), evam (like this), yāsyasi (you shall get), pāṇḍava (Arjun, the son of Pandu), yena (by this), bhūtāni (living beings), aśeṣāṇi (all), drakṣyasi (you will see), ātmani (within me (Shree Krishna)), atho (that is to say), mayi (in me)
Meaning: Knowing this, O Pandava, you will not be deluded again in this way, and through it, you will see all beings, without exception, in the Self and also in Me.
Verse 36
अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः ।
सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि ॥ ३६॥
అపి చేదసి పాపేభ్యః సర్వేభ్యః పాపకృత్తమః ।
సర్వం జ్ఞాన-ప్లవేనైవ వృజినం సన్తరిష్యసి ॥ ౩౬॥
api ched asi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpa-kṛt-tamaḥ |
sarvaṁ jñāna-plavenaiva vṛjinaṁ santariṣyasi || 36 ||
Words: api (even), chet (if), asi (you are), pāpebhyaḥ (sinners), sarvebhyaḥ (of all), pāpa-kṛt-tamaḥ (most sinful), sarvam (all), jñāna-plavena (by the boat of divine knowledge), eva (certainly), vṛjinam (sin), santariṣyasi (you shall cross over)
Meaning: Even if you are the worst sinner among all sinners, you can still cross over all wickedness with the raft of Knowledge alone.
Verse 37
यथैधांसि समिद्धोऽग्निर्भस्मसात्कुरुतेऽर्जुन ।
ज्ञानाग्निः सर्वकर्माणि भस्मसात्कुरुते तथा ॥ ३७॥
యథైధాంసి సమిద్ధోఽగ్నిర్భస్మసాత్కురుతేఽర్జున ।
జ్ఞాన-అగ్నిః సర్వకర్మాణి భస్మసాత్కురుతే తథా ॥ ౩౭॥
yathaidhānsi samiddho ’gnir bhasma-sāt kurute ’rjuna |
jñānāgniḥ sarva-karmāṇi bhasma-sāt kurute tathā || 37 ||
Words: yathā (as), edhānsi (firewood), samiddhaḥ (blazing), agniḥ (fire), bhasma-sāt (to ashes), kurute (turns), arjuna (Arjun), jñāna-agniḥ (the fire of knowledge), sarva-karmāṇi (all reactions from material activities), bhasma-sāt (to ashes), kurute (it turns), tathā (similarly)
Meaning: O Arjuna, just as a blazing fire reduces wood to ashes, similarly the fire of Knowledge reduces all actions to ashes.
Verse 38
न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति ॥ ३८॥
న హి జ్ఞానేన సదృశం పవిత్రమిహ విద్యతే ।
తత్స్వయం యోగసంసిద్ధః కాలేనాత్మని విన్దతి ॥ ౩౮॥
na hi jñānena sadṛśaṁ pavitramiha vidyate |
tatsvayaṁ yogasansiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati || 38 ||
Words: na (not), hi (certainly), jñānena (with divine knowledge), sadṛśam (like), pavitram (pure), iha (in this world), vidyate (exists), tat (that), svayam (oneself), yoga (practice of yog), sansiddhaḥ (he who has attained perfection), kālena (in course of time), ātmani (wihtin the heart), vindati (finds)
Meaning: Indeed, there is nothing purifying here comparable to Knowledge. One who has become perfected over time through yoga, realizes That in their own heart.
Verse 39
श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः ।
ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥ ३९॥
శ్రద్ధావాఁల్లభతే జ్ఞానం తత్పరః సంయతేన్ద్రియః ।
జ్ఞానం లబ్ధ్వా పరాం శాన్తిమచిరేణాధిగచ్ఛతి ॥ ౩౯॥
śraddhāvān labhate jñānaṁ tat-paraḥ sanyatendriyaḥ |
jñānaṁ labdhvā parāṁ śāntim achireṇādhigachchhati || 39 ||
Words: śraddhā-vān (a faithful person), labhate (achieves), jñānam (divine knowledge), tat-paraḥ (devoted (to that)), sanyata (controlled), indriyaḥ (senses), jñānam (transcendental knowledge), labdhvā (having achieved), parām (supreme), śāntim (peace), achireṇa (without delay), adhigachchhati (attains)
Meaning: The one who has faith, is diligent, and has control over their organs, attains Knowledge. Achieving Knowledge, one soon attains supreme Peace.
Verse 40
अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति ।
नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः ॥ ४०॥
అజ్ఞశ్చాశ్రద్దధానశ్చ సంశయాత్మా వినశ్యతి ।
నాయం లోకోఽస్తి న పరో న సుఖం సంశయాత్మనః ॥ ౪౦॥
ajñaś chāśraddadhānaś cha sanśayātmā vinaśyati |
nāyaṁ loko ’sti na paro na sukhaṁ sanśayātmanaḥ || 40 ||
Words: ajñaḥ (the ignorant), cha (and), aśraddadhānaḥ (without faith), cha (and), sanśaya (skeptical), ātmā (a person), vinaśyati (falls down), na (never), ayam (in this), lokaḥ (world), asti (is), na (not), paraḥ (in the next), na (not), sukham (happiness), sanśaya-ātmanaḥ (for the skeptical soul)
Meaning: One who is ignorant and faithless, and has a doubting mind, perishes. Neither this world nor the next, nor happiness, exists for one who has a doubting mind.
Verse 41
योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसंछिन्नसंशयम् ।
आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय ॥ ४१॥
యోగ-సంన్యస్త-కర్మాణం జ్ఞానసంఛిన్నసంశయమ్ ।
ఆత్మవన్తం న కర్మాణి నిబధ్నన్తి ధనఞ్జయ ॥ ౪౧॥
yoga-sannyasta-karmāṇaṁ jñāna-sañchhinna-sanśayam |
ātmavantaṁ na karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya || 41 ||
Words: yoga-sannyasta-karmāṇam (those who renounce ritualistic karm, dedicating their body, mind, and soul to God), jñāna (by knowledge), sañchhinna (dispelled), sanśayam (doubts), ātma-vantam (situated in knowledge of the self), na (not), karmāṇi (actions), nibadhnanti (bind), dhanañjaya (Arjun, the conqueror of wealth)
Meaning: O Dhananjaya (Arjuna), actions do not bind one who has renounced actions through yoga, whose doubts have been fully dispelled by Knowledge, and who is not inattentive.
Verse 42
तस्मादज्ञानसंभूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनाऽऽत्मनः ।
छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत ॥ ४२॥
తస్మాదజ్ఞానసంభూతం హృత్స్థం జ్ఞానాసినాఽఽత్మనః ।
ఛిత్త్వైనం సంశయం యోగమాతిష్ఠోత్తిష్ఠ భారత ॥ ౪౨॥
tasmād ajñāna-sambhūtaṁ hṛt-sthaṁ jñānāsinātmanaḥ |
chhittvainaṁ sanśayaṁ yogam ātiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhārata || 42 ||
Words: tasmāt (therefore), ajñāna-sambhūtam (born of ignorance), hṛt-stham (situated in the heart), jñāna (of knowledge), asinā (with the sword), ātmanaḥ (of the self), chhittvā (cut asunder), enam (this), sanśayam (doubt), yogam (in karm yog), ātiṣṭha (take shelter), uttiṣṭha (arise), bhārata (Arjun, descendant of Bharat)
Meaning: Therefore, O scion of the Bharata dynasty, take recourse to yoga and rise up, cutting asunder with the sword of knowledge this doubt in your heart, arising from ignorance.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे ज्ञानकर्मसंन्यासयोगो नाम चतुर्थोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the fourth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Jñāna Karma Sannyāsa Yoga — the Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation of Action.
