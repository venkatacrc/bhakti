Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 12

अध्यायः १२ · भक्तियोगः — The Yoga of Devotion

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 20 verses.

Arjuna asks whether worship of the personal Lord or of the unmanifest Absolute is superior. Krishna answers that devotion to Him with faith is the easier and higher path for the embodied, and describes the qualities of the devotee who is most dear to Him.

The Twenty Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते । ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः ॥ १॥
ఏవం సతత-యుక్తా యే భక్తాస్-త్వాం పర్యుపాసతే ।
యే చాప్యక్షరమవ్యక్తం తేషాం కే యోగ-విత్-తమాః ॥ 1 ॥
evaṃ satata-yuktā ye bhaktās tvāṃ paryupāsate | ye cāpy akṣaram avyaktaṃ teṣāṃ ke yoga-vit-tamāḥ || 1 ||
Words: evam (thus), satata-yuktāḥ (constantly engaged), ye (those who), bhaktāḥ (devotees), tvām (You), paryupāsate (worship), ye ca api (and those who), akṣaram avyaktam (the imperishable unmanifest), teṣām (of them), ke (which), yoga-vit-tamāḥ (best knowers of yoga).
Meaning: Arjuna said: Those devotees who, ever steadfast, worship You, and those who worship the imperishable unmanifest — which of them are the better knowers of yoga?
Verse 2
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते । श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः ॥ २॥
మయ్యావేశ్య మనో యే మాం నిత్య-యుక్తా ఉపాసతే ।
శ్రద్ధయా పరయోపేతాస్-తే మే యుక్త-తమా మతాః ॥ 2 ॥
mayy āveśya mano ye māṃ nitya-yuktā upāsate | śraddhayā parayopetās te me yukta-tamā matāḥ || 2 ||
Words: mayi (in Me), āveśya (having fixed), manaḥ (mind), ye (those who), mām (Me), nitya-yuktāḥ (ever steadfast), upāsate (worship), śraddhayā parayā (with supreme faith), upetāḥ (endowed), te (they), me (by Me), yukta-tamāḥ (most perfected in yoga), matāḥ (are considered).
Meaning: The Blessed Lord said: Those who fix their minds on Me, worship Me ever steadfastly, and are endowed with supreme faith — I consider them the most perfected in yoga.
Verse 3
ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते । सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यञ्च कूटस्थमचलन्ध्रुवम् ॥ ३॥
యే త్వక్షరమనిర్దేశ్యమవ్యక్తం పర్యుపాసతే ।
సర్వత్ర-గమచింత్యం-చ కూట-స్థమచలం-ధ్రువమ్ ॥ 3 ॥
ye tv akṣaram anirdeśyam avyaktaṃ paryupāsate | sarvatra-gam acintyaṃ ca kūṭa-stham acalaṃ dhruvam || 3 ||
Words: ye tu (but those who), akṣaram (the imperishable), anirdeśyam (the indefinable), avyaktam (the unmanifest), paryupāsate (worship), sarvatra-gam (all-pervading), acintyam (inconceivable), ca (and), kūṭa-stham (unchanging / immutable), acalam (immovable), dhruvam (eternal / fixed).
Meaning: But those who worship the imperishable, the indefinable, the unmanifest, the all-pervading, the inconceivable, the unchanging, the immovable, and the eternal…
Verse 4
सन्नियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः । ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ ४॥
సన్నియమ్యేంద్రియ-గ్రామం సర్వత్ర సమ-బుద్ధయః ।
తే ప్రాప్నువంతి మామేవ సర్వ-భూత-హితే రతాః ॥ 4 ॥
sanniyamyendriya-grāmaṃ sarvatra sama-buddhayaḥ | te prāpnuvanti mām eva sarva-bhūta-hite ratāḥ || 4 ||
Words: sanniyamya (having restrained), indriya-grāmam (the host of senses), sarvatra (everywhere), sama-buddhayaḥ (even-minded), te (they), prāpnuvanti (attain), mām eva (Me alone), sarva-bhūta-hite ratāḥ (devoted to the welfare of all beings).
Meaning: …having restrained all the senses, being even-minded everywhere, and intent on the welfare of all beings — they too verily attain Me.
Verse 5
क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् । अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते ॥ ५॥
క్లేశోఽధికతరస్-తేషామవ్యక్తాసక్త-చేతసామ్ ।
అవ్యక్తా హి గతిర్-దుఃఖం దేహ-వద్భిరవాప్యతే ॥ 5 ॥
kleśo 'dhikataras teṣām avyaktāsakta-cetasām | avyaktā hi gatir duḥkhaṃ deha-vadbhir avāpyate || 5 ||
Words: kleśaḥ (difficulty / hardship), adhikataraḥ (greater), teṣām (for them), avyakta-āsakta-cetasām (whose minds are attached to the unmanifest), avyaktā gatiḥ (the path of the unmanifest), hi (indeed), duḥkham (hard / painful), deha-vadbhiḥ (by the embodied), avāpyate (is attained).
Meaning: Greater is the difficulty for those whose minds are set on the unmanifest, for the goal of the unmanifest is very hard for the embodied to reach.
Verse 6
ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि सन्न्यस्य मत्पराः । अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते ॥ ६॥
యే తు సర్వాణి కర్మాణి మయి సన్న్యస్య మత్-పరాః ।
అనన్యేనైవ యోగేన మాం ధ్యాయంత ఉపాసతే ॥ 6 ॥
ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi mayi sannyasya mat-parāḥ | ananyenaiva yogena māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate || 6 ||
Words: ye tu (but those who), sarvāṇi karmāṇi (all actions), mayi (in Me), sannyasya (having renounced / offered), mat-parāḥ (devoted to Me as the supreme), ananyena eva yogena (with undivided yoga alone), mām (Me), dhyāyantaḥ (meditating), upāsate (worship).
Meaning: But those who renounce all actions in Me, regarding Me as the supreme goal, and worship Me, meditating on Me with single-minded yoga…
Verse 7
तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् । भवामि न चिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम् ॥ ७॥
తేషామహం సముద్ధర్తా మృత్యు-సంసార-సాగరాత్ ।
భవామి న చిరాత్-పార్థ మయ్యావేశిత-చేతసామ్ ॥ 7 ॥
teṣām ahaṃ samuddhartā mṛtyu-saṃsāra-sāgarāt | bhavāmi na cirāt pārtha mayy āveśita-cetasām || 7 ||
Words: teṣām (for them), aham (I), samuddhartā (the deliverer / savior), mṛtyu-saṃsāra-sāgarāt (from the ocean of death and worldly existence), bhavāmi (I become), na cirāt (before long / soon), pārtha (O son of Pṛthā), mayi āveśita-cetasām (whose minds are fixed on Me).
Meaning: …of those whose minds are set on Me, O Pārtha, I soon become the deliverer from the ocean of death and saṃsāra.
Verse 8
मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय । निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥ ८॥
మయ్యేవ మన ఆధత్స్వ మయి బుద్ధిం నివేశయ ।
నివసిష్యసి మయ్యేవ అత ఊర్ధ్వం న సంశయః ॥ 8 ॥
mayy eva mana ādhatsva mayi buddhiṃ niveśaya | nivasiṣyasi mayy eva ata ūrdhvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ || 8 ||
Words: mayi eva (in Me alone), manaḥ (mind), ādhatsva (fix / place), mayi (in Me), buddhim (intellect), niveśaya (settle / establish), nivasiṣyasi (you will dwell), mayi eva (in Me alone), ataḥ ūrdhvam (thereafter), na saṃśayaḥ (no doubt).
Meaning: Fix your mind on Me alone; establish your intellect in Me. Thereafter you will dwell in Me alone — of this there is no doubt.
Verse 9
अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् । अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय ॥ ९॥
అథ చిత్తం సమాధాతుం న శక్నోషి మయి స్థిరమ్ ।
అభ్యాస-యోగేన తతో మామిచ్ఛాప్తుం ధనంజయ ॥ 9 ॥
atha cittaṃ samādhātuṃ na śaknoṣi mayi sthiram | abhyāsa-yogena tato mām icchāptuṃ dhanañjaya || 9 ||
Words: atha (if / then), cittam (mind), samādhātum (to fix), na śaknoṣi (you are not able), mayi (in Me), sthiram (steadily), abhyāsa-yogena (by the yoga of practice), tataḥ (then), mām (Me), iccha (seek / desire), āptum (to attain), dhanañjaya (O conqueror of wealth).
Meaning: If you are unable to fix your mind steadily on Me, then seek to reach Me by the yoga of constant practice, O Dhanañjaya.
Verse 10
अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव । मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि ॥ १०॥
అభ్యాసేఽప్యసమర్థోఽసి మత్-కర్మ-పరమో భవ ।
మదర్థమపి కర్మాణి కుర్వన్-సిద్ధిమవాప్స్యసి ॥ 10 ॥
abhyāse 'py asamartho 'si mat-karma-paramo bhava | mad-artham api karmāṇi kurvan siddhim avāpsyasi || 10 ||
Words: abhyāse api (even in practice), asamarthaḥ asi (you are unable), mat-karma-paramaḥ (intent on actions for Me), bhava (be), mad-artham api (even for My sake), karmāṇi (actions), kurvan (performing), siddhim (perfection), avāpsyasi (you will attain).
Meaning: If you are unable even to practice, then be intent on doing actions for My sake; even by performing actions for My sake, you will attain perfection.
Verse 11
अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः । सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान् ॥ ११॥
అథైతదప్యశక్తోఽసి కర్తుం మద్-యోగమాశ్రితః ।
సర్వ-కర్మ-ఫల-త్యాగం తతః కురు యతాత్మవాన్ ॥ 11 ॥
athaitad apy aśakto 'si kartuṃ mad-yogam āśritaḥ | sarva-karma-phala-tyāgaṃ tataḥ kuru yatātmavān || 11 ||
Words: atha (if), etat api (even this), aśaktaḥ asi (you are unable), kartum (to do), mad-yogam āśritaḥ (taking refuge in My yoga), sarva-karma-phala-tyāgam (renunciation of the fruits of all actions), tataḥ (then), kuru (perform), yata-ātmavān (self-controlled).
Meaning: If you are unable even to do this, then, taking refuge in My yoga, renounce the fruits of all actions, with self-control.
Verse 12
श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते । ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम् ॥ १२॥
శ్రేయో హి జ్ఞానమభ్యాసాజ్-జ్ఞానాద్-ధ్యానం విశిష్యతే ।
ధ్యానాత్-కర్మ-ఫల-త్యాగస్-త్యాగాచ్-ఛాంతిరనంతరమ్ ॥ 12 ॥
śreyo hi jñānam abhyāsāj jñānād dhyānaṃ viśiṣyate | dhyānāt karma-phala-tyāgas tyāgāc chāntir anantaram || 12 ||
Words: śreyaḥ (better / superior), hi (indeed), jñānam (knowledge), abhyāsāt (than practice), jñānāt (than knowledge), dhyānam (meditation), viśiṣyate (is superior), dhyānāt (than meditation), karma-phala-tyāgaḥ (renunciation of the fruits of action), tyāgāt (from renunciation), śāntiḥ (peace), anantaram (immediately follows).
Meaning: Better indeed is knowledge than practice; better than knowledge is meditation; better than meditation is renunciation of the fruits of action — from renunciation, peace follows at once.
Verse 13
अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च । निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥ १३॥
అద్వేష్టా సర్వ-భూతానాం మైత్రః కరుణ ఏవ చ ।
నిర్మమో నిరహంకారః సమ-దుఃఖ-సుఖః క్షమీ ॥ 13 ॥
adveṣṭā sarva-bhūtānāṃ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca | nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ kṣamī || 13 ||
Words: adveṣṭā (one who hates none), sarva-bhūtānām (toward all beings), maitraḥ (friendly), karuṇaḥ (compassionate), eva ca (also), nirmamaḥ (free from possessiveness), nir-ahaṅkāraḥ (free from egoism), sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ (equal in pain and pleasure), kṣamī (forgiving / patient).
Meaning: He who hates no being, who is friendly and compassionate, free from possessiveness and egoism, equal in pain and pleasure, and forgiving…
Verse 14
सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः । मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १४॥
సంతుష్టః సతతం యోగీ యతాత్మా దృఢ-నిశ్చయః ।
మయ్యర్పిత-మనో-బుద్ధిర్-యో మద్-భక్తః స మే ప్రియః ॥ 14 ॥
santuṣṭaḥ satataṃ yogī yatātmā dṛḍha-niścayaḥ | mayy arpita-mano-buddhir yo mad-bhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ || 14 ||
Words: santuṣṭaḥ (content), satatam (always), yogī (a yogi), yata-ātmā (self-controlled), dṛḍha-niścayaḥ (of firm resolve), mayi arpita (offered / fixed in Me), manaḥ-buddhiḥ (mind and intellect), yaḥ (who), mad-bhaktaḥ (My devotee), saḥ (he), me priyaḥ (is dear to Me).
Meaning: …ever content, a yogi, self-controlled, of firm resolve, with mind and intellect offered to Me — such a devotee of Mine is dear to Me.
Verse 15
यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः । हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १५॥
యస్మాన్-నోద్విజతే లోకో లోకాన్-నోద్విజతే చ యః ।
హర్షామర్ష-భయోద్వేగైర్-ముక్తో యః స చ మే ప్రియః ॥ 15 ॥
yasmān nodvijate loko lokān nodvijate ca yaḥ | harṣāmarṣa-bhayodvegair mukto yaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ || 15 ||
Words: yasmāt (from whom), na udvijate (is not agitated), lokaḥ (the world / people), lokāt (from the world), na udvijate (is not agitated), ca yaḥ (and who), harṣa (elation), amarṣa (impatience / envy), bhaya (fear), udvegaiḥ (and anxiety), muktaḥ (freed), yaḥ saḥ (he who), ca me priyaḥ (is also dear to Me).
Meaning: He by whom the world is not agitated, and who is not agitated by the world, who is free from elation, envy, fear, and anxiety — he too is dear to Me.
Verse 16
अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः । सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥ १६॥
అనపేక్షః శుచిర్-దక్ష ఉదాసీనో గత-వ్యథః ।
సర్వారంభ-పరిత్యాగీ యో మద్-భక్తః స మే ప్రియః ॥ 16 ॥
anapekṣaḥ śucir dakṣa udāsīno gata-vyathaḥ | sarvārambha-parityāgī yo mad-bhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ || 16 ||
Words: anapekṣaḥ (without dependence / expectation), śuciḥ (pure), dakṣaḥ (skillful / able), udāsīnaḥ (impartial), gata-vyathaḥ (free from distress), sarva-ārambha-parityāgī (renouncer of all selfish undertakings), yaḥ mad-bhaktaḥ (who is My devotee), saḥ me priyaḥ (he is dear to Me).
Meaning: He who is free from dependence, pure, able, impartial, untroubled, and who has renounced all selfish undertakings — such a devotee of Mine is dear to Me.
Verse 17
यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति । शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः ॥ १७॥
యో న హృష్యతి న ద్వేష్టి న శోచతి న కాంక్షతి ।
శుభాశుభ-పరిత్యాగీ భక్తిమాన్-యః స మే ప్రియః ॥ 17 ॥
yo na hṛṣyati na dveṣṭi na śocati na kāṅkṣati | śubhāśubha-parityāgī bhaktimān yaḥ sa me priyaḥ || 17 ||
Words: yaḥ (who), na hṛṣyati (neither rejoices), na dveṣṭi (nor hates), na śocati (nor grieves), na kāṅkṣati (nor desires), śubha-aśubha-parityāgī (renouncer of good and bad / agreeable and disagreeable), bhaktimān (full of devotion), yaḥ saḥ me priyaḥ (he is dear to Me).
Meaning: He who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor desires, who renounces both the agreeable and the disagreeable, and is full of devotion — he is dear to Me.
Verse 18
समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः । शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः ॥ १८॥
సమః శత్రౌ చ మిత్రే చ తథా మానాపమానయోః ।
శీతోష్ణ-సుఖ-దుఃఖేషు సమః సంగ-వివర్జితః ॥ 18 ॥
samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca tathā mānāpamānayoḥ | śītoṣṇa-sukha-duḥkheṣu samaḥ saṅga-vivarjitaḥ || 18 ||
Words: samaḥ (equal / balanced), śatrau ca mitre ca (toward enemy and friend), tathā (likewise), māna-apamānayoḥ (in honor and dishonor), śīta-uṣṇa-sukha-duḥkheṣu (in cold and heat, pleasure and pain), samaḥ (balanced), saṅga-vivarjitaḥ (free from attachment).
Meaning: He who is the same to foe and friend, and in honor and dishonor; the same in cold and heat, pleasure and pain; free from attachment…
Verse 19
तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् । अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः ॥ १९॥
తుల్య-నిందా-స్తుతిర్-మౌనీ సంతుష్టో యేన కేనచిత్ ।
అనికేతః స్థిర-మతిర్-భక్తిమాన్-మే ప్రియో నరః ॥ 19 ॥
tulya-nindā-stutir maunī santuṣṭo yena kenacit | aniketaḥ sthira-matir bhaktimān me priyo naraḥ || 19 ||
Words: tulya-nindā-stutiḥ (to whom blame and praise are alike), maunī (silent / restrained in speech), santuṣṭaḥ (content), yena kenacit (with whatever comes), aniketaḥ (without a fixed home / unattached to dwelling), sthira-matiḥ (of steady mind), bhaktimān (devoted), me priyaḥ naraḥ (that person is dear to Me).
Meaning: …to whom censure and praise are equal, who is silent, content with anything, homeless (unattached), of steady mind, and full of devotion — that person is dear to Me.
Verse 20
ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते । श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः ॥ २०॥
యే తు ధర్మ్యామృతమిదం యథోక్తం పర్యుపాసతే ।
శ్రద్దధానా మత్-పరమా భక్తాస్-తేఽతీవ మే ప్రియాః ॥ 20 ॥
ye tu dharmyāmṛtam idaṃ yathoktaṃ paryupāsate | śraddadhānā mat-paramā bhaktās te 'tīva me priyāḥ || 20 ||
Words: ye tu (but those who), dharmya-amṛtam idam (this nectar of dharma), yathā-uktam (as declared), paryupāsate (follow / practice), śraddadhānāḥ (with faith), mat-paramāḥ (holding Me as the supreme), bhaktāḥ te (those devotees), atīva (exceedingly), me priyāḥ (are dear to Me).
Meaning: But those who follow this immortal teaching of dharma as declared, with faith, holding Me as the supreme — those devotees are exceedingly dear to Me.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे भक्तियोगो नाम द्वादशोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the twelfth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Bhakti Yoga — the Yoga of Devotion.
