Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 6

अध्यायः ६ · ध्यानयोगः — The Yoga of Meditation

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 47 verses.

Krishna teaches the yoga of self-discipline and meditation — lifting oneself by the Self, seating in solitude, restraining the restless mind, and seeing the same Self in all. Arjuna doubts the mind can be controlled; Krishna assures that practice and detachment succeed, and that even the fallen yogi is not lost. The highest yogi is the one who worships Him with faith, absorbed within.

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca

sa sannyāsī cha yogī cha na niragnir na chākriyaḥ || 1 ||
Words: anāśritaḥ (not desiring), karma-phalam (results of actions), kāryam (obligatory), karma (work), karoti (perform), yaḥ (one who), saḥ (that person), sanyāsī (in the renounced order), cha (and), yogī (yogi), cha (and), na (not), niḥ (without), agniḥ (fire), na (not), cha (also), akriyaḥ (without activity)
Meaning: He who performs an action which is his duty, without depending on the result of the action, is a monk and a yogi; not so in he who does not keep afire and is actionless."

अभ्यासेन तु कौन्तेय वैराग्येण च गृह्यते ॥ ३५॥
అసంశయం మహా-బాహో మనో దుర్నిగ్రహం చలం ।
అభ్యాసేన తు కౌన్తేయ వైరాగ్యేణ చ గృహ్యతే ॥ ౩౫॥
asanśayaṁ mahā-bāho mano durnigrahaṁ chalam |
abhyāsena tu kaunteya vairāgyeṇa cha gṛhyate || 35 ||
Words: asanśayam (undoubtedly), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed one), manaḥ (the mind), durnigraham (difficult to restrain), chalam (restless), abhyāsena (by practice), tu (but), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), vairāgyeṇa (by detachment), cha (and), gṛhyate (can be controlled)
Meaning: O mighty-armed one, undoubtedly the mind is untamable and restless. But, O son of Kunti, it can be brought under control through practice and detachment.
Verse 36
असंयतात्मना योगो दुष्प्राप इति मे मतिः ।
वश्यात्मना तु यतता शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः ॥ ३६॥
అసంయతాత్మనా యోగో దుష్ప్రాప ఇతి మే మతిః ।
వశ్యాత్మనా తు యతతా శక్యోఽవాప్తుముపాయతః ॥ ౩౬॥
asaṅyatātmanā yogo duṣprāpa iti me matiḥ |
vaśyātmanā tu yatatā śakyo ’vāptum upāyataḥ || 36 ||
Words: asanyata-ātmanā (one whose mind is unbridled), yogaḥ (Yog), duṣprāpaḥ (difficult to attain), iti (thus), me (my), matiḥ (opinion), vaśya-ātmanā (by one whose mind is controlled), tu (but), yatatā (one who strives), śakyaḥ (possible), avāptum (to achieve), upāyataḥ (by right means)
Meaning: My conviction is that Yoga is difficult to attain for one with an uncontrolled mind. But it is possible to attain through the aforementioned means for one who strives and has a controlled mind.
Verse 37
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाच्चलितमानसः ।
अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति ॥ ३७॥
అయతిః శ్రద్ధయోపేతో యోగాచ్చలితమానసః ।
అప్రాప్య యోగ-సంసిద్ధిం కాం గతిం కృష్ణ గచ్ఛతి ॥ ౩౭॥
ayatiḥ śraddhayopeto yogāch chalita-mānasaḥ |
aprāpya yoga-sansiddhiṁ kāṅ gatiṁ kṛṣṇa gachchhati || 37 ||
Words: ayatiḥ (lax), śraddhayā (with faith), upetaḥ (possessed), yogāt (from Yog), chalita-mānasaḥ (whose mind becomes deviated), aprāpya (failing to attain), yoga-sansiddhim (the highest perfection in yog), kām (which), gatim (destination), kṛṣṇa (Shree Krishna), gachchhati (goes)
Meaning: O Krsna, what goal does one attain who, despite having faith, fails to achieve perfection in Yoga due to lack of diligence, and whose mind becomes deflected from Yoga?"
Verse 38
कच्चिन्नोभयविभ्रष्टश्छिन्नाभ्रमिव नश्यति ।
अप्रतिष्ठो महाबाहो विमूढो ब्रह्मणः पथि ॥ ३८॥
కచ్చిన్నోభయవిభ్రష్టశ్ఛిన్నాభ్రమివ నశ్యతి ।
అప్రతిష్ఠో మహా-బాహో విమూఢో బ్రహ్మణః పథి ॥ ౩౮॥
kachchin nobhaya-vibhraṣṭaś chhinnābhram iva naśyati |
apratiṣṭho mahā-bāho vimūḍho brahmaṇaḥ pathi || 38 ||
Words: kachchit (whether), na (not), ubhaya (both), vibhraṣṭaḥ (deviated from), chhinna (broken), abhram (cloud), iva (like), naśyati (perishes), apratiṣṭhaḥ (without any support), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed Krishna), vimūḍhaḥ (bewildered), brahmaṇaḥ (of God-realization), pathi (one on the path)
Meaning: O Mighty-armed one, having fallen from both, without support, deluded on the path to Brahman, does he not get ruined like a scattered cloud?
Verse 39
एतन्मे संशयं कृष्ण छेत्तुमर्हस्यशेषतः ।
त्वदन्यः संशयस्यास्य छेत्ता न ह्युपपद्यते ॥ ३९॥
ఏతన్మే సంశయం కృష్ణ ఛేత్తుమర్హస్యశేషతః ।
త్వదన్యః సంశయస్యాస్య ఛేత్తా న హ్యుపపద్యతే ॥ ౩౯॥
etan me sanśayaṁ kṛṣṇa chhettum arhasyaśeṣataḥ |
tvad-anyaḥ sanśayasyāsya chhettā na hyupapadyate || 39 ||
Words: etat (this), me (my), sanśayam (doubt), kṛṣṇa (Krishna), chhettum (to dispel), arhasi (you can), aśeṣataḥ (completely), tvat (than you), anyaḥ (other), sanśayasya (of doubt), asya (this), chhettā (a dispeller), na (never), hi (certainly), upapadyate (is fit)
Meaning: O Krsna, you should totally eradicate this doubt of mine, for none other than yourself can be the dispeller of this doubt!
Verse 40
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते ।
नहि कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद्दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति ॥ ४०॥
పార్థ నైవేహ నాముత్ర వినాశస్తస్య విద్యతే ।
నహి కల్యాణకృత్కశ్చిద్దుర్గతిం తాత గచ్ఛతి ॥ ౪౦॥
pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśas tasya vidyate |
na hi kalyāṇa-kṛt kaśchid durgatiṁ tāta gachchhati || 40 ||
Words: pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), na eva (never), iha (in this world), na (never), amutra (in the next world), vināśaḥ (destruction), tasya (his), vidyate (exists), na (never), hi (certainly), kalyāṇa-kṛt (one who strives for God-realization), kaśchit (anyone), durgatim (evil destination), tāta (my friend), gachchhati (goes)
Meaning: O Partha, there is certainly no ruin for him here or hereafter, for no one engaged in good meets with a deplorable end, my son!"
Verse 41
प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः ।
शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते ॥ ४१॥
ప్రాప్య పుణ్యకృతాం లోకానుషిత్వా శాశ్వతీః సమాః ।
శుచీనాం శ్రీమతాం గేహే యోగ-భ్రష్టోఽభిజాయతే ॥ ౪౧॥
prāpya puṇya-kṛtāṁ lokān uṣitvā śāśvatīḥ samāḥ |
śuchīnāṁ śrīmatāṁ gehe yoga-bhraṣṭo’bhijāyate || 41 ||
Words: prāpya (attain), puṇya-kṛtām (of the virtuous), lokān (abodes), uṣitvā (after dwelling), śāśvatīḥ (many), samāḥ (ages), śuchīnām (of the pious), śrī-matām (of the prosperous), gehe (in the house), yoga-bhraṣṭaḥ (the unsuccessful yogis), abhijāyate (take birth)
Meaning: Attaining the worlds of the righteous and residing there for eternal years, the man who has fallen from Yoga is born in the house of the pious and prosperous.
Verse 42
अथवा योगिनामेव कुले भवति धीमताम् ।
एतद्धि दुर्लभतरं लोके जन्म यदीदृशम् ॥ ४२॥
అథవా యోగినామేవ కులే భవతి ధీమతామ్ ।
ఏతద్ధి దుర్లభతరం లోకే జన్మ యదీదృశమ్ ॥ ౪౨॥
atha vā yoginām eva kule bhavati dhīmatām |
etad dhi durlabhataraṁ loke janma yad īdṛśam || 42 ||
Words: atha vā (else), yoginām (of those endowed with divine wisdom), eva (certainly), kule (in the family), bhavati (take birth), dhī-matām (of the wise), etat (this), hi (certainly), durlabha-taram (very rare), loke (in this world), janma (birth), yat (which), īdṛśam (like this)
Meaning: Or, he is born in the family of wise yogis. Such a birth is indeed more difficult to come by in the world.
Verse 43
तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम् ।
यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन ॥ ४३॥
తత్ర తం బుద్ధిసంయోగం లభతే పౌర్వదేహికమ్ ।
యతతే చ తతో భూయః సంసిద్ధౌ కురు-నన్దన ॥ ౪౩॥
tatra taṁ buddhi-sanyogaṁ labhate paurva-dehikam |
yatate cha tato bhūyaḥ sansiddhau kuru-nandana || 43 ||
Words: tatra (there), tam (that), buddhi-sanyogam (reawaken their wisdom), labhate (obtains), paurva-dehikam (from the previous lives), yatate (strives), cha (and), tataḥ (thereafter), bhūyaḥ (again), sansiddhau (for perfection), kuru-nandana (Arjun, descendant of the Kurus)
Meaning: There he becomes endowed with the wisdom acquired in the previous body, and he strives even more than before for perfection, O scion of the Kuru dynasty.
Verse 44
पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव ह्रियते ह्यवशोऽपि सः ।
जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य शब्दब्रह्मातिवर्तते ॥ ४४॥
పూర్వాభ్యాసేన తేనైవ హ్రియతే హ్యవశోఽపి సః ।
జిజ్ఞాసురపి యోగస్య శబ్దబ్రహ్మాతివర్తతే ॥ ౪౪॥
pūrvābhyāsena tenaiva hriyate hyavaśo ’pi saḥ |
jijñāsur api yogasya śabda-brahmātivartate || 44 ||
Words: pūrva (past), abhyāsena (discipline), tena (by that), eva (certainly), hriyate (is attracted), hi (surely), avaśaḥ (helplessly), api (although), saḥ (that person), jijñāsuḥ (inquisitive), api (even), yogasya (about yog), śabda-brahma (fruitive portion of the Vedas), ativartate (transcends)
Meaning: For, by that very past practice, he is carried forward even in spite of himself! Even a seeker of Yoga transcends the results of the performance of Vedic rituals!
Verse 45
प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संशुद्धकिल्बिषः ।
अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम् ॥ ४५॥
ప్రయత్నాద్యతమానస్తు యోగీ సంశుద్ధకిల్బిషః ।
అనేకజన్మసంసిద్ధస్తతో యాతి పరాం గతిమ్ ॥ ౪౫॥
prayatnād yatamānas tu yogī sanśuddha-kilbiṣaḥ |
aneka-janma-sansiddhas tato yāti parāṁ gatim || 45 ||
Words: prayatnāt (with great effort), yatamānaḥ (endeavoring), tu (and), yogī (a yogi), sanśuddha (purified), kilbiṣaḥ (from material desires), aneka (after many, many), janma (births), sansiddhaḥ (attain perfection), tataḥ (then), yāti (attains), parām (the highest), gatim (path)
Meaning: However, the yogi, applying himself assiduously, becoming purified from sin and attaining perfection through many births, they achieve the highest Goal.
Verse 46
तपस्विभ्योऽधिको योगी ज्ञानिभ्योऽपि मतोऽधिकः ।
कर्मिभ्यश्चाधिको योगी तस्माद्योगी भवार्जुन ॥ ४६॥
తపస్విభ్యోఽధికో యోగీ జ్ఞానిభ్యోఽపి మతోఽధికః ।
కర్మిభ్యశ్చాధికో యోగీ తస్మాద్యోగీ భవార్జున ॥ ౪౬॥
tapasvibhyo ’dhiko yogī |
jñānibhyo ’pi mato ’dhikaḥ karmibhyaś chādhiko yogī tasmād yogī bhavārjuna || 46 ||
Words: tapasvibhyaḥ (than the ascetics), adhikaḥ (superior), yogī (a yogi), jñānibhyaḥ (than the persons of learning), api (even), mataḥ (considered), adhikaḥ (superior), karmibhyaḥ (than the ritualistic performers), cha (and), adhikaḥ (superior), yogī (a yogi), tasmāt (therefore), yogī (a yogi), bhava (just become), arjuna (Arjun)
Meaning: A yogi is higher than those of austerity; they are considered higher even than those of knowledge. The yogi is also higher than those of action. Therefore, O Arjuna, become a yogi.
Verse 47
योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना ।
श्रद्धावान्भजते यो मां स मे युक्ततमो मतः ॥ ४७॥
యోగినామపి సర్వేషాం మద్గతేన అన్తర-ఆత్మనా ।
శ్రద్ధావాన్ భజతే యో మాం స మే యుక్త-తమో మతః ॥ ౪౭॥
yoginām api sarveṣāṁ mad-gatenāntar-ātmanā |
śraddhāvān bhajate yo māṁ sa me yuktatamo mataḥ || 47 ||
Words: yoginām (of all yogis), api (however), sarveṣām (all types of), mat-gatena (absorbed in me (God)), antaḥ (inner), ātmanā (with the mind), śraddhā-vān (with great faith), bhajate (engage in devotion), yaḥ (who), mām (to me), saḥ (he), me (by me), yukta-tamaḥ (the highest yogi), mataḥ (is considered)
Meaning: Even among all the yogis, he who adores Me with his mind fixed on Me and with faith is considered by Me to be the best of the yogis.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे ध्यानयोगो नाम षष्ठोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the sixth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Dhyāna Yoga — the Yoga of Meditation.
