Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 18

अध्यायः १८ · मोक्षसंन्यासयोगः — The Yoga of Liberation through Renunciation

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 78 verses.

Arjuna asks the difference between renunciation and abandonment. Krishna explains tyāga and sannyāsa, the threefold nature of knowledge, action, doer, intellect, firmness, and happiness, the duties of the four varṇas, and the path of surrender — culminating in Arjuna’s resolve to fight and Sañjaya’s closing praise of the dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna.

The Seventy-Eight Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
संन्यासस्य महाबाहो तत्त्वमिच्छामि वेदितुम् ।
त्यागस्य च हृषीकेश पृथक्केशिनिषूदन ॥ १॥
సంన్యాసస్య మహా-బాహో తత్త్వమిచ్ఛామి వేదితుమ్ ।
త్యాగస్య చ హృషీకేశ పృథక్-కేశి-నిషూదన ॥ 1 ॥
sannyāsasya mahā-bāho tattvam ichchhāmi veditum |
tyāgasya cha hṛṣīkeśa pṛthak keśi-niṣūdana || 1 ||
Words: sanyāsasya (of renunciation of actions), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed one), tattvam (the truth), ichchhāmi (I wish), veditum (to understand), tyāgasya (of renunciation of desires for enjoying the fruits of actions), cha (and), hṛṣīkeśa (Krishna, the Lord of the senses), pṛthak (distinctively), keśī-niṣūdana (Krishna, the killer of the Keshi demon)
Meaning: O mighty-armed Hrsikesa, O slayer of Kesi, I want to know separately the truth about sannyasa as well as about tyaga.
Verse 2
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासं संन्यासं कवयो विदुः ।
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं प्राहुस्त्यागं विचक्षणाः ॥ २॥

సర్వ-కర్మ-ఫల-త్యాగం ప్రాహుస్-త్యాగం విచక్షణాః ॥ 2 ॥

Words: kāmyānām (desireful), karmaṇām (of actions), nyāsam (giving up), sanyāsam (renunciation of actions), kavayaḥ (the learned), viduḥ (to understand), sarva (all), karma-phala (fruits of actions), tyāgam (renunciation of desires for enjoying the fruits of actions), prāhuḥ (declare), tyāgam (renunciation of desires for enjoying the fruits of actions), vichakṣaṇāḥ (the wise)
Meaning: The learned ones know sannyasa to be the giving up of actions done with a desire for reward. The adepts call the abandonment of the results of all works 'tyaga'."

Meaning: The practice of sacrifice, charity, and austerity should not be abandoned; it should certainly be undertaken. Sacrifice, charity, and austerity are indeed the purifiers of the wise.
Verse 6

Meaning: But even these actions must be undertaken by renouncing attachment and hankering for results. This is My firm and definitive conclusion, O Partha.

భక్తిం మయి పరాం కృత్వా మామేవైష్యత్యసంశయః ॥ 68 ॥
ya idaṁ paramaṁ guhyaṁ mad-bhakteṣv abhidhāsyati |
bhaktiṁ mayi parāṁ kṛtvā mām evaiṣyaty asanśayaḥ || 68 ||
Words: yaḥ (who), idam (this), paramam (most), guhyam (confidential knowledge), mat-bhakteṣu (amongst my devotees), abhidhāsyati (teaches), bhaktim (greatest act of love), mayi (to me), parām (transcendental), kṛtvā (doing), mām (to me), eva (certainly), eṣyati (comes), asanśayaḥ (without doubt)
Meaning: He who, entertaining supreme devotion to Me, speaks of this highest secret to My devotees, will without doubt reach Me alone.
Verse 69
न च तस्मान्मनुष्येषु कश्चिन्मे प्रियकृत्तमः ।
भविता न च मे तस्मादन्यः प्रियतरो भुवि ॥ ६९॥
న చ తస్మాన్-మనుష్యేషు కశ్చిన్-మే ప్రియ-కృత్తమః ।
భవితా న చ మే తస్మాదన్యః ప్రియతరో భువి ॥ 69 ॥
na cha tasmān manuṣyeṣu kaśchin me priya-kṛttamaḥ |
bhavitā na cha me tasmād anyaḥ priyataro bhuvi || 69 ||
Words: na (no), cha (and), tasmāt (than them), manuṣyeṣu (amongst human beings), kaśchit (anyone), me (to me), priya-kṛt-tamaḥ (more dear), bhavitā (will be), na (never), cha (and), me (to me), tasmāt (than them), anyaḥ (another), priya-taraḥ (dearer), bhuvi (on this earth)
Meaning: And compared to him, no other human is better at accomplishing what is dear to Me. Furthermore, there will be no one else in the world dearer to Me than him.
Verse 70
अध्येष्यते च य इमं धर्म्यं संवादमावयोः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेनाहमिष्टः स्यामिति मे मतिः ॥ ७०॥
అధ్యేష్యతే చ య ఇమం ధర్మ్యం సంవాదమావయోః ।
జ్ఞాన-యజ్ఞేన తేనాహమిష్టః స్యామితి మే మతిః ॥ 70 ॥
adhyeṣyate cha ya imaṁ dharmyaṁ saṁvādam āvayoḥ |
jñāna-yajñena tenāham iṣṭaḥ syām iti me matiḥ || 70 ||
Words: adhyeṣyate (study), cha (and), yaḥ (who), imam (this), dharmyam (sacred), saṁvādam (dialogue), āvayoḥ (of ours), jñāna (of knowledge), yajñena-tena (through the sacrifice of knowledge), aham (I), iṣṭaḥ (worshipped), syām (shall be), iti (such), me (my), matiḥ (opinion)
Meaning: And he who will study this sacred conversation between us two, which is conducive to virtue, by him I shall be adored through the Sacrifice of Knowledge. This is My judgment.
Verse 71
श्रद्धावाननसूयश्च शृणुयादपि यो नरः ।
सोऽपि मुक्तः शुभाँल्लोकान्प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यकर्मणाम् ॥ ७१॥
శ్రద్ధావాననసూయశ్చ శృణుయాదపి యో నరః ।
సోఽపి ముక్తః శుభాంల్లోకాన్ప్రాప్నుయాత్పుణ్యకర్మణామ్ ॥ 71 ॥
śraddhāvān anasūyaś cha śṛṇuyād api yo naraḥ |
so ‘pi muktaḥ śubhāl lokān prāpnuyāt puṇya-karmaṇām || 71 ||
Words: śraddhā-vān (faithful), anasūyaḥ (without envy), cha (and), śṛṇuyāt (listen), api (certainly), yaḥ (who), naraḥ (a person), saḥ (that person), api (also), muktaḥ (liberated), śubhān (the auspicious), lokān (abodes), prāpnuyāt (attain), puṇya-karmaṇām (of the pious)
Meaning: Any man who, being reverent and free from caviling, listens to this (teaching) with reverence, he too, becoming free, shall attain the blessed worlds of those who perform virtuous deeds.
Verse 72
कच्चिदेतच्छ्रुतं पार्थ त्वयैकाग्रेण चेतसा ।
कच्चिदज्ञानसंमोहः प्रनष्टस्ते धनञ्जय ॥ ७२॥
కచ్చిదేతచ్-ఛ్రుతం పార్థ త్వయైకాగ్రేణ చేతసా ।
కచ్చిదజ్ఞాన-సంమోహః ప్రనష్టస్-తే ధనంజయ ॥ 72 ॥
kachchid etach chhrutaṁ pārtha tvayaikāgreṇa chetasā |
kachchid ajñāna-sammohaḥ pranaṣṭas te dhanañjaya || 72 ||
Words: kachchit (whether), etat (this), śrutam (heard), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), tvayā (by you), eka-agreṇa chetasā (with a concentrated mind), kachchit (whether), ajñāna (ignorance), sammohaḥ (delusion), pranaṣṭaḥ (destroyed), te (your), dhanañjaya (Arjun, conqueror of wealth)
Meaning: O Partha, have you listened to this with a one-pointed mind? O Dhananjaya, has your delusion caused by ignorance been dispelled?
Verse 73
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
नष्टो मोहः स्मृतिर्लब्धा त्वत्प्रसादान्मयाच्युत ।
स्थितोऽस्मि गतसन्देहः करिष्ये वचनं तव ॥ ७३॥
నష్టో మోహః స్మృతిర్-లబ్ధా త్వత్-ప్రసాదాన్-మయాచ్యుత ।
స్థితోఽస్మి గత-సందేహః కరిష్యే వచనం తవ ॥ 73 ॥
naṣṭo mohaḥ smṛtir labdhā tvat-prasādān mayāchyuta |
sthito ‘smi gata-sandehaḥ kariṣye vachanaṁ tava || 73 ||
Words: naṣṭaḥ (dispelled), mohaḥ (illusion), smṛtiḥ (memory), labdhā (regained), tvat-prasādāt (by your grace), mayā (by me), achyuta (Shree Krishna, the infallible one), sthitaḥ (situated), asmi (I am), gata-sandehaḥ (free from doubts), kariṣye (I shall act), vachanam (instructions), tava (your)
Meaning: O Acyuta, my delusion has been destroyed and my memory has been regained through Your grace. I stand with my doubt removed; I shall follow Your instructions."
Verse 74
सञ्जय उवाच · sañjaya uvāca
इत्यहं वासुदेवस्य पार्थस्य च महात्मनः ।
संवादमिममश्रौषमद्भुतं रोमहर्षणम् ॥ ७४॥
ఇత్యహం వాసుదేవస్య పార్థస్య చ మహాత్మనః ।
సంవాదమిమమశ్రౌషమద్భుతం రోమ-హర్షణమ్ ॥ 74 ॥
ity ahaṁ vāsudevasya pārthasya cha mahātmanaḥ |
saṁvādam imam aśrauṣam adbhutaṁ roma-harṣaṇam || 74 ||
Words: iti (thus), aham (I), vāsudevasya (of Shree Krishna), pārthasya (Arjun), cha (and), mahā-ātmanaḥ (the noble hearted soul), saṁvādam (conversation), imam (this), aśrauṣam (have heard), adbhutam (wonderful), roma-harṣaṇam (which causes the hair to stand on end)
Meaning: I thus heard this conversation between Vasudeva and the great-souled Partha, which is unique and makes one's hair stand on end."
Verse 75
व्यासप्रसादाच्छ्रुतवानेतद्गुह्यमहं परम् ।
योगं योगेश्वरात्कृष्णात्साक्षात्कथयतः स्वयम् ॥ ७५॥
వ్యాస-ప్రసాదాచ్-ఛ్రుతవానేతద్-గుహ్యమహం పరమ్ ।
యోగం యోగేశ్వరాత్-కృష్ణాత్-సాక్షాత్-కథయతః స్వయమ్ ॥ 75 ॥
vyāsa-prasādāch chhrutavān etad guhyam ahaṁ param |

Words: vyāsa-prasādāt (by the grace of Ved Vyas), śrutavān (have heard), etat (this), guhyam (secret), aham (I), param (supreme), yogam (Yog), yoga-īśvarāt (from the Lod of Yog), kṛṣṇāt (from Shree Krishna), sākṣāt (directly), kathayataḥ (speaking), svayam (himself)
Meaning: Through the favor of Vyasa, I heard this secret concerning the supreme Yoga from Krishna, the Lord of Yogas, while He Himself was speaking!
Verse 76

केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः ॥ ७६॥
రాజన్-సంస్మృత్య సంస్మృత్య సంవాదమిమమద్భుతమ్ ।
కేశవార్జునయోః పుణ్యం హృష్యామి చ ముహుర్-ముహుః ॥ 76 ॥
rājan sansmṛtya sansmṛtya saṁvādam imam adbhutam |
keśavārjunayoḥ puṇyaṁ hṛṣyāmi cha muhur muhuḥ || 76 ||
Words: rājan (King), sansmṛtya saṁsmṛtya (repeatedly recalling), saṁvādam (dialogue), imam (this), adbhutam (astonishing), keśava-arjunayoḥ (between Lord Shree Krishna and Arjun), puṇyam (pious), hṛṣyāmi (I rejoice), cha (and), muhuḥ muhuḥ (repeatedly)
Meaning: And, O King, while repeatedly remembering this unique, sacred dialogue between Kesava and Arjuna, I rejoice every moment.
Verse 77
तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः ।

Words: tat (that), cha (and), sansmṛtya saṁsmṛtya (remembering repeatedly), rūpam (cosmic form), ati (most), adbhutam (wonderful), hareḥ (of Lord Krishna), vismayaḥ (astonishment), me (my), mahān (great), rājan (King), hṛṣyāmi (I am thrilled with joy), cha (and), punaḥ punaḥ (over and over again)
Meaning: O King, repeatedly recollecting that greatly extraordinary form of Hari, I am struck with wonder and rejoice again and again.
Verse 78

Words: yatra (wherever), yoga-īśvaraḥ (Shree Krishna, the Lord of Yog), kṛṣṇaḥ (Shree Krishna), yatra (wherever), pārthaḥ (Arjun, the son of Pritha), dhanuḥ-dharaḥ (the supreme archer), tatra (there), śrīḥ (opulence), vijayaḥ (victory), bhūtiḥ (prosperity), dhruvā (unending), nītiḥ (righteousness), matiḥ mama (my opinion)
Meaning: Where there is Krishna, the Lord of yogas, and where there is Partha, the wielder of the bow, there is fortune, victory, prosperity, and unfailing prudence. Such is my conviction.
