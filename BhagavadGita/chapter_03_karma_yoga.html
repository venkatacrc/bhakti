Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 3

अध्यायः ३ · कर्मयोगः — The Yoga of Action

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 43 verses.

Arjuna asks why he should act if knowledge is superior. Krishna teaches Karma Yoga — performing one’s duty without attachment, for the welfare of the world, offering all action to Him — and identifies desire and anger as the enemies that bind the soul.

The Forty-Three Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव ॥ १॥
జ్యాయసీ చేత్-కర్మణస్-తే మతా బుద్ధిర్-జనార్దన ।
తత్-కిం కర్మణి ఘోరే మాం నియోజయసి కేశవ ॥ 1 ॥
jyāyasī chet karmaṇas te matā buddhir janārdana |
tat kiṁ karmaṇi ghore māṁ niyojayasi keśava || 1 ||
Words: jyāyasī (superior), chet (if), karmaṇaḥ (than fruitive action), te (by you), matā (is considered), buddhiḥ (intellect), janārdana (he who looks after the public, Krishna), tat (then), kim (why), karmaṇi (action), ghore (terrible), mām (me), niyojayasi (do you engage), keśava (Krishna, the killer of the demon named Keshi)
Meaning: O Janardana (Krishna), if it is Your opinion that wisdom is superior to action, why do You urge me to take such horrible action, O Kesava?"
Verse 2
व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम् ॥ २॥
వ్యామిశ్రేణేవ వాక్యేన బుద్ధిం మోహయసీవ మే ।
తదేకం వద నిశ్చిత్య యేన శ్రేయోఽహమాప్నుయామ్ ॥ 2 ॥
vyāmiśreṇeva vākyena buddhiṁ mohayasīva me |
tad ekaṁ vada niśchitya yena śreyo ’ham āpnuyām || 2 ||
Words: vyāmiśreṇa iva (by your apparently ambiguous), vākyena (words), buddhim (intellect), mohayasi (I am getting bewildered), iva (as it were), me (my), tat (therefore), ekam (one), vada (please tell), niśchitya (decisively), yena (by which), śreyaḥ (the highest good), aham (I), āpnuyām (may attain)
Meaning: You bewilder my understanding, as it were, with a seemingly conflicting statement! Tell me for certain which one of these I may use to attain the highest Good.
Verse 3
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ ।
ज्ञानयोगेन सांख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम् ॥ ३॥
లోకేఽస్మిం-ద్వి-విధా నిష్ఠా పురా ప్రోక్తా మయానఘ ।
జ్ఞాన-యోగేన సాంఖ్యానాం కర్మ-యోగేన యోగినామ్ ॥ 3 ॥
loke’smin dvi-vidhā niṣṭhā purā proktā mayānagha |
jñāna-yogena sāṅkhyānāṁ karma-yogena yoginām || 3 ||
Words: loke (in the world), asmin (this), dvi-vidhā (two kinds of), niṣṭhā (faith), purā (previously), proktā (explained), mayā (by me (Shree Krishna)), anagha (sinless), jñāna-yogena (through the path of knowledge), sānkhyānām (for those inclined toward contemplation), karma-yogena (through the path of action), yoginām (of the yogis)
Meaning: O unblemished one, two kinds of steadfastness in this world were spoken of by Me in days gone by—through the Yoga of Knowledge for the men of realization; through the Yoga of Action for the yogis.
Verse 4
न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते ।
न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति ॥ ४॥
న కర్మణామనారంభాన్-నైష్కర్మ్యం పురుషోఽశ్నుతే ।
న చ సంన్యసనాదేవ సిద్ధిం సమధిగచ్ఛతి ॥ 4 ॥
na karmaṇām anārambhān naiṣkarmyaṁ puruṣo ’śnute |
na cha sannyasanād eva siddhiṁ samadhigachchhati || 4 ||
Words: na (not), karmaṇām (of actions), anārambhāt (by abstaining from), naiṣkarmyam (freedom from karmic reactions), puruṣaḥ (a person), aśnute (attains), na (not), cha (and), sannyasanāt (by renunciation), eva (only), siddhim (perfection), samadhigachchhati (attains)
Meaning: A person does not attain freedom from action by abstaining from action; nor does he attain fulfillment merely through renunciation.
Verse 5
न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥ ५॥
న హి కశ్చిత్-క్షణమపి జాతు తిష్ఠత్యకర్మ-కృత్ ।
కార్యతే హ్యవశః కర్మ సర్వః ప్రకృతి-జైర్-గుణైః ॥ 5 ॥
na hi kaśchit kṣaṇam api jātu tiṣṭhatyakarma-kṛt |
kāryate hyavaśaḥ karma sarvaḥ prakṛti-jair guṇaiḥ || 5 ||
Words: na (not), hi (certainly), kaśchit (anyone), kṣaṇam (a moment), api (even), jātu (ever), tiṣṭhati (can remain), akarma-kṛt (without action), kāryate (are performed), hi (certainly), avaśaḥ (helpless), karma (work), sarvaḥ (all), prakṛti-jaiḥ (born of material nature), guṇaiḥ (by the qualities)
Meaning: For no one ever remains even for a moment without doing work, for all are made to work under compulsion by the gunas born of Nature.
Verse 6
कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते ॥ ६॥
కర్మేంద్రియాణి సంయమ్య య ఆస్తే మనసా స్మరన్ ।
ఇంద్రియార్థాన్-విమూఢాత్మా మిథ్యాచారః స ఉచ్యతే ॥ 6 ॥
karmendriyāṇi sanyamya ya āste manasā smaran |
indriyārthān vimūḍhātmā mithyāchāraḥ sa uchyate || 6 ||
Words: karma-indriyāṇi (the organs of action), sanyamya (restrain), yaḥ (who), āste (remain), manasā (in the mind), smaran (to remember), indriya-arthān (sense objects), vimūḍha-ātmā (the deluded), mithyā-āchāraḥ (hypocrite), saḥ (they), uchyate (are called)
Meaning: One who, after withdrawing the organs of action, sits mentally recollecting the objects of the senses, is called a hypocrite, for they have a deluded mind.
Verse 7
यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।
कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते ॥ ७॥
యస్-త్వింద్రియాణి మనసా నియమ్యారభతేఽర్జున ।
కర్మేంద్రియైః కర్మ-యోగమసక్తః స విశిష్యతే ॥ 7 ॥
yas tvindriyāṇi manasā niyamyārabhate ’rjuna |
karmendriyaiḥ karma-yogam asaktaḥ sa viśiṣyate || 7 ||
Words: yaḥ (who), tu (but), indriyāṇi (the senses), manasā (by the mind), niyamya (control), ārabhate (begins), arjuna (Arjun), karma-indriyaiḥ (by the working senses), karma-yogam (karm yog), asaktaḥ (without attachment), saḥ (they), viśiṣyate (are superior)
Meaning: But, O Arjuna, one who engages in Karma-yoga, controlling the organs of action with the mind and becoming unattached, excels.
Verse 8
नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः ॥ ८॥
నియతం కురు కర్మ త్వం కర్మ జ్యాయో హ్యకర్మణః ।
శరీర-యాత్రాపి చ తే న ప్రసిద్ధ్యేదకర్మణః ॥ 8 ॥
niyataṁ kuru karma tvaṁ karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ |
śarīra-yātrāpi cha te na prasiddhyed akarmaṇaḥ || 8 ||
Words: niyatam (constantly), kuru (perform), karma (Vedic duties), tvam (you), karma (action), jyāyaḥ (superior), hi (certainly), akarmaṇaḥ (than inaction), śarīra (bodily), yātrā (maintenance), api (even), cha (and), te (your), na prasiddhyet (would not be possible), akarmaṇaḥ (inaction)
Meaning: You should perform your obligatory duties, for action is superior to inaction. Moreover, even the maintenance of your body will not be possible without action.
Verse 9
यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।
तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसंगः समाचर ॥ ९॥
యజ్ఞార్థాత్-కర్మణోఽన్యత్ర లోకోఽయం కర్మ-బంధనః ।
తదర్థం కర్మ కౌంతేయ ముక్త-సంగః సమాచర ॥ 9 ॥
yajñārthāt karmaṇo ’nyatra loko ’yaṁ karma-bandhanaḥ |
tad-arthaṁ karma kaunteya mukta-saṅgaḥ samācara || 9 ||
Words: yajña-arthāt (for the sake of sacrifice), karmaṇaḥ (than action), anyatra (else), lokaḥ (material world), ayam (this), karma-bandhanaḥ (bondage through one’s work), tat (that), artham (for the sake of), karma (action), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), mukta-saṅgaḥ (free from attachment), samāchara (perform properly)
Meaning: This man becomes bound by actions other than those intended for God. Without being attached, O son of Kunti, perform actions for Him.
Verse 10
सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः ।
अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक् ॥ १०॥
సహ-యజ్ఞాః ప్రజాః సృష్ట్వా పురోవాచ ప్రజాపతిః ।
అనేన ప్రసవిష్యధ్వమేష వోఽస్త్విష్ట-కామ-ధుక్ ॥ 10 ॥
saha-yajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā purovāca prajāpatiḥ |
anena prasaviṣyadhvam eṣa vo ’stviṣṭa-kāma-dhuk || 10 ||
Words: saha (along with), yajñāḥ (sacrifices), prajāḥ (humankind), sṛṣṭvā (created), purā (in beginning), uvāca (said), prajā-patiḥ (Brahma), anena (by this), prasaviṣyadhvam (increase prosperity), eṣaḥ (these), vaḥ (your), astu (shall be), iṣṭa-kāma-dhuk (bestower of all wishes)
Meaning: In days of yore, having created the beings together with the sacrifices, Prajapati said, "By this, you shall multiply. Let this be your yielder of coveted objects of desire."
Verse 11
देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः ।
परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ ॥ ११॥
దేవాన్-భావయతానేన తే దేవా భావయంతు వః ।
పరస్పరం భావయంతః శ్రేయః పరమవాప్స్యథ ॥ 11 ॥
devān bhāvayatānena te devā bhāvayantu vaḥ |
parasparaṁ bhāvayantaḥ śreyaḥ param avāpsyatha || 11 ||
Words: devān (celestial gods), bhāvayatā (will be pleased), anena (by these (sacrifices)), te (those), devāḥ (celestial gods), bhāvayantu (will be pleased), vaḥ (you), parasparam (one another), bhāvayantaḥ (pleasing one another), śreyaḥ (prosperity), param (the supreme), avāpsyatha (shall achieve)
Meaning: 'You nourish the gods with this. Let those gods nourish you in return. By mutually nourishing each other, you shall attain the supreme Good.'
Verse 12
इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः ॥ १२॥
ఇష్టాన్-భోగాన్-హి వో దేవా దాస్యంతే యజ్ఞ-భావితాః ।
తైర్-దత్తానప్రదాయైభ్యో యో భుంక్తే స్తేన ఏవ సః ॥ 12 ॥
iṣṭān bhogān hi vo devā dāsyante yajña-bhāvitāḥ |
tair dattān apradāyaibhyo yo bhuṅkte stena eva saḥ || 12 ||
Words: iṣṭān (desired), bhogān (necessities of life), hi (certainly), vaḥ (unto you), devāḥ (the celestial gods), dāsyante (will grant), yajña-bhāvitāḥ (satisfied by sacrifice), taiḥ (by them), dattān (things granted), apradāya (without offering), ebhyaḥ (to them), yaḥ (who), bhuṅkte (enjoys), stenaḥ (thieves), eva (verily), saḥ (they)
Meaning: 'Being nourished by sacrifices, the gods will indeed give you the coveted enjoyments. He is certainly a thief who enjoys what has been given by them without offering it to them.'
Verse 13
यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषैः ।
भुञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात् ॥ १३॥
యజ్ఞ-శిష్టాశినః సంతో ముచ్యంతే సర్వ-కిల్బిషైః ।
భుంజతే తే త్వఘం పాపా యే పచంత్యాత్మ-కారణాత్ ॥ 13 ॥
yajña-śiṣṭāśinaḥ santo muchyante sarva-kilbiṣaiḥ |
bhuñjate te tvaghaṁ pāpā ye pachantyātma-kāraṇāt || 13 ||
Words: yajña-śiṣṭa (of remnants of food offered in sacrifice), aśinaḥ (eaters), santaḥ (saintly persons), muchyante (are released), sarva (all kinds of), kilbiṣaiḥ (from sins), bhuñjate (enjoy), te (they), tu (but), agham (sins), pāpāḥ (sinners), ye (who), pachanti (cook (food)), ātma-kāraṇāt (for their own sake)
Meaning: By partaking in the remnants of sacrifices, they become freed from all sins. But those who are unholy and cook for themselves incur sin.
Verse 14
अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः ।
यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः ॥ १४॥
అన్నాద్-భవంతి భూతాని పర్జన్యాదన్న-సంభవః ।
యజ్ఞాద్-భవతి పర్జన్యో యజ్ఞః కర్మ-సముద్భవః ॥ 14 ॥

yajñād bhavati parjanyo yajñaḥ karma-samudbhavaḥ || 14 ||
Words: annāt (from food), bhavanti (subsist), bhūtāni (living beings), parjanyāt (from rains), anna (of food grains), sambhavaḥ (production), yajñāt (from the performance of sacrifice), bhavati (becomes possible), parjanyaḥ (rain), yajñaḥ (performance of sacrifice), karma (prescribed duties), samudbhavaḥ (born of)
Meaning: From food are born the creatures; the origin of food is from rain; rain originates from sacrifice; sacrifice has action as its source.
Verse 15
कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् ।
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम् ॥ १५॥
కర్మ బ్రహ్మోద్భవం విద్ధి బ్రహ్మాక్షర-సముద్భవమ్ ।
తస్మాత్-సర్వ-గతం బ్రహ్మ నిత్యం యజ్ఞే ప్రతిష్ఠితమ్ ॥ 15 ॥

tasmāt sarva-gataṁ brahma nityaṁ yajñe pratiṣṭhitam || 15 ||
Words: karma (duties), brahma (in the Vedas), udbhavam (manifested), viddhi (you should know), brahma (The Vedas), akṣara (from the Imperishable (God)), samudbhavam (directly manifested), tasmāt (therefore), sarva-gatam (all-pervading), brahma (The Lord), nityam (eternally), yajñe (in sacrifice), pratiṣṭhitam (established)
Meaning: Know that action has the Vedas as its origin; the Vedas have the Immutable as their source. Hence, the all-pervading Vedas are forever based on sacrifice.
Verse 16
एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।
अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थ स जीवति ॥ १६॥

అఘాయురింద్రియారామో మోఘం పార్థ స జీవతి ॥ 16 ॥

Words: evam (thus), pravartitam (set into motion), chakram (cycle), na (not), anuvartayati (follow), iha (in this life), yaḥ (who), agha-āyuḥ (sinful living), indriya-ārāmaḥ (for the delight of their senses), mogham (vainly), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), saḥ (they), jīvati (live)
Meaning: O Partha, he who does not follow the wheel thus set in motion lives in vain, their life is sinful, and they indulge in the senses.
Verse 17
यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च मानवः ।
आत्मन्येव च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते ॥ १७॥
యస్-త్వాత్మ-రతిరేవ స్యాదాత్మ-తృప్తశ్-చ మానవః ।
ఆత్మన్యేవ చ సంతుష్టస్-తస్య కార్యం న విద్యతే ॥ 17 ॥

Verse 35
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥ ३५॥
శ్రేయాన్స్వ-ధర్మో విగుణః పర-ధర్మాత్స్వనుష్ఠితాత్ ।
స్వధర్మే నిధనం శ్రేయః పర-ధర్మో భయావహః ॥ 35 ॥
śreyān swa-dharmo viguṇaḥ para-dharmāt sv-anuṣṭhitāt |
swa-dharme nidhanaṁ śreyaḥ para-dharmo bhayāvahaḥ || 35 ||
Words: śreyān (better), swa-dharmaḥ (personal duty), viguṇaḥ (tinged with faults), para-dharmāt (than another’s prescribed duties), su-anuṣṭhitāt (perfectly done), swa-dharme (in one’s personal duties), nidhanam (death), śreyaḥ (better), para-dharmaḥ (duties prescribed for others), bhaya-āvahaḥ (fraught with fear)
Meaning: One's own duty, though defective, is superior to another's duty well-performed. Death is better while engaged in one's own duty; another's duty is full of fear.
Verse 36
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः ।
अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः ॥ ३६॥
అథ కేన ప్రయుక్తోఽయం పాపం చరతి పూరుషః ।
అనిచ్ఛన్నపి వార్ష్ణేయ బలాదివ నియోజితః ॥ 36 ॥
atha kena prayukto ’yaṁ pāpaṁ charati pūruṣaḥ |
anichchhann api vārṣṇeya balād iva niyojitaḥ || 36 ||
Words: atha (then), kena (by what), prayuktaḥ (impelled), ayam (one), pāpam (sins), charati (commit), pūruṣaḥ (a person), anichchhan (unwillingly), api (even), vārṣṇeya (he who belongs to the Vrishni clan, Shree Krishna), balāt (by force), iva (as if), niyojitaḥ (engaged)
Meaning: Now then, O scion of the Vrsni dynasty (Krsna), what compels this man to commit sin even against his will, as if he were constrained by force?"
Verse 37
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।
महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम् ॥ ३७॥
కామ ఏష క్రోధ ఏష రజో-గుణ-సముద్భవః ।
మహాశనో మహా-పాప్మా విద్ధ్యేనమిహ వైరిణమ్ ॥ 37 ॥
kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajo-guṇa-samudbhavaḥ |
mahāśano mahā-pāpmā viddhyenam iha vairiṇam || 37 ||
Words: śri-bhagavān uvāca (the Supreme Lord said), kāmaḥ (desire), eṣaḥ (this), krodhaḥ (wrath), eṣaḥ (this), rajaḥ-guṇa (the mode of passion), samudbhavaḥ (born of), mahā-aśanaḥ (all-devouring), mahā-pāpmā (greatly sinful), viddhi (know), enam (this), iha (in the material world), vairiṇam (the enemy)
Meaning: This desire, this anger, born of the quality of rajas, is a great devourer, a great source of sin. Know this to be the enemy here."
Verse 38
धूमेनाव्रियते वह्निर्यथाऽऽदर्शो मलेन च ।
यथोल्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तथा तेनेदमावृतम् ॥ ३८॥
ధూమేనావ్రియతే వహ్నిర్-యథాఽఽదర్శో మలేన చ ।
యథోల్బేనావృతో గర్భస్-తథా తేనేదమావృతమ్ ॥ 38 ॥
dhūmenāvriyate vahnir yathādarśo malena cha |
yatholbenāvṛto garbhas tathā tenedam āvṛtam || 38 ||
Words: dhūmena (by smoke), āvriyate (is covered), vahniḥ (fire), yathā (just as), ādarśaḥ (mirror), malena (by dust), cha (also), yathā (just as), ulbena (by the womb), āvṛtaḥ (is covered), garbhaḥ (embryo), tathā (similarly), tena (by that (desire)), idam (this), āvṛtam (is covered)
Meaning: As fire is enveloped by smoke, as a mirror is obscured by dirt, and as a fetus remains enclosed in the womb, so is this shrouded by that.
Verse 39
आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।
कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च ॥ ३९॥
ఆవృతం జ్ఞానమేతేన జ్ఞానినో నిత్య-వైరిణా ।
కామ-రూపేణ కౌంతేయ దుష్పూరేణానలేన చ ॥ 39 ॥
āvṛtaṁ jñānam etena jñānino nitya-vairiṇā |
kāma-rūpeṇa kaunteya duṣpūreṇānalena cha || 39 ||
Words: āvṛtam (covered), jñānam (knowledge), etena (by this), jñāninaḥ (of the wise), nitya-vairiṇā (by the perpetual enemy), kāma-rūpeṇa (in the form of desires), kaunteya (Arjun the son of Kunti), duṣpūreṇa (insatiable), analena (like fire), cha (and)
Meaning: O son of Kunti, knowledge is covered by this constant enemy of the wise, in the form of desire, which is an insatiable fire.
Verse 40
इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।
एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम् ॥ ४०॥
ఇంద్రియాణి మనో బుద్ధిరస్యాధిష్ఠానముచ్యతే ।
ఏతైర్-విమోహయత్యేష జ్ఞానమావృత్య దేహినమ్ ॥ 40 ॥
indriyāṇi mano buddhir asyādhiṣṭhānam uchyate |
etair vimohayatyeṣa jñānam āvṛtya dehinam || 40 ||
Words: indriyāṇi (the senses), manaḥ (the mind), buddhiḥ (the intellect), asya (of this), adhiṣṭhānam (dwelling place), uchyate (are said to be), etaiḥ (by these), vimohayati (deludes), eṣaḥ (this), jñānam (knowledge), āvṛtya (clouds), dehinam (the embodied soul)
Meaning: The organs, mind, and intellect are said to be its abode. This one diversely deludes the embodied being, veiling Knowledge with the help of these.
Verse 41
तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।
पाप्मानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम् ॥ ४१॥
తస్మాత్-త్వమింద్రియాణ్యాదౌ నియమ్య భరతర్షభ ।
పాప్మానం ప్రజహి హ్యేనం జ్ఞాన-విజ్ఞాన-నాశనమ్ ॥ 41 ॥
tasmāt tvam indriyāṇyādau niyamya bharatarṣabha |
pāpmānaṁ prajahi hyenaṁ jñāna-vijñāna-nāśanam || 41 ||
Words: tasmāt (therefore), tvam (you), indriyāṇi (senses), ādau (in the very beginning), niyamya (having controlled), bharata-ṛṣabha (Arjun, the best of the Bharatas), pāpmānam (the sinful), prajahi (slay), hi (certainly), enam (this), jñāna (knowledge), vijñāna (realization), nāśanam (the destroyer)
Meaning: Therefore, O scion of the Bharata dynasty, after first controlling the organs, completely renounce this one which is sinful and a destroyer of learning and wisdom.
Verse 42
इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः ॥ ४२॥
ఇంద్రియాణి పరాణ్యాహురింద్రియేభ్యః పరం మనః ।
మనసస్-తు పరా బుద్ధిర్-యో బుద్ధేః పరతస్-తు సః ॥ 42 ॥
indriyāṇi parāṇyāhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ |
manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ || 42 ||
Words: indriyāṇi (senses), parāṇi (superior), āhuḥ (are said), indriyebhyaḥ (than the senses), param (superior), manaḥ (the mind), manasaḥ (than the mind), tu (but), parā (superior), buddhiḥ (intellect), yaḥ (who), buddheḥ (than the intellect), parataḥ (more superior), tu (but), saḥ (that (soul))
Meaning: They say that the organs are superior to the gross body; the mind is superior to the organs; and the intellect is superior to the mind. However, He is superior to the intellect.
Verse 43
एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।
जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम् ॥ ४३॥
ఏవం బుద్ధేః పరం బుద్ధ్వా సంస్తభ్యాత్మానమాత్మనా ।
జహి శత్రుం మహా-బాహో కామ-రూపం దురాసదమ్ ॥ 43 ॥
evaṁ buddheḥ paraṁ buddhvā sanstabhyātmānam ātmanā |
jahi śatruṁ mahā-bāho kāma-rūpaṁ durāsadam || 43 ||
Words: evam (thus), buddheḥ (than the intellect), param (superior), buddhvā (knowing), sanstabhya (subdue), ātmānam (the lower self (senses, mind, and intellect)), ātmanā (by higher self (soul)), jahi (kill), śatrum (the enemy), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed one), kāma-rūpam (in the form of desire), durāsadam (formidable)
Meaning: Understanding the Self thus as superior to the intellect, and completely establishing (the Self) through spiritual absorption with the (help of) the mind, O mighty-armed one, the enemy in the form of desire, which is difficult to subdue, vanishes.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे कर्मयोगो नाम तृतीयोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the third chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Karma Yoga — the Yoga of Action.
