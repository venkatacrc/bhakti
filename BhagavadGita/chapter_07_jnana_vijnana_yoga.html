Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 7

अध्यायः ७ · ज्ञानविज्ञानयोगः — The Yoga of Knowledge and Realization

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 30 verses.

Krishna reveals His lower and higher natures — the material elements and the living soul — and how He pervades all as the taste in water, light of sun and moon, and life in all beings. Among devotees, the wise who know Him as all are dearest; few truly know Him in full.

The Thirty Verses

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थ योगं युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः ।
असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु ॥ १॥
మయ్యాసక్తమనాః పార్థ యోగం యుఞ్జన్మదాశ్రయః ।
అసంశయం సమగ్రం మాం యథా జ్ఞాస్యసి తచ్ఛృణు ॥ ౧॥
mayyāsakta-manāḥ pārtha yogaṁ yuñjan mad-āśrayaḥ |
asanśayaṁ samagraṁ māṁ yathā jñāsyasi tach chhṛṇu || 1 ||
Words: mayi (to me), āsakta-manāḥ (with the mind attached), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), yogam (bhakti yog), yuñjan (practicing), mat-āśrayaḥ (surrendering to me), asanśayam (free from doubt), samagram (completely), mām (me), yathā (how), jñāsyasi (you shall know), tat (that), śṛṇu (listen)
Meaning: O Partha, hear how, with your mind fixed on Me, practicing the Yoga of Meditation and taking refuge in Me, you will certainly and fully know Me.
Verse 2

యజ్జ్ఞాత్వా నేహ భూయోఽన్యజ్జ్ఞాతవ్యమవశిష్యతే ॥ ౨॥

yaj jñātvā neha bhūyo ’nyaj jñātavyam-avaśiṣyate || 2 ||
Words: jñānam (knowledge), te (unto you), aham (I), sa (with), vijñānam (wisdom), idam (this), vakṣyāmi (shall reveal), aśeṣataḥ (in full), yat (which), jñātvā (having known), na (not), iha (in this world), bhūyaḥ (further), anyat (anything else), jñātavyam (to be known), avaśiṣyate (remains)
Meaning: I shall tell you in detail of this Knowledge, which is combined with realization, after experiencing which there remains nothing else here to be known.
Verse 3

Meaning: Among thousands of men, a rare one endeavors for perfection. Even among the perfected ones who are diligent, one perchance knows Me in truth.
Verse 4

Meaning: O mighty-armed one, this is the inferior Prakrti. Know the other Prakrti of Mine, which is however higher than this, which has taken the form of individual souls, and by which this world is upheld.
Verse 6

Words: etat yonīni (these two (energies) are the source of), bhūtāni (living beings), sarvāṇi (all), iti (that), upadhāraya (know), aham (I), kṛtsnasya (entire), jagataḥ (creation), prabhavaḥ (the source), pralayaḥ (dissolution), tathā (and)
Meaning: Understand thus that all things—sentient and insentient—have these as their source. I am the origin as well as the end of the entire universe.
Verse 7

Words: mattaḥ (than me), para-taram (superior), na (not), anyat kiñchit (anything else), asti (there is), dhanañjaya (Arjun, conqueror of wealth), mayi (in me), sarvam (all), idam (which we see), protam (is strung), sūtre (on a thread), maṇi-gaṇāḥ (beads), iva (like)
Meaning: O Dhananjaya, there is nothing else higher than Myself. All this is strung on Me like pearls on a string.

Meaning: O son of Kunti, I am the taste of water, I am the effulgence of the moon and the sun; the letter 'Om' in all the Vedas, the sound in space, and manhood in men.

Meaning: I am also the sweet fragrance in the earth; I am the brilliance in the fire, and the life in all beings; and I am the austerity of the ascetics.
Verse 10

Meaning: O Partha, know Me to be the eternal seed of all beings. I am the intellect of the intelligent, and I am the courage of the courageous.

Meaning: And of the strong, I am the strength that is devoid of passion and attachment. Among creatures, I am desire that is not contrary to righteousness, O scion of the Bharata dynasty.

Meaning: Those things that are made of the quality of sattva, and those things that are made of the quality of rajas and tamas, know them to have sprung from Me alone. However, I am not in them; they are within Me!

Meaning: All this world, deluded as it is by these three qualities made of the gunas, does not know Me, who am transcendental to these and undecaying.
Verse 14

Meaning: Since this divine Maya of Mine, which is constituted by the gunas, is difficult to cross over, those who take refuge in Me alone can transcend this Maya.
Verse 15

Meaning: The foolish evildoers, who are the most depraved among men, who are deprived of their wisdom by Maya, and who resort to demonic ways, do not take refuge in Me.
Verse 16

Meaning: O Arjuna, foremost of the Bharata dynasty, four classes of people of virtuous deeds adore Me: the afflicted, the seekers of Knowledge, the seekers of wealth, and the man of Knowledge.

teṣāṁ jñānī nitya-yukta eka-bhaktir viśiṣyate |
priyo hi jñānino ’tyartham ahaṁ sa cha mama priyaḥ || 17 ||
Words: teṣām (amongst these), jñānī (those who are situated in knowledge), nitya-yuktaḥ (ever steadfast), eka (exclusively), bhaktiḥ (devotion), viśiṣyate (highest), priyaḥ (very dear), hi (certainly), jñāninaḥ (to the person in knowledge), atyartham (highly), aham (I), saḥ (he), cha (and), mama (to me), priyaḥ (dear)
Meaning: Of them, the man of knowledge, endowed with constant steadfastness and one-pointed devotion, excels. For I am very dear to the man of knowledge, and he too is dear to me.
Verse 18
उदाराः सर्व एवैते ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव मे मतम् ।
आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम् ॥ १८॥
ఉదారాః సర్వ ఏవైతే జ్ఞానీ త్వాత్మైవ మే మతమ్ ।
ఆస్థితః స హి యుక్తాత్మా మామేవానుత్తమాం గతిమ్ ॥ ౧౮॥
udārāḥ sarva evaite jñānī tvātmaiva me matam |
āsthitaḥ sa hi yuktātmā mām evānuttamāṁ gatim || 18 ||
Words: udārāḥ (noble), sarve (all), eva (indeed), ete (these), jñānī (those in knowledge), tu (but), ātmā eva (my very self), me (my), matam (opinion), āsthitaḥ (situated), saḥ (he), hi (certainly), yukta-ātmā (those who are united), mām (in me), eva (certainly), anuttamām (the supreme), gatim (goal)
Meaning: Indeed, all of these are noble, but the man of Knowledge is the Self itself. This is my opinion. For, with a steadfast mind, he is set on the path leading only to Me, who am the supreme Goal.
Verse 19
बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते ।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः ॥ १९॥
బహూనాం జన్మనామన్తే జ్ఞానవాన్మాం ప్రపద్యతే ।
వాసుదేవః సర్వమితి స మహాత్మా సుదుర్లభః ॥ ౧౯॥
bahūnāṁ janmanām ante jñānavān māṁ prapadyate |
vāsudevaḥ sarvam iti sa mahātmā su-durlabhaḥ || 19 ||
Words: bahūnām (many), janmanām (births), ante (after), jñāna-vān (one who is endowed with knowledge), mām (unto me), prapadyate (surrenders), vāsudevaḥ (Shree Krishna, the son of Vasudev), sarvam (all), iti (that), saḥ (that), mahā-ātmā (great soul), su-durlabhaḥ (very rare)
Meaning: At the end of many births, the man of knowledge attains Me, realizing that Vasudeva is all. Such a high-souled one is very rare.
Verse 20
कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः ।
तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया ॥ २०॥
కామైస్తైస్తైర్హృతజ్ఞానాః ప్రపద్యన్తేఽన్యదేవతాః ।
తం తం నియమమాస్థాయ ప్రకృత్యా నియతాః స్వయా ॥ ౨౦॥
kāmais tais tair hṛta-jñānāḥ prapadyante ’nya-devatāḥ |
taṁ taṁ niyamam āsthāya prakṛtyā niyatāḥ svayā || 20 ||
Words: kāmaiḥ (by material desires), taiḥ taiḥ (by various), hṛta-jñānāḥ (whose knowledge has been carried away), prapadyante (surrender), anya (to other), devatāḥ (celestial gods), tam tam (the various), niyamam (rules and regulations), āsthāya (following), prakṛtyā (by nature), niyatāḥ (controlled), svayā (by their own)
Meaning: People, deprived of their wisdom by desires for various objects and guided by their own nature, resort to other deities, following the relevant methods.
Verse 21
यो यो यां यां तनुं भक्तः श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति ।
तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विदधाम्यहम् ॥ २१॥
యో యో యాం యాం తనుం భక్తః శ్రద్ధయార్చితుమిచ్ఛతి ।
తస్య తస్యాచలాం శ్రద్ధాం తామేవ విదధామ్యహమ్ ॥ ౨౧॥
yo yo yāṁ yāṁ tanuṁ bhaktaḥ śraddhayārchitum ichchhati |
tasya tasyācalāṁ śraddhāṁ tām eva vidadhāmyaham || 21 ||
Words: yaḥ yaḥ (whoever), yām yām (whichever), tanum (form), bhaktaḥ (devotee), śraddhayā (with faith), architum (to worship), ichchhati (desires), tasya tasya (to him), achalām (steady), śraddhām (faith), tām (in that), eva (certainly), vidadhāmi (bestow), aham (I)
Meaning: Whichever form of a deity any devotee wants to worship with faith, I strengthen that very firm faith of theirs.
Verse 22
स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्तस्याराधनमीहते ।
लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान् हि तान् ॥ २२॥
స తయా శ్రద్ధయా యుక్తస్తస్యారాధనమీహతే ।
లభతే చ తతః కామాన్మయైవ విహితాన్ హి తాన్ ॥ ౨౨॥
sa tayā śraddhayā yuktas tasyārādhanam īhate |
labhate cha tataḥ kāmān mayaiva vihitān hi tān || 22 ||
Words: saḥ (he), tayā (with that), śraddhayā (faith), yuktaḥ (endowed with), tasya (of that), ārādhanam (worship), īhate (tries to engange in), labhate (obtains), cha (and), tataḥ (from that), kāmān (desires), mayā (by me), eva (alone), vihitān (granted), hi (certainly), tān (those)
Meaning: Being imbued with that faith, that person engages in worshipping that form, and they get those very desired results as dispensed by Me alone.
Verse 23
अन्तवत्तु फलं तेषां तद्भवत्यल्पमेधसाम् ।
देवान्देवयजो यान्ति मद्भक्ता यान्ति मामपि ॥ २३॥
అన్తవత్తు ఫలం తేషాం తద్భవత్యల్పమేధసామ్ ।
దేవాన్దేవయజో యాన్తి మద్భక్తా యాన్తి మామపి ॥ ౨౩॥
antavat tu phalaṁ teṣāṁ tad bhavatyalpa-medhasām |
devān deva-yajo yānti mad-bhaktā yānti mām api || 23 ||
Words: anta-vat (perishable), tu (but), phalam (fruit), teṣām (by them), tat (that), bhavati (is), alpa-medhasām (people of small understanding), devān (to the celestial gods), deva-yajaḥ (the worshipers of the celestial gods), yānti (go), mat (my), bhaktāḥ (devotees), yānti (go), mām (to me), api (whereas)
Meaning: The result of those who are of poor intellect is indeed limited. The worshippers of gods go to the gods, while My devotees go to Me alone.
Verse 24
अव्यक्तं व्यक्ितमापन्नं मन्यन्ते मामबुद्धयः ।
परं भावमजानन्तो ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम् ॥ २४॥
అవ్యక్తం వ్యక్ితమాపన్నం మన్యన్తే మామబుద్ధయః ।
పరం భావమజానన్తో మమావ్యయమనుత్తమమ్ ॥ ౨౪॥
avyaktaṁ vyaktim āpannaṁ manyante mām abuddhayaḥ |
paraṁ bhāvam ajānanto mamāvyayam anuttamam || 24 ||
Words: avyaktam (formless), vyaktim (possessing a personality), āpannam (to have assumed), manyante (think), mām (me), abuddhayaḥ (less intelligent), param (Supreme), bhāvam (nature), ajānantaḥ (not understanding), mama (my), avyayam (imperishable), anuttamam (excellent)
Meaning: The unintelligent, unaware of My supreme state which is immutable and unsurpassable, think of Me as having become manifest from the unmanifest.
Verse 25
नाहं प्रकाशः सर्वस्य योगमायासमावृतः ।
मूढोऽयं नाभिजानाति लोको मामजमव्ययम् ॥ २५॥
నాహం ప్రకాశః సర్వస్య యోగమాయాసమావృతః ।
మూఢోఽయం నాభిజానాతి లోకో మామజమవ్యయమ్ ॥ ౨౫॥
nāhaṁ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yoga-māyā-samāvṛtaḥ |
mūḍho ’yaṁ nābhijānāti loko mām ajam avyayam || 25 ||
Words: na (not), aham (I), prakāśaḥ (manifest), sarvasya (to everyone), yoga-māyā (God’s supreme (divine) energy), samāvṛtaḥ (veiled), mūḍhaḥ (deluded), ayam (these), na (not), abhijānāti (know), lokaḥ (persons), mām (me), ajam (unborn), avyayam (immutable)
Meaning: Being enveloped by yoga-maya, I do not become manifest to all. This deluded world does not know Me, who am birthless and undecaying.
Verse 26
वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन ।
भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन ॥ २६॥
వేదాహం సమతీతాని వర్తమానాని చార్జున ।
భవిష్యాణి చ భూతాని మాం తు వేద న కశ్చన ॥ ౨౬॥
vedāhaṁ samatītāni vartamānāni chārjuna |
bhaviṣyāṇi cha bhūtāni māṁ tu veda na kaśchana || 26 ||
Words: veda (know), aham (I), samatītāni (the past), vartamānāni (the present), cha (and), arjuna (Arjun), bhaviṣyāṇi (the future), cha (also), bhūtāni (all living beings), mām (me), tu (but), veda (knows), na kaśchana (no one)
Meaning: O Arjuna, I know the past, present, and future beings; but no one knows Me!
Verse 27
इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वन्द्वमोहेन भारत ।
सर्वभूतानि संमोहं सर्गे यान्ति परन्तप ॥ २७॥
ఇచ్ఛాద్వేషసముత్థేన ద్వన్ద్వమోహేన భారత ।
సర్వభూతాని సంమోహం సర్గే యాన్తి పరన్తప ॥ ౨౭॥
ichchhā-dveṣa-samutthena dvandva-mohena bhārata |
sarva-bhūtāni sammohaṁ sarge yānti parantapa || 27 ||
Words: ichchhā (desire), dveṣa (aversion), samutthena (arise from), dvandva (of duality), mohena (from the illusion), bhārata (Arjun, descendant of Bharat), sarva (all), bhūtāni (living beings), sammoham (into delusion), sarge (since birth), yānti (enter), parantapa (Arjun, conqueror of enemies)
Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, O destroyer of foes, due to the delusion of duality arising from likes and dislikes, all creatures become bewildered upon their birth.
Verse 28
येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम् ।
ते द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता भजन्ते मां दृढव्रताः ॥ २८॥
యేషాం త్వన్తగతం పాపం జనానాం పుణ్యకర్మణామ్ ।
తే ద్వన్ద్వమోహనిర్ముక్తా భజన్తే మాం దృఢవ్రతాః ॥ ౨౮॥
yeṣāṁ tvanta-gataṁ pāpaṁ janānāṁ puṇya-karmaṇām |
te dvandva-moha-nirmuktā bhajante māṁ dṛḍha-vratāḥ || 28 ||
Words: yeṣām (whose), tu (but), anta-gatam (completely destroyed), pāpam (sins), janānām (of persons), puṇya (pious), karmaṇām (activities), te (they), dvandva (of dualities), moha (illusion), nirmuktāḥ (free from), bhajante (worship), mām, dṛḍha-vratāḥ (with determination)
Meaning: On the other hand, those persons who have done virtuous deeds, whose sin has come to an end, they, being free from the delusion of duality and firm in their convictions, adore Me.
Verse 29
जरामरणमोक्षाय मामाश्रित्य यतन्ति ये ।
ते ब्रह्म तद्विदुः कृत्स्नमध्यात्मं कर्म चाखिलम् ॥ २९॥
జరామరణమోక్షాయ మామ్ ఆశ్రిత్య యతన్తి యే ।
తే బ్రహ్మ తద్విదుః కృత్స్నమధ్యాత్మం కర్మ చాఖిలమ్ ॥ ౨౯॥
jarā-maraṇa-mokṣāya mām āśritya yatanti ye |
te brahma tadviduḥ kṛtsnam adhyātmaṁ karma chākhilam || 29 ||
Words: jarā (from old age), maraṇa (and death), mokṣāya (for liberation), mām (me), āśritya (take shelter in), yatanti (strive), ye (who), te (they), brahma (Brahman), tat (that), viduḥ (know), kṛtsnam (everything), adhyātmam (the individual self), karma (karmic action), cha (and), akhilam (entire)
Meaning: Those who strive by resorting to Me for becoming free from old age and death, they know Brahman, the individual Self, and all about actions.
Verse 30
साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः ।
प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः ॥ ३०॥
సాధిభూతాధిదైవం మాం సాధియజ్ఞం చ యే విదుః ।
ప్రయాణకాలేఽపి చ మాం తే విదుర్యుక్తచేతసః ॥ ౩౦॥
sādhibhūtādhidaivaṁ māṁ sādhiyajñaṁ cha ye viduḥ |
prayāṇa-kāle ’pi cha māṁ te vidur yukta-chetasaḥ || 30 ||
Words: sa-adhibhūta (governing principle of the field of matter), adhidaivam (governing principle of the celestial gods), mām (me), sa-adhiyajñam (governing principle of the Lord all sacrificial performances), cha (and), ye (who), viduḥ (know), prayāṇa (of death), kāle (at the time), api (even), cha (and), mām (me), te (they), viduḥ (know), yukta-chetasaḥ (in full consciousness of me)
Meaning: Those who know me as existing in both the physical and divine planes, and also in the context of the sacrifice, they with concentrated minds know me even at the time of death.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे ज्ञानविज्ञानयोगो नाम सप्तमोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the seventh chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Jñāna Vijñāna Yoga — the Yoga of Knowledge and Realization.
