Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 10

अध्यायः १० · विभूतियोगः — The Yoga of Divine Glories

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 42 verses.

Krishna declares Himself the origin of all and describes how devotees who know this are dear to Him. At Arjuna’s request, He recounts His vibhūtis — divine manifestations in the cosmos — culminating in the teaching that a single fraction of His being sustains the entire universe.

The Forty-Two Verses

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
भूय एव महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया ॥ १॥
భూయ ఏవ మహా-బాహో శృణు మే పరమం వచః ।
యత్తేఽహం ప్రీయమాణాయ వక్ష్యామి హిత-కామ్యయా ॥ 1 ॥
bhūya eva mahā-bāho śṛṇu me paramaṁ vacaḥ |
yatte ’haṁ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hita-kāmyayā || 1 ||
Words: bhūyaḥ (again), eva (verily), mahā-bāho (mighty armed one), śṛṇu (hear), me (my), paramam (divine), vachaḥ (teachings), yat (which), te (to you), aham (I), prīyamāṇāya (you are my beloved confidant), vakṣyāmi (say), hita-kāmyayā (for desiring your welfare)
Meaning: O mighty-armed one, listen once more to My supreme utterance, which I, wishing for your welfare, shall speak to you who take delight in it.
Verse 2

अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः ॥ २॥

అహమాదిర్-హి దేవానాం మహర్షీణాం చ సర్వశః ॥ 2 ॥
na me viduḥ sura-gaṇāḥ prabhavaṁ na maharṣayaḥ |
aham ādir hi devānāṁ maharṣīṇāṁ cha sarvaśaḥ || 2 ||
Words: na (neither), me (my), viduḥ (know), sura-gaṇāḥ (the celestial gods), prabhavam (origin), na (nor), mahā-ṛṣayaḥ (the great sages), aham (I), ādiḥ (the source), hi (certainly), devānām (of the celestial gods), mahā-ṛṣīṇām (of the great seers), cha (also), sarvaśaḥ (in every way)
Meaning: Neither the gods nor the great sages know My majesty, for I am the source of them all in all respects.

అసమ్మూఢః స మర్త్యేషు సర్వ-పాపైః ప్రముచ్యతే ॥ 3 ॥

Words: verseyaḥ (who), mām (me), ajam (unborn), anādim (beginningless), cha (and), vetti (know), loka (of the universe), mahā-īśvaram (the Supreme Lord), asammūḍhaḥ (undeluded), saḥ (they), martyeṣu (among mortals), sarva-pāpaiḥ (from all evils), pramuchyate (are freed from-3)
Meaning: He who knows Me—the birthless, beginningless, and great Lord of the worlds—he, the undeluded one among mortals, becomes freed from all sins.
Verse 4

Meaning: Intelligence, wisdom, non-delusion, forgiveness, truth, control of the external organs, control of the internal organs, happiness, sorrow, birth, death, and fearlessness;
Verse 5
अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः ।

భవంతి భావా భూతానాం మత్త ఏవ పృథగ్-విధాః ॥ 5 ॥
ahiṁsā samatā tuṣṭis tapo dānaṁ yaśo 'yaśaḥ |

Words: ahiṁsā (nonviolence), samatā (equilibrium), tuṣṭiḥ (satisfaction), tapaḥ (penance), dānam (charity), yaśaḥ (fame), ayaśaḥ (infamy), bhavanti (become), bhāvāḥ (natures), bhūtānām (of living entities), mattaḥ (from Me), eva (certainly), pṛthakvidhāḥ (differently arranged.)
Meaning: Non-injury, equanimity, satisfaction, austerity, charity, fame, and infamy—these different dispositions of beings spring from Me alone.
Verse 6
महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तथा ।
मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः ॥ ६॥

మద్-భావా మానసా జాతా యేషాం లోక ఇమాః ప్రజాః ॥ 6 ॥
maharṣayaḥ sapta pūrve chatvāro manavas tathā |
mad-bhāvā mānasā jātā yeṣāṁ loka imāḥ prajāḥ || 6 ||
Words: mahā-ṛṣayaḥ (the great Sages), sapta (seven), pūrve (before), chatvāraḥ (four), manavaḥ (Manus), tathā (also), mat bhāvāḥ (are born from me), mānasāḥ (mind), jātāḥ (born), yeṣām (from them), loke (in the world), imāḥ (all these), prajāḥ (people)
Meaning: The seven great sages, as well as the four Manus of ancient days, from whom all these creatures in the world have descended, had their thoughts fixed on Me, and they were born from My mind.
Verse 7
एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ७॥
ఏతాం విభూతిం యోగం చ మమ యో వేత్తి తత్త్వతః ।
సోఽవికంపేన యోగేన యుజ్యతే నాత్ర సంశయః ॥ 7 ॥
etāṁ vibhūtiṁ yogaṁ cha mama yo vetti tattvataḥ |
so ’vikampena yogena yujyate nātra sanśayaḥ || 7 ||
Words: etām (these), vibhūtim (glories), yogam (divine powers), cha (and), mama (my), yaḥ (those who), vetti (know), tattvataḥ (in truth), saḥ (they), avikalpena (unwavering), yogena (in bhakti yog), yujyate (becomes united), na (never), atra (here), sanśayaḥ (doubt)
Meaning: One who knows truly this majesty and yoga of Mine, he becomes imbued with unwavering yoga. There is no doubt about it.
Verse 8

ఇతి మత్వా భజంతే మాం బుధా భావ-సమన్వితాః ॥ 8 ॥
ahaṁ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṁ pravartate |

Words: aham (I), sarvasya (of all creation), prabhavaḥ (the origin of), mattaḥ (from me), sarvam (everything), pravartate (proceeds), iti (thus), matvā (having known), bhajante (worship), mām (me), budhāḥ (the wise), bhāva-samanvitāḥ (endowed with great faith and devotion)
Meaning: I am the origin of all; everything moves on due to Me. Realizing this, the wise ones, filled with fervor, adore Me.
Verse 9

Meaning: With their minds fixed on Me, dedicating their lives to Me, enlightening one another, and always speaking of Me, they derive satisfaction and rejoice.

Meaning: To those who are ever devoted and worship Me with love, I grant the possession of wisdom that leads them to Me.

Meaning: Out of compassion for them alone, I, residing in their hearts, destroy the darkness born of ignorance with the luminous lamp of knowledge.
Verse 12

mṛtyuḥ sarva-haraś chāham udbhavaś cha bhaviṣyatām |
kīrtiḥ śrīr vāk cha nārīṇāṁ smṛtir medhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā || 34 ||
Words: mṛtyuḥ (death), sarva-haraḥ (all-devouring), cha (and), aham (I), udbhavaḥ (the origin), cha (and), bhaviṣyatām (those things that are yet to be), kīrtiḥ (fame), śrīḥ (prospective), vāk (fine speech), cha (and), nārīṇām (amongst feminine qualities), smṛtiḥ (memory), medhā (intelligence), dhṛtiḥ (courage), kṣamā (forgiveness)
Meaning: And I am Death, the destroyer of all; and the bestower of prosperity to those destined to be prosperous. Of the feminine (I am) fame, beauty, speech, memory, intelligence, fortitude, and forbearance.
Verse 35
बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् ।
मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः ॥ ३५॥
బృహత్-సామ తథా సామ్నాం గాయత్రీ ఛందసామహమ్ ।
మాసానాం మార్గ-శీర్షోఽహమృతూనాం కుసుమాకరః ॥ 35 ॥
bṛhat-sāma tathā sāmnāṁ gāyatrī chhandasām aham |
māsānāṁ mārga-śīrṣo ’ham ṛtūnāṁ kusumākaraḥ || 35 ||
Words: bṛhat-sāma (the Brihatsama), tathā (also), sāmnām (amongst the hymns in the Sama Veda), gāyatrī (the Gayatri mantra), chhandasām (amongst poetic meters), aham (I), māsānām (of the twelve months), mārga-śīrṣaḥ (the month of November-December), aham (I), ṛtūnām (of all seasons), kusuma-ākaraḥ (spring)
Meaning: I am also the Brhat-sama of the Sama-mantras; of the meters, Gayatri. Of the months, I am Marga-sirsa, and of the seasons, spring.
Verse 36
द्यूतं छलयतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ।
जयोऽस्मि व्यवसायोऽस्मि सत्त्वं सत्त्ववतामहम् ॥ ३६॥
ద్యూతం ఛలయతామస్మి తేజస్-తేజస్వినామహమ్ ।
జయోఽస్మి వ్యవసాయోఽస్మి సత్త్వం సత్త్వవతామహమ్ ॥ 36 ॥
dyūtaṁ chhalayatām asmi tejas tejasvinām aham |
jayo ’smi vyavasāyo ’smi sattvaṁ sattvavatām aham || 36 ||
Words: dyūtam (gambling), chhalayatām (of all cheats), asmi (I am), tejaḥ (the splendor), tejasvinām (of the splendid), aham (I), jayaḥ (victory), asmi (I am), vyavasāyaḥ (firm resolve), asmi (I am), sattvam (virtue), sattva-vatām (of the virtuous), aham (I)
Meaning: Of the fraudulent, I am the gambling; I am the irresistible end of the mighty. I am excellence, I am effort, I am the sattva quality of those possessed of sattva.
Verse 37
वृष्णीनां वासुदेवोऽस्मि पाण्डवानां धनंजयः ।
मुनीनामप्यहं व्यासः कवीनामुशना कविः ॥ ३७॥
వృష్ణీనాం వాసుదేవోఽస్మి పాండవానాం ధనంజయః ।
మునీనామప్యహం వ్యాసః కవీనాముశనా కవిః ॥ 37 ॥
vṛṣṇīnāṁ vāsudevo ’smi pāṇḍavānāṁ dhanañjayaḥ |
munīnām apyahaṁ vyāsaḥ kavīnām uśanā kaviḥ || 37 ||
Words: vṛṣṇīnām (amongst the descendants of Vrishni), vāsudevaḥ (Krishna, the son of Vasudev), asmi (I am), pāṇḍavānām (amongst the Pandavas), dhanañjayaḥ (Arjun, the conqueror of wealth), munīnām (amongst the sages), api (also), aham (I), vyāsaḥ (Ved Vyas), kavīnām (amongst the great thinkers), uśanā (Shukracharya), kaviḥ (the thinker)
Meaning: Of the Vrsnis, I am Vasudeva; of the Pandavas, Dhananjaya (Arjuna). Of the wise, I am Vyasa; of the omniscient, the omniscient Usanas.
Verse 38
दण्डो दमयतामस्मि नीतिरस्मि जिगीषताम् ।
मौनं चैवास्मि गुह्यानां ज्ञानं ज्ञानवतामहम् ॥ ३८॥
దండో దమయతామస్మి నీతిరస్మి జిగీషతామ్ ।
మౌనం చైవాస్మి గుహ్యానాం జ్ఞానం జ్ఞానవతామహమ్ ॥ 38 ॥
daṇḍo damayatām asmi nītir asmi jigīṣatām |
maunaṁ chaivāsmi guhyānāṁ jñānaṁ jñānavatām aham || 38 ||
Words: daṇḍaḥ (punishment), damayatām (amongst means of preventing lawlessness), asmi (I am), nītiḥ (proper conduct), asmi (I am), jigīṣatām (amongst those who seek victory), maunam (silence), cha (and), eva (also), asmi (I am), guhyānām (amongst secrets), jñānam (wisdom), jñāna-vatām (in the wise), aham (I)
Meaning: Of the punishers, I am the rod; I am the righteous policy of those who desire to conquer. And of things secret, I am verily silence; I am the knowledge of the wise.
Verse 39
यच्चापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तदहमर्जुन ।
न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम् ॥ ३९॥
యచ్-చాపి సర్వ-భూతానాం బీజం తదహమర్జున ।
న తదస్తి వినా యత్-స్యాన్-మయా భూతం చరాచరమ్ ॥ 39 ॥
yach chāpi sarva-bhūtānāṁ bījaṁ tad aham arjuna |
na tad asti vinā yat syān mayā bhūtaṁ charācaram || 39 ||
Words: yat (which), cha (and), api (also), sarva-bhūtānām (of all living beings), bījam (generating seed), tat (that), aham (I), arjuna (Arjun), na (not), tat (that), asti (is), vinā (without), yat (which), syāt (may exist), mayā (me), bhūtam (creature), chara-acharam (moving and nonmoving)
Meaning: Moreover, O Arjuna, I am the seed of all beings. There is nothing, moving or non-moving, that can exist without Me.
Verse 40
नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परंतप ।
एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया ॥ ४०॥
నాంతోఽస్తి మమ దివ్యానాం విభూతీనాం పరంతప ।
ఏష తూద్దేశతః ప్రోక్తో విభూతేర్-విస్తరో మయా ॥ 40 ॥
nānto ’sti mama divyānāṁ vibhūtīnāṁ parantapa |
eṣa tūddeśataḥ prokto vibhūter vistaro mayā || 40 ||
Words: na (not), antaḥ (end), asti (is), mama (my), divyānām (divine), vibhūtīnām (manifestations), parantapa (Arjun, the conqueror of the enemies), eṣaḥ (this), tu (but), uddeśataḥ (just one portion), proktaḥ (declared), vibhūteḥ (of (my) glories), vistaraḥ (the breath of the topic), mayā (by me)
Meaning: O destroyer of enemies, there is no limit to My divine manifestations. This description of My manifestations, however, has been stated by Me as an illustration.
Verse 41
यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा ।
तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसंभवम् ॥ ४१॥
యద్-యద్-విభూతిమత్-సత్త్వం శ్రీమదూర్జితమేవ వా ।
తత్-తదేవావగచ్ఛ త్వం మమ తేజోంఽశ-సంభవమ్ ॥ 41 ॥
yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śrīmad ūrjitam eva vā |
tat tad evāvagachchha tvaṁ mama tejo ’nśa-sambhavam || 41 ||
Words: yat yat (whatever), vibhūtimat (opulent), sattvam (being), śrī-mat (beautiful), ūrjitam (glorious), eva (also), vā (or), tat tat (all that), eva (only), avagachchha (know), tvam (you), mama (my), tejaḥ-anśa-sambhavam (splendor), anśa (a part), sambhavam (born of)
Meaning: Whatever object is verily endowed with majesty, possessed of prosperity, or is energetic, you know for certain each of them as having a part of My power as its source.
Verse 42
अथवा बहुनैतेन किं ज्ञातेन तवार्जुन ।
विष्टभ्याहमिदं कृत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत् ॥ ४२॥
అథ-వా బహునైతేన కిం జ్ఞాతేన తవార్జున ।
విష్టభ్యాహమిదం కృత్స్నమేకాంశేన స్థితో జగత్ ॥ 42 ॥
atha vā bahunaitena kiṁ jñātena tavārjuna |
viṣṭabhyāham idaṁ kṛtsnam ekānśena sthito jagat || 42 ||
Words: athavā (or), bahunā (detailed), etena (by this), kim (what), jñātena tava (can be known by you), arjuna (Arjun), viṣṭabhya (pervade and support), aham (I), idam (this), kṛtsnam (entire), eka (by one), anśena (fraction), sthitaḥ (am situated), jagat (creation)
Meaning: Or, on the other hand, what is the need for you to know this extensively, O Arjuna? I remain sustaining this whole creation in a special way with a part of Myself.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे विभूतियोगो नाम दशमोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the tenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Vibhūti Yoga — the Yoga of Divine Glories.
