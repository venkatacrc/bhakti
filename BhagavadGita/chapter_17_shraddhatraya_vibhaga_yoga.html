Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 17

अध्यायः १७ · श्रद्धात्रयविभागयोगः — The Yoga of Distinguishing the Three Kinds of Faith

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 28 verses.

Arjuna asks about those who worship with faith but outside scriptural rule. Krishna explains faith, food, sacrifice, austerity, and charity as threefold — sāttvic, rājasic, and tāmasic — and teaches the sacred formula Om Tat Sat that consecrates acts of sacrifice, gift, and penance.

The Twenty-Eight Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयाऽन्विताः ।
तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः ॥ १॥
యే శాస్త్ర-విధిముత్సృజ్య యజంతే శ్రద్ధయాఽన్వితాః ।
తేషాం నిష్ఠా తు కా కృష్ణ సత్త్వమాహో రజస్-తమః ॥ 1 ॥
ye śāstra-vidhim utsṛjya yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ |
teṣāṁ niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvam āho rajas tamaḥ || 1 ||
Words: ye (who), śāstra-vidhim (scriptural injunctions), utsṛjya (disregard), yajante (worship), śraddhayā-anvitāḥ (with faith), teṣām (their), niṣṭhā (faith), tu (indeed), kā (what), kṛṣṇa (Krishna), sattvam (mode of goodness), āho (or), rajaḥ (mode of passion), tamaḥ (mode of ignorance)
Meaning: But, O Krsna, what is the state of those who, endued with faith, perform sacrifices, distribute wealth, etc. in honour of gods and others, while ignoring the injunctions of the scriptures? Is it sattva, rajas, or tamas?
Verse 2
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ।
सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु ॥ २॥
త్రి-విధా భవతి శ్రద్ధా దేహినాం సా స్వభావ-జా ।
సాత్త్వికీ రాజసీ చైవ తామసీ చేతి తాం శృణు ॥ 2 ॥
tri-vidhā bhavati śraddhā dehināṁ sā svabhāva-jā |
sāttvikī rājasī chaiva tāmasī cheti tāṁ śṛṇu || 2 ||
Words: tri-vidhā (of three kinds), bhavati (is), śraddhā (faith), dehinām (embodied beings), sā (which), sva-bhāva-jā (born of one’s innate nature), sāttvikī (of the mode of goodness), rājasī (of the mode of passion), cha (and), eva (certainly), tāmasī (of the mode of ignorance), cha (and), iti (thus), tām (about this), śṛṇu (hear)
Meaning: That faith of the embodied beings, born of their own nature, is threefold—born of sattva, rajas, and tamas. Hear about it."
Verse 3
सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥ ३॥
సత్త్వానురూపా సర్వస్య శ్రద్ధా భవతి భారత ।
శ్రద్ధా-మయోఽయం పురుషో యో యచ్-ఛ్రద్ధః స ఏవ సః ॥ 3 ॥
sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata |
śraddhā-mayo ‘yaṁ puruṣo yo yach-chhraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ || 3 ||
Words: sattva-anurūpā (conforming to the nature of one’s mind), sarvasya (all), śraddhā (faith), bhavati (is), bhārata (Arjun, the scion of Bharat), śraddhāmayaḥ (possessing faith), ayam (that), puruṣaḥ (human being), yaḥ (who), yat-śraddhaḥ (whatever the nature of their faith), saḥ (their), eva (verily), saḥ (they)
Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, the faith of all beings is in accordance with their minds. This person is composed of faith as the dominant factor. He is indeed what his faith is.
Verse 4
यजन्ते सात्त्विका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः ।
प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जनाः ॥ ४॥
యజంతే సాత్త్వికా దేవాన్-యక్ష-రక్షాంసి రాజసాః ।
ప్రేతాన్-భూత-గణాంశ్-చాన్యే యజంతే తామసా జనాః ॥ 4 ॥
yajante sāttvikā devān yakṣa-rakṣānsi rājasāḥ |
pretān bhūta-gaṇānś chānye yajante tāmasā janāḥ || 4 ||
Words: yajante (worship), sāttvikāḥ (those in the mode of goodness), devān (celestial gods), yakṣa (semi-celestial beings who exude power and wealth), rakṣānsi (powerful beings who embody sensual enjoyment, revenge, and wrath), rājasāḥ (those in the mode of passion), pretān-bhūta-gaṇān (ghosts and spirits), cha (and), anye (others), yajante (worship), tāmasāḥ (those in the mode of ignorance), janāḥ (persons)
Meaning: Those with sattva quality worship the gods; those with rajas, the demigods and demons; and those with tamas, ghosts and hosts of spirits.
Verse 5
अशास्त्रविहितं घोरं तप्यन्ते ये तपो जनाः ।
दम्भाहङ्कारसंयुक्ताः कामरागबलान्विताः ॥ ५॥
అశాస్త్ర-విహితం ఘోరం తప్యంతే యే తపో జనాః ।
దంభాహంకార-సంయుక్తాః కామ-రాగ-బలాన్వితాః ॥ 5 ॥
aśāstra-vihitaṁ ghoraṁ tapyante ye tapo janāḥ |
dambhāhankāra-sanyuktāḥ kāma-rāga-balānvitāḥ || 5 ||
Words: aśāstra-vihitam (not enjoined by the scriptures), ghoram (stern), tapyante (perform), ye (who), tapaḥ (austerities), janāḥ (people), dambha (hypocrisy), ahankāra (egotism), sanyuktāḥ (possessed of), kāma (desire), rāga (attachment), bala (force), anvitāḥ (impelled by)
Meaning: Those persons who, given to ostentation and pride, and possessed of passion, attachment, and strength, undertake severe austerities not sanctioned by the scriptures;
Verse 6
कर्षयन्तः शरीरस्थं भूतग्राममचेतसः ।
मां चैवान्तःशरीरस्थं तान्विद्ध्यासुरनिश्चयान् ॥ ६॥
కర్షయంతః శరీర-స్థం భూత-గ్రామమచేతసః ।
మాం చైవాంతః-శరీర-స్థం తాన్-విద్ధ్యాసుర-నిశ్చయాన్ ॥ 6 ॥
karṣayantaḥ śarīra-sthaṁ bhūta-grāmam achetasaḥ |
māṁ chaivāntaḥ śarīra-sthaṁ tān viddhy āsura-niśchayān || 6 ||
Words: karṣayantaḥ (torment), śarīra-stham (within the body), bhūta-grāmam (elements of the body), achetasaḥ (senseless), mām (me), cha (and), eva (even), antaḥ (within), śarīra-stham (dwelling in the body), tān (them), viddhi (know), āsura-niśchayān (of demoniacal resolves)
Meaning: (And those who,) being non-discriminating, torture all the organs in the body as well as even Me who reside in the body—know them to be possessed of demoniacal conviction.
Verse 7
आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः ।
यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं शृणु ॥ ७॥
ఆహారస్-త్వపి సర్వస్య త్రి-విధో భవతి ప్రియః ।
యజ్ఞస్-తపస్-తథా దానం తేషాం భేదమిమం శృణు ॥ 7 ॥
āhāras tv api sarvasya tri-vidho bhavati priyaḥ |
yajñas tapas tathā dānaṁ teṣāṁ bhedam imaṁ śṛṇu || 7 ||
Words: āhāraḥ (food), tu (indeed), api (even), sarvasya (of all), tri-vidhaḥ (of three kinds), bhavati (is), priyaḥ (dear), yajñaḥ (sacrifice), tapaḥ (austerity), tathā (and), dānam (charity), teṣām (of them), bhedam (distinctions), imam (this), śṛṇu (hear)
Meaning: Food, which is dear to all, is of three kinds; likewise, sacrifices, austerity, and charity are also of three kinds. Listen to this classification of them.
Verse 8
आयुःसत्त्वबलारोग्यसुखप्रीतिविवर्धनाः ।
रस्याः स्निग्धाः स्थिरा हृद्या आहाराः सात्त्विकप्रियाः ॥ ८॥
ఆయుః-సత్త్వ-బలారోగ్య-సుఖ-ప్రీతి-వివర్ధనాః ।
రస్యాః స్నిగ్ధాః స్థిరా హృద్యా ఆహారాః సాత్త్విక-ప్రియాః ॥ 8 ॥
āyuḥ-sattva-balārogya-sukha-prīti-vivardhanāḥ |
rasyāḥ snigdhāḥ sthirā hṛdyā āhārāḥ sāttvika-priyāḥ || 8 ||
Words: āyuḥ sattva (which promote longevity), bala (strength), ārogya (health), sukha (happiness), prīti (satisfaction), vivardhanāḥ (increase), rasyāḥ (juicy), snigdhāḥ (succulent), sthirāḥ (nourishing), hṛdyāḥ (pleasing to the heart), āhārāḥ (food), sāttvika-priyāḥ (dear to those in the mode of goodness)
Meaning: Foods that augment life, firmness of mind, strength, health, happiness, and delight, and which are succulent, oleaginous, substantial, and agreeable, are dear to one endowed with sattva.
Verse 9
कट्वम्ललवणात्युष्णतीक्ष्णरूक्षविदाहिनः ।
आहारा राजसस्येष्टा दुःखशोकामयप्रदाः ॥ ९॥
కట్వమ్ల-లవణాత్యుష్ణ-తీక్ష్ణ-రూక్ష-విదాహినః ।
ఆహారా రాజసస్యేష్టా దుఃఖ-శోకామయ-ప్రదాః ॥ 9 ॥
kaṭv-amla-lavaṇāty-uṣṇa- tīkṣṇa-rūkṣa-vidāhinaḥ |
āhārā rājasasyeṣṭā duḥkha-śokāmaya-pradāḥ || 9 ||
Words: kaṭu (bitter), amla (sour), lavaṇa (salty), ati-uṣṇa (very hot), tīkṣṇa (pungent), rūkṣa (dry), vidāhinaḥ (chiliful), āhārāḥ (food), rājasasya (to persons in the mode of passion), iṣṭāḥ (dear), duḥkha (pain), śoka (grief), āmaya (disease), pradāḥ (produce)
Meaning: Foods that are bitter, sour, salty, very hot, pungent, dry, and burning, producing pain, sorrow, and disease, are dear to one who has rajas.
Verse 10
यातयामं गतरसं पूति पर्युषितं च यत् ।
उच्छिष्टमपि चामेध्यं भोजनं तामसप्रियम् ॥ १०॥
యాత-యామం గత-రసం పూతి పర్యుషితం చ యత్ ।
ఉచ్ఛిష్టమపి చామేధ్యం భోజనం తామస-ప్రియమ్ ॥ 10 ॥
yāta-yāmaṁ gata-rasaṁ pūti paryuṣitaṁ cha yat |
uchchhiṣṭam api chāmedhyaṁ bhojanaṁ tāmasa-priyam || 10 ||
Words: yāta-yāmam (stale foods), gata-rasam (tasteless), pūti (putrid), paryuṣitam (polluted), cha (and), yat (which), uchchhiṣṭam (left over), api (also), cha (and), amedhyam (impure), bhojanam (foods), tāmasa (to persons in the mode of ignorance), priyam (dear)
Meaning: Food that is not properly cooked, lacking in essence, putrid and stale, as well as that which is unfit for sacrifice, is dear to one possessed of tamas.
Verse 11
अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते ।
यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः ॥ ११॥
అఫలాకాంక్షిభిర్యజ్ఞో విధిదృష్టో య ఇజ్యతే ।
యష్టవ్యమేవేతి మనః సమాధాయ స సాత్త్వికః ॥ 11 ॥
aphalākāṅkṣibhir yajño vidhi-driṣṭo ya ijyate |
yaṣṭavyam eveti manaḥ samādhāya sa sāttvikaḥ || 11 ||
Words: aphala-ākāṅkṣibhiḥ (without expectation of any reward), yajñaḥ (sacrifice), vidhi-driṣṭaḥ (that is in accordance with the scriptural injunctions), yaḥ (which), ijyate (is performed), yaṣṭavyam-eva-iti (ought to be offered), manaḥ (mind), samādhāya (with conviction), saḥ (that), sāttvikaḥ (of the nature of goodness)
Meaning: That sacrifice which is in accordance with the injunctions, performed by persons who do not hanker after results, and with the mental conviction that it is surely obligatory, is done through sattva.
Verse 12
अभिसंधाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् ।
इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम् ॥ १२॥
అభిసంధాయ తు ఫలం దంభార్థమపి చైవ యత్ ।
ఇజ్యతే భరత-శ్రేష్ఠ తం యజ్ఞం విద్ధి రాజసమ్ ॥ 12 ॥
abhisandhāya tu phalaṁ dambhārtham api chaiva yat |
ijyate bharata-śreṣṭha taṁ yajñaṁ viddhi rājasam || 12 ||
Words: abhisandhāya (motivated by), tu (but), phalam (the result), dambha (pride), artham (for the sake of), api (also), cha (and), eva (certainly), yat (that which), ijyate (is performed), bharata-śreṣṭha (Arjun, the best of the Bharatas), tam (that), yajñam (sacrifice), viddhi (know), rājasam (in the mode of passion)
Meaning: But that sacrifice which is performed with an aim for a result, as well as for show—know that sacrifice to be done through rajas, O greatest among the descendants of Bharata.
Verse 13
विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् ।
श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते ॥ १३॥
విధి-హీనమసృష్టాన్నం మంత్ర-హీనమదక్షిణమ్ ।
శ్రద్ధా-విరహితం యజ్ఞం తామసం పరిచక్షతే ॥ 13 ॥
vidhi-hīnam asṛṣṭānnaṁ mantra-hīnam adakṣiṇam |
śraddhā-virahitaṁ yajñaṁ tāmasaṁ parichakṣate || 13 ||
Words: vidhi-hīnam (without scriptural direction), asṛṣṭa-annam (without distribution of prasādam), mantra-hīnam (with no chanting of the Vedic hymns), adakṣiṇam (with no remunerations to the priests), śraddhā (faith), virahitam (without), yajñam (sacrifice), tāmasam (in the mode of ignorance), parichakṣate (is to be considered)
Meaning: They declare that sacrifice as 'done through tamas' which is contrary to injunction, in which no food is distributed, in which no mantras are used, in which no offerings are made to priests, and which is devoid of faith.
Verse 14
देवद्विजगुरुप्राज्ञपूजनं शौचमार्जवम् ।
ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते ॥ १४॥

బ్రహ్మచర్యమహింసా చ శారీరం తప ఉచ్యతే ॥ 14 ॥
deva-dwija-guru-prājña- pūjanaṁ śaucham ārjavam |

Words: deva (the Supreme Lord), dwija (the Brahmins), guru (the spiritual master), prājña (the elders), pūjanam (worship), śaucham (cleanliness), ārjavam (simplicity), brahmacharyam (celibacy), ahinsā (non-violence), cha (and), śārīram (of the body), tapaḥ (austerity), uchyate (is declared as)
Meaning: The worship of gods, twice-borns, venerable persons, and the wise; purity, straightforwardness, celibacy, and non-injury—these are said to be bodily austerities.
Verse 15
अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् ।

Words: anudvega-karam (not causing distress), vākyam (words), satyam (truthful), priya- hitam (beneficial), cha (and), yat (which), svādhyāya-abhyasanam (recitation of the Vedic scriptures), cha eva (as well as), vāṅ-mayam (of speech), tapaḥ (austerity), uchyate (are declared as)
Meaning: That speech which causes no pain, which is true, agreeable, and beneficial; as well as the practice of studying the scriptures—is said to be the austerity of speech.
Verse 16

భావ-సంశుద్ధిరిత్యేతత్-తపో మానసముచ్యతే ॥ 16 ॥

Meaning: Tranquility of mind, gentleness, reticence, withdrawal of the mind, purity of heart—these are what are called mental austerities.
Verse 17

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्युक्तैः सात्त्विकं परिचक्षते ॥ १७॥
శ్రద్ధయా పరయా తప్తం తపస్-తత్-త్రి-విధం నరైః ।
అఫలాకాంక్షిభిర్-యుక్తైః సాత్త్వికం పరిచక్షతే ॥ 17 ॥
śraddhayā parayā taptaṁ tapas tat tri-vidhaṁ naraiḥ |
aphalākāṅkṣibhir yuktaiḥ sāttvikaṁ parichakṣate || 17 ||
Words: śraddhayā (with faith), parayā (transcendental), taptam (practiced), tapaḥ (austerity), tat (that), tri-vidham (three-fold), naraiḥ (by persons), aphala-ākāṅkṣibhiḥ (without yearning for material rewards), yuktaiḥ (steadfast), sāttvikam (in the mode of goodness), parichakṣate (are designated)
Meaning: When that threefold austerity is undertaken with supreme faith by people who do not hanker after results and are self-controlled, they speak of it as being born of sattva.
Verse 18
सत्कारमानपूजार्थं तपो दम्भेन चैव यत् ।
क्रियते तदिह प्रोक्तं राजसं चलमध्रुवम् ॥ १८॥
సత్కార-మాన-పూజార్థం తపో దంభేన చైవ యత్ ।
క్రియతే తదిహ ప్రోక్తం రాజసం చలమధ్రువమ్ ॥ 18 ॥
satkāra-māna-pūjārthaṁ tapo dambhena chaiva yat |
kriyate tad iha proktaṁ rājasaṁ chalam adhruvam || 18 ||
Words: sat-kāra (respect), māna (honor), pūjā (adoration), artham (for the sake of), tapaḥ (austerity), dambhena (with ostentation), cha (also), eva (certainly), yat (which), kriyate (is performed), tat (that), iha (in this world), proktam (is said), rājasam (in the mode of passion), chalam (flickering), adhruvam (temporary)
Meaning: That austerity which is undertaken for earning a name, being honored and worshipped, and also ostentatiously—that is spoken of as born of rajas, belonging to this world, uncertain, and transitory.
Verse 19
मूढग्राहेणात्मनो यत्पीडया क्रियते तपः ।
परस्योत्सादनार्थं वा तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ १९॥
మూఢ-గ్రాహేణాత్మనో యత్-పీడయా క్రియతే తపః ।
పరస్యోత్సాదనార్థం వా తత్-తామసముదాహృతమ్ ॥ 19 ॥
mūḍha-grāheṇātmano yat pīḍayā kriyate tapaḥ |
parasyotsādanārthaṁ vā tat tāmasam udāhṛtam || 19 ||
Words: mūḍha (those with confused notions), grāheṇa (with endeavor), ātmanaḥ (one’s own self), yat (which), pīḍayā (torturing), kriyate (is performed), tapaḥ (austerity), parasya (of others), utsādana-artham (for harming), vā (or), tat (that), tāmasam (in the mode of ignorance), udāhṛtam (is described to be)
Meaning: That austerity which is undertaken with a foolish intent, causing pain to oneself or for the destruction of others, is said to be born of tamas.
Verse 20
दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे ।
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥ २०॥
దాతవ్యమితి యద్-దానం దీయతేఽనుపకారిణే ।
దేశే కాలే చ పాత్రే చ తద్-దానం సాత్త్వికం స్మృతమ్ ॥ 20 ॥
dātavyam iti yad dānaṁ dīyate ‘nupakāriṇe |
deśe kāle cha pātre cha tad dānaṁ sāttvikaṁ smṛtam || 20 ||
Words: dātavyam (worthy of charity), iti (thus), yat (which), dānam (charity), dīyate (is given), anupakāriṇe (to one who cannot give in return), deśe (in the proper place), kāle (at the proper time), cha (and), pātre (to a worthy person), cha (and), tat (that), dānam (charity), sāttvikam (in the mode of goodness), smṛtam (is stated to be)
Meaning: That gift is referred to as born of sattva which is given with the idea that it should be given, to one who will not serve in return, at the right place, right time, and to the right person.
Verse 21
यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः ।
दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम् ॥ २१॥
యత్-తు ప్రత్యుపకారార్థం ఫలముద్దిశ్య వా పునః ।
దీయతే చ పరిక్లిష్టం తద్-దానం రాజసం స్మృతమ్ ॥ 21 ॥
yat tu pratyupakārārthaṁ phalam uddiśya vā punaḥ |
dīyate cha parikliṣṭaṁ tad dānaṁ rājasaṁ smṛtam || 21 ||
Words: yat (which), tu (but), prati-upakāra-artham (with the hope of a return), phalam (reward), uddiśya (expectation), vā (or), punaḥ (again), dīyate (is given), cha (and), parikliṣṭam (reluctantly), tat (that), dānam (charity), rājasam (in the mode of passion), smṛtam (is said to be)
Meaning: But the gift which is given expecting reciprocation, or with a desire for its result, and grudgingly, is considered to be born of rajas.
Verse 22
अदेशकाले यद्दानमपात्रेभ्यश्च दीयते ।
असत्कृतमवज्ञातं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम् ॥ २२॥
అదేశ-కాలే యద్-దానమపాత్రేభ్యశ్-చ దీయతే ।
అసత్-కృతమవజ్ఞాతం తత్-తామసముదాహృతమ్ ॥ 22 ॥
adeśa-kāle yad dānam apātrebhyaś cha dīyate |
asat-kṛtam avajñātaṁ tat tāmasam udāhṛtam || 22 ||
Words: adeśa (at the wrong place), kāle (at the wrong time), yat (which), dānam (charity), apātrebhyaḥ (to unworthy persons), cha (and), dīyate (is given), asat-kṛtam (without respect), avajñātam (with contempt), tat (that), tāmasam (of the nature of nescience), udāhṛtam (is held to be)
Meaning: The gift which is made at an inappropriate place and time, and to undeserving persons, without proper respect and with disdain, is declared to be born of tamas.
Verse 23
ॐ तत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः ।
ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥ २३॥
ఓం తత్-సదితి నిర్దేశో బ్రహ్మణస్-త్రి-విధః స్మృతః ।
బ్రాహ్మణాస్-తేన వేదాశ్-చ యజ్ఞాశ్-చ విహితాః పురా ॥ 23 ॥
oṁ tat sad iti nirdeśo brahmaṇas tri-vidhaḥ smṛtaḥ |
brāhmaṇās tena vedāś cha yajñāś cha vihitāḥ purā || 23 ||
Words: om tat sat (syllables representing aspects of transcendence), iti (thus), nirdeśaḥ (symbolic representatives), brahmaṇaḥ (the Supreme Absolute Truth), tri-vidhaḥ (of three kinds), smṛtaḥ (have been declared), brāhmaṇāḥ (the priests), tena (from them), vedāḥ (scriptures), cha (and), yajñāḥ (sacrifice), cha (and), vihitāḥ (came about), purā (from the beginning of creation)
Meaning: "Om-tat-sat" - this is considered to be the threefold designation of Brahman. The Brahmanas, Vedas, and sacrifices were ordained by that in days of yore.
Verse 24
तस्मादोमित्युदाहृत्य यज्ञदानतपःक्रियाः ।
प्रवर्तन्ते विधानोक्ताः सततं ब्रह्मवादिनाम् ॥ २४॥
తస్మాదోమిత్యుదాహృత్య యజ్ఞ-దాన-తపః-క్రియాః ।
ప్రవర్తంతే విధానోక్తాః సతతం బ్రహ్మ-వాదినామ్ ॥ 24 ॥
tasmād oṁ ity udāhṛtya yajña-dāna-tapaḥ-kriyāḥ |
pravartante vidhānoktāḥ satataṁ brahma-vādinām || 24 ||
Words: tasmāt (therefore), om (sacred syllable om), iti (thus), udāhṛtya (by uttering), yajña (sacrifice), dāna (charity), tapaḥ (penance), kriyāḥ (performing), pravartante (begin), vidhāna-uktāḥ (according to the prescriptions of Vedic injunctions), satatam (always), brahma-vādinām (expounders of the Vedas)
Meaning: Therefore, acts of sacrifice, charity, and austerity, as prescribed through injunctions, of those who study and expound the Vedas, always commence after uttering the syllable ‘Om’.
Verse 25
तदित्यनभिसन्धाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः ।
दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षि ॥ २५॥
తదిత్యనభిసంధాయ ఫలం యజ్ఞతపఃక్రియాః ।
దానక్రియాశ్చ వివిధాః క్రియంతే మోక్షకాంక్షి ॥ 25 ॥
tad ity anabhisandhāya phalaṁ yajña-tapaḥ-kriyāḥ |
dāna-kriyāś cha vividhāḥ kriyante mokṣa-kāṅkṣibhiḥ || 25 ||
Words: tat (the syllable Tat), iti (thus), anabhisandhāya (without desiring), phalam (fruitive rewards), yajña (sacrifice), tapaḥ (austerity), kriyāḥ (acts), dāna (charity), kriyāḥ (acts), cha (and), vividhāḥ (various), kriyante (are done), mokṣa-kāṅkṣibhiḥ (by seekers of freedom from material entanglements)
Meaning: After uttering the word "tat," acts of sacrifice and austerity, as well as various acts of charity, are performed without regard for results by persons aspiring for liberation.
Verse 26
सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते ।
प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सच्छब्दः पार्थ युज्यते ॥ २६॥
సద్-భావే సాధు-భావే చ సదిత్యేతత్-ప్రయుజ్యతే ।
ప్రశస్తే కర్మణి తథా సచ్-ఛబ్దః పార్థ యుజ్యతే ॥ 26 ॥
sad-bhāve sādhu-bhāve cha sad ity etat prayujyate |
praśaste karmaṇi tathā sach-chhabdaḥ pārtha yujyate || 26 ||
Words: sat-bhāve (with the intention of eternal existence and goodness), sādhu-bhāve (with auspicious intention), cha (also), sat (the syllable Sat), iti (thus), etat (this), prayujyate (is used), praśaste (auspicious), karmaṇi (action), tathā (also), sat-śabdaḥ (the word “Sat”), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), yujyate (is used)
Meaning: This word "sat" is used with regard to something coming into being and someone becoming good. Likewise, O son of Prtha, the word "sat" is used with regard to an auspicious rite.
Verse 27
यज्ञे तपसि दाने च स्थितिः सदिति चोच्यते ।
कर्म चैव तदर्थीयं सदित्येवाभिधीयते ॥ २७॥
యజ్ఞే తపసి దానే చ స్థితిః సదితి చోచ్యతే ।
కర్మ చైవ తదర్థీయం సదిత్యేవాభిధీయతే ॥ 27 ॥
yajñe tapasi dāne cha sthitiḥ sad iti chochyate |
karma chaiva tad-arthīyaṁ sad ity evābhidhīyate || 27 ||
Words: yajñe (in sacrifice), tapasi (in penance), dāne (in charity), cha (and), sthitiḥ (established in steadiness), sat (the syllable Sat), iti (thus), cha (and), uchyate (is pronounced), karma (action), cha (and), eva (indeed), tat-arthīyam (for such purposes), sat (the syllable Sat), iti (thus), eva (indeed), abhidhīyate (is described)
Meaning: And the steadfastness in sacrifice, austerity, and charity is spoken of as sat (good). And even the action meant for these is, indeed, called as sat.
Verse 28
अश्रद्धया हुतं दत्तं तपस्तप्तं कृतं च यत् ।
असदित्युच्यते पार्थ न च तत्प्रेत्य नो इह ॥ २८॥
అశ్రద్ధయా హుతం దత్తం తపస్-తప్తం కృతం చ యత్ ।
అసదిత్యుచ్యతే పార్థ న చ తత్-ప్రేత్య నో ఇహ ॥ 28 ॥
aśraddhayā hutaṁ dattaṁ tapas taptaṁ kṛtaṁ cha yat |
asad ity uchyate pārtha na cha tat pretya no iha || 28 ||
Words: aśraddhayā (without faith), hutam (sacrifice), dattam (charity), tapaḥ (penance), taptam (practiced), kṛtam (done), cha (and), yat (which), asat (perishable), iti (thus), uchyate (are termed as), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), na (not), cha (and), tat (that), pretya (in the next world), na u (not), iha (in this world)
Meaning: O son of Prtha, whatever is offered in sacrifice and given in charity, as also whatever austerity is undertaken or whatever is done without faith, is said to be of no avail. And it is of no consequence after death, nor here.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे श्रद्धात्रयविभागयोगो नाम सप्तदशोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the seventeenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Śraddhātraya Vibhāga Yoga — the Yoga of Distinguishing the Three Kinds of Faith.
