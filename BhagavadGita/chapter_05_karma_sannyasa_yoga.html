Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 5

अध्यायः ५ · कर्मसंन्यासयोगः — The Yoga of Renunciation of Action

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 29 verses.

Arjuna asks whether renunciation or karma-yoga is better. Krishna answers that both lead to the highest good, yet karma-yoga is superior for most — the wise act without attachment, see the same Self in all beings, and attain brahma-nirvāṇa through equanimity and devotion.

The Twenty-Nine Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
संन्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंससि ।
यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रूहि सुनिश्चितम् ॥ १॥
సంన్యాసం కర్మణాం కృష్ణ పునర్-యోగం చ శంససి ।
యచ్-ఛ్రేయ ఏతయోరేకం తన్-మే బ్రూహి సు-నిశ్చితమ్ ॥ 1 ॥
sannyāsaṁ karmaṇāṁ kṛṣṇa punar yogaṁ cha śansasi |
yach chhreya etayor ekaṁ tan me brūhi su-niśchitam || 1 ||
Words: sanyāsam (renunciation), karmaṇām (of actions), kṛṣṇa (Shree Krishna), punaḥ (again), yogam (about karm yog), cha (also), śansasi (you praise), yat (which), śreyaḥ (more beneficial), etayoḥ (of the two), ekam (one), tat (that), me (unto me), brūhi (please tell), su-niśchitam (conclusively)
Meaning: O Krsna, you praise renunciation of actions and, at the same time, Karma-yoga. Please tell me for certain which one is better between these two."
Verse 2
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
संन्यासः कर्मयोगश्च निःश्रेयसकरावुभौ ।
तयोस्तु कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो विशिष्यते ॥ २॥
సంన్యాసః కర్మ-యోగశ్-చ నిఃశ్రేయస-కరావుభౌ ।
తయోస్-తు కర్మ-సంన్యాసాత్-కర్మ-యోగో విశిష్యతే ॥ 2 ॥
sannyāsaḥ karma-yogaś cha niḥśreyasa-karāvubhau |
tayos tu karma-sannyāsāt karma-yogo viśiṣyate || 2 ||
Words: sanyāsaḥ (renunciation), karma-yogaḥ (working in devotion), cha (and), niḥśreyasa-karau (lead to the supreme goal), ubhau (both), tayoḥ (of the two), tu (but), karma-sanyāsāt (renunciation of actions), karma-yogaḥ (working in devotion), viśiṣyate (is superior)
Meaning: Both renunciation of actions and Karma-yoga lead to liberation; however, Karma-yoga excels over renunciation of actions."
Verse 3
ज्ञेयः स नित्यसंन्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न काङ्क्षति ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते ॥ ३॥
జ్ఞేయః స నిత్య-సంన్యాసీ యో న ద్వేష్టి న కాంక్షతి ।
నిర్ద్వంద్వో హి మహా-బాహో సుఖం బంధాత్-ప్రముచ్యతే ॥ 3 ॥
jñeyaḥ sa nitya-sannyāsī yo na dveṣṭi na kāṅkṣati |
nirdvandvo hi mahā-bāho sukhaṁ bandhāt pramuchyate || 3 ||
Words: jñeyaḥ (should be considered), saḥ (that person), nitya (always), sanyāsī (practising renunciation), yaḥ (who), na (never), dveṣṭi (hate), na (nor), kāṅkṣati (desire), nirdvandvaḥ (free from all dualities), hi (certainly), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed one), sukham (easily), bandhāt (from bondage), pramuchyate (is liberated)
Meaning: He who neither hates nor craves should be known as a man of constant renunciation.
Verse 4
सांख्ययोगौ पृथग्बालाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः ।
एकमप्यास्थितः सम्यगुभयोर्विन्दते फलम् ॥ ४॥
సాంఖ్య-యోగౌ పృథగ్-బాలాః ప్రవదంతి న పండితాః ।
ఏకమప్యాస్థితః సమ్యగుభయోర్-విందతే ఫలమ్ ॥ 4 ॥
sānkhya-yogau pṛthag bālāḥ pravadanti na paṇḍitāḥ |
ekamapyāsthitaḥ samyag ubhayor vindate phalam || 4 ||
Words: sānkhya (renunciation of actions), yogau (karm yog), pṛthak (different), bālāḥ (the ignorant), pravadanti (say), na (never), paṇḍitāḥ (the learned), ekam (in one), api (even), āsthitaḥ (being situated), samyak (completely), ubhayoḥ (of both), vindate (achieve), phalam (the result)
Meaning: The fools, not the learned ones, speak of Sankhya and (Karma-) yoga as being different. Anyone who properly resorts to either one of them gets the results of both.
Verse 5
यत्सांख्यैः प्राप्यते स्थानं तद्योगैरपि गम्यते ।
एकं सांख्यं च योगं च यः पश्यति स पश्यति ॥ ५॥
యత్-సాంఖ్యైః ప్రాప్యతే స్థానం తద్-యోగైరపి గమ్యతే ।
ఏకం సాంఖ్యం చ యోగం చ యః పశ్యతి స పశ్యతి ॥ 5 ॥
yat sānkhyaiḥ prāpyate sthānaṁ tad yogair api gamyate |
ekaṁ sānkhyaṁ cha yogaṁ cha yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati || 5 ||
Words: yat (what), sānkhyaiḥ (by means of karm sanyās), prāpyate (is attained), sthānam (place), tat (that), yogaiḥ (by working in devotion), api (also), gamyate (is attained), ekam (one), sānkhyam (renunciation of actions), cha (and), yogam (karm yog), cha (and), yaḥ (who), paśyati (sees), saḥ (that person), paśyati (actually sees)
Meaning: The state that is reached by the Sankhyas, and that is also reached by the yogis, is seen by one who sees Sankhya and yoga as one.
Verse 6
संन्यासस्तु महाबाहो दुःखमाप्तुमयोगतः ।
योगयुक्तो मुनिर्ब्रह्म नचिरेणाधिगच्छति ॥ ६॥
సంన్యాసస్-తు మహా-బాహో దుఃఖమాప్తుమయోగతః ।
యోగ-యుక్తో మునిర్-బ్రహ్మ న-చిరేణాధిగచ్ఛతి ॥ 6 ॥
sannyāsas tu mahā-bāho duḥkham āptum ayogataḥ |
yoga-yukto munir brahma na chireṇādhigachchhati || 6 ||
Words: sanyāsaḥ (renunciation), tu (but), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed one), duḥkham (distress), āptum (attains), ayogataḥ (without karm yog), yoga-yuktaḥ (one who is adept in karm yog), muniḥ (a sage), brahma (Brahman), na chireṇa (quickly), adhigachchhati (goes)
Meaning: But, O mighty-armed one, renunciation is hard to attain without (Karma-) yoga. The meditative man equipped with yoga attains Brahman without delay.
Verse 7
योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ।
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते ॥ ७॥
యోగ-యుక్తో విశుద్ధాత్మా విజితాత్మా జితేంద్రియః ।
సర్వ-భూతాత్మ-భూతాత్మా కుర్వన్నపి న లిప్యతే ॥ 7 ॥
yoga-yukto viśuddhātmā vijitātmā jitendriyaḥ |
sarva-bhūtātma-bhūtātmā kurvann api na lipyate || 7 ||
Words: yoga-yuktaḥ (united in consciousness with God), viśuddha-ātmā (one with purified intellect), vijita-ātmā (one who has conquered the mind), jita-indriyaḥ (having conquered the senses), sarva-bhūta-ātma-bhūta-ātmā (one who sees the Soul of all souls in every living being), kurvan (performing), api (although), na (never), lipyate (entangled)
Meaning: Endowed with yoga, pure in mind, controlled in body, a conqueror of the organs, the Self of the selves of all beings—he does not become tainted even while performing actions. When this person resorts to nitya and naimittika rites and duties as a means to the achievement of full illumination, and thus becomes fully enlightened, then, even when he acts through the apparent functions of the mind, organs, etc., he does not become affected.
Verse 8
नैव किंचित्करोमीति युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित् ।
पश्यन् शृण्वन्स्पृशञ्जिघ्रन्नश्नन्गच्छन्स्वपन् श्वसन् ॥ ८॥
నైవ కించిత్కరోమీతి యుక్తో మన్యేత తత్త్వవిత్ ।
పశ్యన్ శృణ్వన్స్పృశంజిఘ్రన్నశ్నన్గచ్ఛన్స్వపన్ శ్వసన్ ॥ 8 ॥

Meaning: - Remaining absorbed in the Self, the knower of Reality should think, 'I certainly do not do anything', even while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, moving, sleeping, breathing, speaking, releasing, holding, opening, and closing the eyes—remembering that the organs function in relation to the objects of the organs.

Verse 10
ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः ।

బ్రహ్మణ్యాధాయ కర్మాణి సంగం త్యక్త్వా కరోతి యః ।

brahmaṇyādhāya karmāṇi saṅgaṁ tyaktvā karoti yaḥ |

Words: brahmaṇi (to God), ādhāya (dedicating), karmāṇi (all actions), saṅgam (attachment), tyaktvā (abandoning), karoti (performs), yaḥ (who), lipyate (is affected), na (never), saḥ (that person), pāpena (by sin), padma-patram (a lotus leaf), iva (like), ambhasā (by water)
Meaning: One who acts by dedicating their actions to Brahman and by renouncing attachment, they do not become polluted by sin, just as a lotus leaf is not by water.
Verse 11
कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि ।
योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वाऽऽत्मशुद्धये ॥ ११॥

యోగినః కర్మ కుర్వంతి సంగం త్యక్త్వాఽఽత్మ-శుద్ధయే ॥ 11 ॥
kāyena manasā buddhyā kevalair indriyair api |

Words: kāyena (with the body), manasā (with the mind), buddhyā (with the intellect), kevalaiḥ (only), indriyaiḥ (with the senses), api (even), yoginaḥ (the yogis), karma (actions), kurvanti (perform), saṅgam (attachment), tyaktvā (giving up), ātma (of the self), śuddhaye (for the purification)
Meaning: By giving up attachment, the yogis undertake work solely through the body, mind, intellect, and even the organs, for their own purification.
Verse 12

अयुक्तः कामकारेण फले सक्तो निबध्यते ॥ १२॥

అయుక్తః కామ-కారేణ ఫలే సక్తో నిబధ్యతే ॥ 12 ॥
yuktaḥ karma-phalaṁ tyaktvā śāntim āpnoti naiṣṭhikīm |
ayuktaḥ kāma-kāreṇa phale sakto nibadhyate || 12 ||
Words: yuktaḥ (one who is united in consciousness with God), karma-phalam (the results of all activities), tyaktvā (giving up), śāntim (peace), āpnoti (attains), naiṣṭhikīm (everlasting), ayuktaḥ (one who is not united with God in consciousness), kāma-kāreṇa (impelled by desires), phale (in the result), saktaḥ (attached), nibadhyate (becomes entangled)
Meaning: Giving up the results of work by becoming resolute in faith, one attains peace arising from steadfastness. One who is lacking in resolute faith, being attached to the results under the impulse of desire, becomes bound.
Verse 13

Meaning: The embodied man of self-control, having given up all actions mentally, continues happily in the city of nine gates, without doing or causing anyone else to do anything.
Verse 14

Meaning: The Self does not create agency or any objects of desire for anyone, nor does it associate with the results of actions; rather, it is Nature that acts.
Verse 15

Meaning: The Omnipresent neither accepts anyone's sin nor even virtue; knowledge remains covered by ignorance, thus the creatures become deluded.
Verse 16

Meaning: But in the case of those whose ignorance is destroyed by knowledge (of the Self), their knowledge, like the sun, reveals that supreme Reality.
Verse 17
तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः ।
गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः ॥ १७॥
తద్-బుద్ధయస్-తదాత్మానస్-తన్-నిష్ఠాస్-తత్-పరాయణాః ।
గచ్ఛంత్యపునరావృత్తిం జ్ఞాన-నిర్ధూత-కల్మషాః ॥ 17 ॥
tad-buddhayas tad-ātmānas tan-niṣṭhās tat-parāyaṇāḥ |
gachchhantyapunar-āvṛttiṁ jñāna-nirdhūta-kalmaṣāḥ || 17 ||
Words: tat-buddhayaḥ (those whose intellect is directed toward God), tat-ātmānaḥ (those whose heart (mind and intellect) is solely absorbed in God), tat-niṣṭhāḥ (those whose intellect has firm faith in God), tat-parāyaṇāḥ (those who strive after God as the supreme goal and refuge), gachchhanti (go), apunaḥ-āvṛttim (not returning), jñāna (by knowledge), nirdhūta (dispelled), kalmaṣāḥ (sins)
Meaning: Those whose intellect is absorbed in That, whose Self is That, who are steadfast in That, and who have That as their supreme Goal—they attain the state of non-returning, their impurities having been removed by Knowledge.
Verse 18
विद्याविनयसंपन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि ।
शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः ॥ १८॥
విద్యా-వినయ-సంపన్నే బ్రాహ్మణే గవి హస్తిని ।
శుని చైవ శ్వ-పాకే చ పండితాః సమ-దర్శినః ॥ 18 ॥
vidyā-vinaya-sampanne brāhmaṇe gavi hastini |
śuni chaiva śva-pāke cha paṇḍitāḥ sama-darśinaḥ || 18 ||
Words: vidyā (divine knowledge), vinaya (humbleness), sampanne (equipped with), brāhmaṇe (a Brahmin), gavi (a cow), hastini (an elephant), śuni (a dog), cha (and), eva (certainly), śva-pāke (a dog-eater), cha (and), paṇḍitāḥ (the learned), sama-darśinaḥ (see with equal vision)
Meaning: The learned ones look with equanimity on a Brahmana endowed with learning and humility, a cow, an elephant, and even a dog, as well as one who eats dog meat.
Verse 19
इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः ।
निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद्ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः ॥ १९॥
ఇహైవ తైర్-జితః సర్గో యేషాం సామ్యే స్థితం మనః ।
నిర్దోషం హి సమం బ్రహ్మ తస్మాద్-బ్రహ్మణి తే స్థితాః ॥ 19 ॥
ihaiva tair jitaḥ sargo yeṣāṁ sāmye sthitaṁ manaḥ |
nirdoṣaṁ hi samaṁ brahma tasmād brahmaṇi te sthitāḥ || 19 ||
Words: iha eva (in this very life), taiḥ (by them), jitaḥ (conquer), sargaḥ (the creation), yeṣām (whose), sāmye (in equanimity), sthitam (situated), manaḥ (mind), nirdoṣam (flawless), hi (certainly), samam (in equality), brahma (God), tasmāt (therefore), brahmaṇi (in the Absolute Truth), te (they), sthitāḥ (are seated)
Meaning: Here itself is mirth attained by those whose minds are established in sameness. Since Brahman is the same in all and free from defects, they are established in Brahman.
Verse 20
न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् ।
स्थिरबुद्धिरसम्मूढो ब्रह्मविद्ब्रह्मणि स्थितः ॥ २०॥
న ప్రహృష్యేత్-ప్రియం ప్రాప్య నోద్విజేత్-ప్రాప్య చాప్రియమ్ ।
స్థిర-బుద్ధిరసమ్మూఢో బ్రహ్మ-విద్-బ్రహ్మణి స్థితః ॥ 20 ॥
na prahṛṣyet priyaṁ prāpya nodvijet prāpya chāpriyam |
sthira-buddhir asammūḍho brahma-vid brahmaṇi sthitaḥ || 20 ||
Words: na (neither), prahṛṣyet (rejoice), priyam (the pleasant), prāpya (obtaining), na (nor), udvijet (become disturbed), prāpya (attaining), cha (also), apriyam (the unpleasant), sthira-buddhiḥ (steady intellect), asammūḍhaḥ (firmly situated), brahma-vit (having a firm understanding of divine knowledge), brahmaṇi (established in God), sthitaḥ (situated)
Meaning: A knower of Brahman, who is established in Brahman, should have their intellect steady and should not be deluded. They should not get delighted by getting what is desirable, nor become dejected by getting what is undesirable.
Verse 21
बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम् ।
स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षयमश्नुते ॥ २१॥
బాహ్య-స్పర్శేష్వసక్తాత్మా విందత్యాత్మని యత్-సుఖమ్ ।
స బ్రహ్మ-యోగ-యుక్తాత్మా సుఖమక్షయమశ్నుతే ॥ 21 ॥
bāhya-sparśeṣvasaktātmā vindatyātmani yat sukham |
sa brahma-yoga-yuktātmā sukham akṣayam aśnute || 21 ||
Words: bāhya-sparśeṣu (external sense pleasure), asakta-ātmā (those who are unattached), vindati (find), ātmani (in the self), yat (which), sukham (bliss), saḥ (that person), brahma-yoga yukta-ātmā (those who are united with God through yog), sukham (happiness), akṣayam (unlimited), aśnute (experiences)
Meaning: With his heart unattached to external objects, he gets the bliss that is in the Self. With his heart absorbed in meditation on Brahman, he acquires undying Bliss.
Verse 22
ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते ।
आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः ॥ २२॥
యే హి సంస్పర్శ-జా భోగా దుఃఖ-యోనయ ఏవ తే ।
ఆద్యంతవంతః కౌంతేయ న తేషు రమతే బుధః ॥ 22 ॥
ye hi sansparśa-jā bhogā duḥkha-yonaya eva te |
ādyantavantaḥ kaunteya na teṣu ramate budhaḥ || 22 ||
Words: ye (which), hi (verily), sansparśa-jāḥ (born of contact with the sense objects), bhogāḥ (pleasures), duḥkha (misery), yonayaḥ (source of), eva (verily), te (they are), ādya-antavantaḥ (having beginning and end), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), na (never), teṣu (in those), ramate (takes delight), budhaḥ (the wise)
Meaning: Since enjoyments that result from contact with objects are indeed the sources of sorrow, having a beginning and an end, O son of Kunti, the wise one does not delight in them.
Verse 23
शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्शरीरविमोक्षणात् ।
कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः ॥ २३॥
శక్నోతీహైవ యః సోఢుం ప్రాక్-శరీర-విమోక్షణాత్ ।
కామ-క్రోధోద్భవం వేగం స యుక్తః స సుఖీ నరః ॥ 23 ॥
śaknotīhaiva yaḥ soḍhuṁ prāk śarīra-vimokṣaṇāt |
kāma-krodhodbhavaṁ vegaṁ sa yuktaḥ sa sukhī naraḥ || 23 ||
Words: śaknoti (is able), iha eva (in the present body), yaḥ (who), soḍhum (to withstand), prāk (before), śarīra (the body), vimokṣaṇāt (giving up), kāma (desire), krodha (anger), udbhavam (generated from), vegam (forces), saḥ (that person), yuktaḥ (yogi), saḥ (that person), sukhī (happy), naraḥ (person)
Meaning: One who can withstand here itself, before departing from the body, the impulse arising from desire and anger, that person is a yogi; they are happy.
Verse 24
योऽन्तःसुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव यः ।
स योगी ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति ॥ २४॥
యోఽంతః-సుఖోఽంతరారామస్-తథాంతర్-జ్యోతిరేవ యః ।
స యోగీ బ్రహ్మ-నిర్వాణం బ్రహ్మ-భూతోఽధిగచ్ఛతి ॥ 24 ॥
yo 'ntaḥ-sukho 'ntar-ārāmas tathāntar-jyotir eva yaḥ |
sa yogī brahma-nirvāṇaṁ brahma-bhūto 'dhigachchhati || 24 ||
Words: yaḥ (who), antaḥ-sukhaḥ (happy within the self), antaḥ-ārāmaḥ (enjoying within the self), tathā, antaḥ-jyotiḥ (illumined by the inner light), eva (certainly), yaḥ (who), saḥ, yogī (yogi), brahma-nirvāṇam (liberation from material existence), brahmabhūtaḥ (united with the Lord), adhigachchhati (attains)
Meaning: One who is content within, whose pleasure is within, and whose light is only within, that yogi, having become Brahman, attains absorption into Brahman.
Verse 25
लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।
छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः ॥ २५॥
లభంతే బ్రహ్మ-నిర్వాణమృషయః క్షీణ-కల్మషాః ।
ఛిన్న-ద్వైధా యతాత్మానః సర్వ-భూత-హితే రతాః ॥ 25 ॥
labhante brahma-nirvāṇam ṛṣayaḥ kṣīṇa-kalmaṣāḥ |
chhinna-dvaidhā yatātmānaḥ sarva-bhūta-hite ratāḥ || 25 ||
Words: labhante (achieve), brahma-nirvāṇam (liberation from material existence), ṛṣayaḥ (holy persons), kṣīṇa-kalmaṣāḥ (whose sins have been purged), chhinna (annihilated), dvaidhāḥ (doubts), yata-ātmānaḥ (whose minds are disciplined), sarva-bhūta (for all living entities), hite (in welfare work), ratāḥ (rejoice)
Meaning: The seers whose sins have been attenuated, who are free from doubt, whose senses are under control, who are engaged in doing good to all beings, attain absorption in Brahman.
Verse 26
कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाम् ।
अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम् ॥ २६॥
కామ-క్రోధ-వియుక్తానాం యతీనాం యత-చేతసామ్ ।
అభితో బ్రహ్మ-నిర్వాణం వర్తతే విదితాత్మనామ్ ॥ 26 ॥
kāma-krodha-viyuktānāṁ yatīnāṁ yata-chetasām |
abhito brahma-nirvāṇaṁ vartate viditātmanām || 26 ||
Words: kāma (desires), krodha (anger), vimuktānām (of those who are liberated), yatīnām (of the saintly persons), yata-chetasām (those self-realized persons who have subdued their mind), abhitaḥ (from every side), brahma (spiritual), nirvāṇam (liberation from material existence), vartate (exists), vidita-ātmanām (of those who are self-realized)
Meaning: To those monks who have control over their internal organs, who are free from desire and anger, who have known the Self, there is absorption into Brahman either way.
Verse 27
स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे भ्रुवोः ।
प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ ॥ २७॥
స్పర్శాన్కృత్వా బహిర్బాహ్యాంశ్చక్షుశ్చైవాంతరే భ్రువోః ।
ప్రాణాపానౌ సమౌ కృత్వా నాసాభ్యంతరచారిణౌ ॥ 27 ॥
sparśān kṛtvā bahir bāhyānś chakṣuś chaivāntare bhruvoḥ |
prāṇāpānau samau kṛtvā nāsābhyantara-chāriṇau yatendriya-mano-buddhir munir mokṣa-parāyaṇaḥ vigatechchhā-bhaya-krodho yaḥ sadā mukta eva saḥ || 27 ||
Words: sparśān (contacts (through senses)), kṛtvā (keeping), bahiḥ (outside), bāhyān (external), chakṣuḥ (eyes), cha (and), eva (certainly), antare (between), bhruvoḥ (of the eyebrows), prāṇa-apānau (the outgoing and incoming breaths), samau (equal), kṛtvā (keeping), nāsa-abhyantara (within the nostrils), chāriṇau (moving), yata (controlled), indriya (senses), manaḥ (mind), buddhiḥ (intellect), muniḥ (the sage), mokṣa (liberation), parāyaṇaḥ (dedicated), vigata (free), ichchhā (desires), bhaya (fear), krodhaḥ (anger), yaḥ (who), sadā (always), muktaḥ (liberated), eva (certainly), saḥ (that person)
Meaning: - Keeping external objects outside, fixing the eyes at the juncture of the eye-brows, and controlling the outgoing and incoming breaths that move through the nostrils, the contemplative who has mastery over his organs, mind, and intellect should be fully intent on Liberation and free from desire, fear, and anger. He who is ever thus is verily free.
Verse 28
यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः ।
विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः ॥ २८॥
యతేంద్రియ-మనో-బుద్ధిర్-మునిర్-మోక్ష-పరాయణః ।
విగతేచ్ఛా-భయ-క్రోధో యః సదా ముక్త ఏవ సః ॥ 28 ॥
yatendriya-mano-buddhir munir mokṣa-parāyaṇaḥ vigatecchā-bhaya-krodho yaḥ |
sadā mukta eva saḥ || 28 ||
Words: yata (controlled), indriya (senses), manaḥ (mind), buddhiḥ (intelligence), muniḥ (the transcendentalist), mokṣa (liberation), parāyaṇaḥ (being so destined), vigata (discarded), icchā (wishes), bhaya (fear), krodhaḥ (anger), yaḥ (one who), sadā (always), muktaḥ (liberated), eva (certainly), saḥ (he is)
Meaning: - Keeping external objects outside, fixing the eyes at the juncture of the eye-brows, and controlling the outgoing and incoming breaths that move through the nostrils, the contemplative who has mastery over his organs, mind, and intellect should be fully intent on Liberation and free from desire, fear, and anger. He who is ever thus is verily free.
Verse 29
भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम् ।
सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति ॥ २९॥
భోక్తారం యజ్ఞ-తపసాం సర్వ-లోక-మహేశ్వరమ్ ।
సుహృదం సర్వ-భూతానాం జ్ఞాత్వా మాం శాంతిమృచ్ఛతి ॥ 29 ॥
bhoktāraṁ yajña-tapasāṁ sarva-loka-maheśvaram |
suhṛdaṁ sarva-bhūtānāṁ jñātvā māṁ śāntim ṛchchhati || 29 ||
Words: bhoktāram (the enjoyer), yajña (sacrifices), tapasām (austerities), sarva-loka (of all worlds), mahā-īśvaram (the Supreme Lord), su-hṛdam (the selfless Friend), sarva (of all), bhūtānām (the living beings), jñātvā (having realized), mām (me (Lord Krishna)), śāntim (peace), ṛchchhati (attains)
Meaning: One attains peace by knowing Me, who am the great Lord of all the worlds, the enjoyer of sacrifices and austerities, and the friend of all creatures.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे कर्मसंन्यासयोगो नाम पञ्चमोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the fifth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Karma Sannyāsa Yoga — the Yoga of Renunciation of Action.
