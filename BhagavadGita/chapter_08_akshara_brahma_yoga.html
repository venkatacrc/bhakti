Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 8

अध्यायः ८ · अक्षरब्रह्मयोगः — The Yoga of the Imperishable Brahman

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 28 verses.

Arjuna asks about Brahman, the Self, karma, and the divine realms. Krishna defines these, teaches remembrance of Him at death, the paths of light and darkness, and the supreme Person beyond the unmanifest — reached by unswerving devotion.

The Twenty-Eight Verses

Verse 1
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते ॥ १॥
కిం తద్బ్రహ్మ కిమధ్యాత్మం కిం కర్మ పురుష-ఉత్తమ ।
అధి-భూతం చ కిం ప్రోక్తమధిదైవం కిముచ్యతే ॥ ౧॥
kiṁ tad brahma kim adhyātmaṁ kiṁ karma puruṣottama |
adhibhūtaṁ cha kiṁ proktam adhidaivaṁ kim uchyate || 1 ||
Words: kim (what), tat (that), brahma (Brahman), kim (what), adhyātmam (the individual soul), kim (what), karma (the principle of karma), puruṣa-uttama (Shree Krishna, the Supreme Divine Personality), adhibhūtam (the material manifestation), cha (and), kim (what), proktam (is called), adhidaivam (the Lord of the celestial gods), kim (what), uchyate (is called)
Meaning: O Supreme Person, what is Brahman? What exists in the individual plane? What is action? What is said to exist in the physical plane? And what is said to exist in the divine plane?
Verse 2
अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।

ప్రయాణ-కాలే చ కథం జ్ఞేయోఽసి నియతాత్మభిః ॥ ౨॥
adhiyajñaḥ kathaṁ ko ’tra dehe ’smin madhusūdana |
prayāṇa-kāle cha kathaṁ jñeyo ’si niyatātmabhiḥ || 2 ||
Words: adhiyajñaḥ (the Lord all sacrificial performances), katham (how), kaḥ (who), atra (here), dehe (in body), asmin (this), madhusūdana (Shree Krishna, the killer of the demon named Madhu), prayāṇa-kāle (at the time of death), cha (and), katham (how), jñeyaḥ (to be known), asi (are (you)), niyata-ātmabhiḥ (by those of steadfast mind)
Meaning: O Madhusudana, how is the entity existing in the sacrifice here in this body, and who is it? And how are You to be known by people of concentrated minds at the time of death?
Verse 3
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः ॥ ३॥
అక్షరం బ్రహ్మ పరమం స్వభావోఽధ్యాత్మముచ్యతే ।
భూతభావోద్భవకరో విసర్గః కర్మసంజ్ఞితః ॥ ౩॥
akṣaraṁ brahma paramaṁ svabhāvo ’dhyātmam uchyate |
bhūta-bhāvodbhava-karo visargaḥ karma-sanjñitaḥ || 3 ||
Words: akṣaram (indestructible), brahma (Brahman), paramam (the Supreme), svabhāvaḥ (nature), adhyātmam (one’s own self), uchyate (is called), bhūta-bhāva-udbhava-karaḥ (Actions pertaining to the material personality of living beings, and its development), visargaḥ (creation), karma (fruitive activities), sanjñitaḥ (are called)
Meaning: The Immutable is the supreme Brahman; selfhood is said to be the entity present in the individual plane. By action is meant the offerings that bring about the origin of existence of things."
Verse 4
अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर ॥ ४॥
అధి-భూతం క్షరో భావః పురుషశ్చాధిదైవతమ్ ।
అధియజ్ఞోఽహమేవాత్ర దేహే దేహభృతాం వర ॥ ౪॥
adhibhūtaṁ kṣaro bhāvaḥ puruṣaś chādhidaivatam |
adhiyajño ’ham evātra dehe deha-bhṛtāṁ vara || 4 ||
Words: adhibhūtam (the ever changing physical manifestation), kṣaraḥ (perishable), bhāvaḥ (nature), puruṣaḥ (the cosmic personality of God, encompassing the material creation), cha (and), adhidaivatam (the Lord of the celestial gods), adhiyajñaḥ (the Lord of all sacrifices), aham (I), eva (certainly), atra (here), dehe (in the body), deha-bhṛtām (of the embodied), vara (O best)
Meaning: That which exists in the physical plane is the mutable entity, and what exists in the divine plane is the Person. O best among the embodied beings, I Myself am the entity that exists in the sacrifice within this body.
Verse 5

యః ప్రయాతి స మద్భావం యాతి నాస్త్యత్ర సంశయః ॥ ౫॥

yaḥ prayāti sa mad-bhāvaṁ yāti nāstyatra sanśayaḥ || 5 ||
Words: anta-kāle (at the time of death), cha (and), mām (me), eva (alone), smaran (remembering), muktvā (relinquish), kalevaram (the body), yaḥ (who), prayāti (goes), saḥ (he), mat-bhāvam (Godlike nature), yāti (achieves), na (no), asti (there is), atra (here), sanśayaḥ (doubt)
Meaning: And at the time of death, anyone who departs, giving up the body while thinking of Me alone, attains My state; there is no doubt about this.
Verse 6
यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥ ६॥
యం యం వాపి స్మరన్భావం త్యజత్యన్తే కలేవరమ్ ।
తం తమేవైతి కౌన్తేయ సదా తద్భావభావితః ॥ ౬॥
yaṁ yaṁ vāpi smaran bhāvaṁ tyajatyante kalevaram |
taṁ tam evaiti kaunteya sadā tad-bhāva-bhāvitaḥ || 6 ||
Words: yam yam (whatever), vā (or), api (even), smaran (remembering), bhāvam (remembrance), tyajati (gives up), ante (in the end), kalevaram (the body), tam (to that), tam (to that), eva (certainly), eti (gets), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), sadā (always), tat (that), bhāva-bhāvitaḥ (absorbed in contemplation)
Meaning: O son of Kunti, thinking of any entity, whichever it may be, one gives up the body at the end; he attains that very one, having been always engrossed in its thought.
Verse 7
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम् ॥ ७॥

మయ్యర్పితమనోబుద్ధిర్మామేవైష్యస్యసంశయమ్ ॥ ౭॥

Words: tasmāt (therefore), sarveṣu (in all), kāleṣu (times), mām (me), anusmara (remember), yudhya (fight), cha (and), mayi (to me), arpita (surrender), manaḥ (mind), buddhiḥ (intellect), mām (to me), eva (surely), eṣyasi (you shall attain), asanśayaḥ (without a doubt)
Meaning: Therefore, think of Me at all times and fight; there is no doubt that by dedicating your mind and intellect to Me, you will attain only Me.
Verse 8
अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।

Meaning: O son of Prtha, by meditating with a mind engaged in the yoga of practice and not straying away to anything else, one reaches the supreme Person existing in the effulgent region.
Verse 9

Meaning: He who meditates on the Omniscient, the Ancient, the Ruler, subtler than the subtle, the Ordainer of everything, of inconceivable form, effulgent like the sun, and beyond darkness—he attains the Supreme Person.

Meaning: At the time of death, having fully fixed the Prana (vital force) between the eyebrows with an unwavering mind, and being imbued with devotion as well as the strength of concentration, he reaches that resplendent Supreme Person.
Verse 11
यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।

Meaning: I will briefly tell you about that immutable Goal which the knowers of the Vedas declare; those who are diligent, free from attachment, and aspire for it, enter into it. People practice celibacy for it.
Verse 12

Meaning: He who departs, leaving the body while uttering the single syllable, "Om," which is Brahman, and thinking of Me, attains the supreme Goal.

Meaning: O son of Prtha, to that yogi of constant concentration and single-minded attention, who remembers Me uninterruptedly and for a long time, I am easily attained.
Verse 15

Meaning: As a result of reaching Me, the exalted ones who have attained the highest perfection do not get rebirth, which is an abode of sorrows and is impermanent.

Meaning: O Arjuna, all the worlds together with the world of Brahma are subject to return. But, O son of Kunti, there is no return after reaching Me.
Verse 17

रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः ॥ १७॥
సహస్రయుగపర్యన్తమహర్యద్బ్రహ్మణో విదుః ।
రాత్రిం యుగసహస్రాన్తాం తేఽహోరాత్రవిదో జనాః ॥ ౧౭॥
sahasra-yuga-paryantam ahar yad brahmaṇo viduḥ |
rātriṁ yuga-sahasrāntāṁ te ’ho-rātra-vido janāḥ || 17 ||
Words: sahasra (one thousand), yuga (age), paryantam (until), ahaḥ (one day), yat (which), brahmaṇaḥ (of Brahma), viduḥ (know), rātrim (night), yuga-sahasra-antām (lasts one thousand yugas), te (they), ahaḥ-rātra-vidaḥ (those who know his day and night), janāḥ (people)
Meaning: Those people who know what day and night are, know the day of Brahma which ends in a thousand yugas, and His night which also ends in a thousand yugas.
Verse 18
अव्यक्ताद्व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे ।
रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके ॥ १८॥
అవ్యక్తాత్ వ్యక్తయః సర్వాః ప్రభవన్త్యహరాగమే ।
రాత్ర్యాగమే ప్రలీయన్తే తత్రైవావ్యక్తసంజ్ఞకే ॥ ౧౮॥
avyaktād vyaktayaḥ sarvāḥ prabhavantyahar-āgame |
rātryāgame pralīyante tatraivāvyakta-sanjñake || 18 ||
Words: avyaktāt (from the unmanifested), vyaktayaḥ (the manifested), sarvāḥ (all), prabhavanti (emanate), ahaḥ-āgame (at the advent of Brahma’s day), rātri-āgame (at the fall of Brahma’s night), pralīyante (they dissolve), tatra (into that), eva (certainly), avyakta-sanjñake (in that which is called the unmanifest)
Meaning: With the coming of day, all manifested things emerge from the Unmanifest, and when night comes, they merge into that which is called the Unmanifested.
Verse 19
भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते ।
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे ॥ १९॥
భూతగ్రామః స ఏవాయం భూత్వా భూత్వా ప్రలీయతే ।
రాత్ర్యాగమేఽవశః పార్థ ప్రభవత్యహరాగమే ॥ ౧౯॥
bhūta-grāmaḥ sa evāyaṁ bhūtvā bhūtvā pralīyate |
rātryāgame ’vaśaḥ pārtha prabhavatyahar-āgame || 19 ||
Words: bhūta-grāmaḥ (the multitude of beings), saḥ (these), eva (certainly), ayam (this), bhūtvā bhūtvā (repeatedly taking birth), pralīyate (dissolves), rātri-āgame (with the advent of night), avaśaḥ (helpless), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), prabhavati (become manifest), ahaḥ-āgame (with the advent of day)
Meaning: O son of Prtha, after being born again and again, that very multitude of beings disappears of its own accord at the approach of night. It comes to life again at the approach of day.
Verse 20
परस्तस्मात्तु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः ।
यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नश्यत्सु न विनश्यति ॥ २०॥
పరస్తస్మాత్తు భావోఽన్యోఽవ్యక్తోఽవ్యక్తాత్సనాతనః ।
యః స సర్వేషు భూతేషు నశ్యత్సు న వినశ్యతి ॥ ౨౦॥
paras tasmāt tu bhāvo ’nyo ’vyakto ’vyaktāt sanātanaḥ |
yaḥ sa sarveṣu bhūteṣu naśyatsu na vinaśyati || 20 ||
Words: paraḥ (transcendental), tasmāt (than that), tu (but), bhāvaḥ (creation), anyaḥ (another), avyaktaḥ (unmanifest), avyaktāt (to the unmanifest), sanātanaḥ (eternal), yaḥ (who), saḥ (that), sarveṣu (all), bhūteṣu (in beings), naśyatsu (cease to exist), na (never), vinaśyati (is annihilated)
Meaning: But distinct from that Unmanifested is the other eternal, unmanifest Reality, who does not get destroyed when all beings are destroyed.
Verse 21
अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् ।
यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम ॥ २१॥
అవ్యక్తః అక్షర ఇత్యుక్తస్తమాహుః పరమాం గతిమ్ ।
యం ప్రాప్య న నివర్తన్తే తద్ధామ పరమం మమ ॥ ౨౧॥
avyakto ’kṣara ityuktas tam āhuḥ paramāṁ gatim |
yaṁ prāpya na nivartante tad dhāma paramaṁ mama || 21 ||
Words: avyaktaḥ (unmanifest), akṣaraḥ (imperishable), iti (thus), uktaḥ (is said), tam (that), āhuḥ (is called), paramām (the supreme), gatim (destination), yam (which), prāpya (having reached), na (never), nivartante (come back), tat (that), dhāma (abode), paramam (the supreme), mama (my)
Meaning: They call Him who has been mentioned as the Unmanifested, the Immutable, the supreme Goal. That is the supreme abode of Mine, from which they do not return once they reach it.
Verse 22
पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया ।
यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ॥ २२॥
పురుషః స పరః పార్థ భక్త్యా లభ్యస్త్వనన్యయా ।
యస్యాన్తఃస్థాని భూతాని యేన సర్వమిదం తతమ్ ॥ ౨౨॥
puruṣaḥ sa paraḥ pārtha bhaktyā labhyas tvananyayā |
yasyāntaḥ-sthāni bhūtāni yena sarvam idaṁ tatam || 22 ||
Words: puruṣaḥ (the Supreme Divine Personality), saḥ (he), paraḥ (greatest), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), bhaktyā (through devotion), labhyaḥ (is attainable), tu (indeed), ananyayā (without another), yasya (of whom), antaḥ-sthāni (situated within), bhūtāni (beings), yena (by whom), sarvam (all), idam (this), tatam (is pervaded)
Meaning: O son of Prtha, that supreme Person—in whom all beings are included and by whom all this is pervaded—is, indeed, reached through one-pointed devotion.
Verse 23
यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः ।
प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ ॥ २३॥
యత్ర కాలే త్వనావృత్తిమావృత్తిం చైవ యోగినః ।
ప్రయాతా యాన్తి తం కాలం వక్ష్యామి భరత-ఋషభ ॥ ౨౩॥
yatra kāle tvanāvṛttim āvṛttiṁ chaiva yoginaḥ |
prayātā yānti taṁ kālaṁ vakṣyāmi bharatarṣabha || 23 ||
Words: yatra (where), kāle (time), tu (certainly), anāvṛttim (no return), āvṛttim (return), cha (and), eva (certainly), yoginaḥ (a yogi), prayātāḥ (having departed), yānti (attain), tam (that), kālam (time), vakṣyāmi (I shall describe), bharata-ṛṣabha (Arjun, the best of the Bharatas)
Meaning: O best of the Bharata dynasty, I shall now speak of that time by which the yogis depart and attain the State of Non-return, and also the State of Return.
Verse 24
अग्निर्ज्योतिरहः शुक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् ।
तत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः ॥ २४॥
అగ్నిర్జ్యోతిరహః శుక్లః షణ్మాసా ఉత్తరాయణమ్ ।
తత్ర ప్రయాతా గచ్ఛన్తి బ్రహ్మ బ్రహ్మవిదో జనాః ॥ ౨౪॥
agnir jyotir ahaḥ śuklaḥ ṣaṇ-māsā uttarāyaṇam |
tatra prayātā gachchhanti brahma brahma-vido janāḥ || 24 ||
Words: agniḥ (fire), jyotiḥ (light), ahaḥ (day), śuklaḥ (the bright fortnight of the moon), ṣaṭ-māsāḥ (six months), uttara-ayanam (the sun’s northern course), tatra (there), prayātāḥ (departed), gachchhanti (go), brahma (Brahman), brahma-vidaḥ (those who know the Brahman), janāḥ (persons)
Meaning: Fire, light, daytime, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern solstice—by following this path, persons who are knowers of Brahman attain Brahman when they die.
Verse 25
धूमो रात्रिस्तथा कृष्णः षण्मासा दक्षिणायनम् ।
तत्र चान्द्रमसं ज्योतिर्योगी प्राप्य निवर्तते ॥ २५॥
ధూమో రాత్రిస్తథా కృష్ణః షణ్మాసా దక్షిణాయనమ్ ।
తత్ర చాన్ద్రమసం జ్యోతిర్యోగీ ప్రాప్య నివర్తతే ॥ ౨౫॥
dhūmo rātris tathā kṛṣṇaḥ ṣaṇ-māsā dakṣiṇāyanam |
tatra chāndramasaṁ jyotir yogī prāpya nivartate || 25 ||
Words: dhūmaḥ (smoke), rātriḥ (night), tathā (and), kṛṣṇaḥ (the dark fortnight of the moon), ṣaṭ-māsāḥ (six months), dakṣiṇa-ayanam (the sun’s southern course), tatra (there), chāndra-masam (lunar), jyotiḥ (light), yogī (a yogi), prāpya (attain), nivartate (comes back)
Meaning: Following this Path, the yogi, having reached the lunar light, returns, smoke, night, as also the dark fortnight and the six months of the Southern solstice.
Verse 26
शुक्लकृष्णे गती ह्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते ।
एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययाऽऽवर्तते पुनः ॥ २६॥
శుక్ల-కృష్ణే గతీ హ్యేతే జగతః శాశ్వతే మతే ।
ఏకయా యాత్యనావృత్తిమన్యయాఽఽవర్తతే పునః ॥ ౨౬॥
śukla-kṛṣṇe gatī hyete jagataḥ śāśvate mate |
ekayā yātyanāvṛttim anyayāvartate punaḥ || 26 ||
Words: śukla (bright), kṛṣṇe (dark), gatī (paths), hi (certainly), ete (these), jagataḥ (of the material world), śāśvate (eternal), mate (opinion), ekayā (by one), yāti (goes), anāvṛttim (to non return), anyayā (by the other), āvartate (comes back), punaḥ (again)
Meaning: These two paths of the world, which are white and black, are indeed considered eternal. Through one, a person goes to the state of non-return; through the other, they return again.
Verse 27
नैते सृती पार्थ जानन्योगी मुह्यति कश्चन ।
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु योगयुक्तो भवार्जुन ॥ २७॥
నైతే సృతీ పార్థ జానన్యోగీ ముహ్యతి కశ్చన ।
తస్మాత్సర్వేషు కాలేషు యోగ-యుక్తో భవార్జున ॥ ౨౭॥
naite sṛtī pārtha jānan yogī muhyati kaśchana |
tasmāt sarveṣu kāleṣu yoga-yukto bhavārjuna || 27 ||
Words: na (never), ete (these two), sṛtī (paths), pārtha (Arjun, the son of Pritha), jānan (knowing), yogī (a yogi), muhyati (bewildered), kaśchana (any), tasmāt (therefore), sarveṣu kāleṣu (always), yoga-yuktaḥ (situated in Yog), bhava (be), arjuna (Arjun)
Meaning: O son of Prtha, no yogi—one steadfast in meditation—whosoever has known these two courses becomes deluded. Therefore, O Arjuna, be steadfast in yoga at all times.
Verse 28
वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपःसु चैव दानेषु यत्पुण्यफलं प्रदिष्टम् ।
अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम् ॥ २८॥
వేదేషు యజ్ఞేషు తపఃసు చైవ దానేషు యత్పుణ్యఫలం ప్రదిష్టమ్ ।
అత్యేతి తత్సర్వమిదం విదిత్వా యోగీ పరం స్థానముపైతి చాద్యమ్ ॥ ౨౮॥
vedeṣu yajñeṣu tapaḥsu chaiva |
dāneṣu yat puṇya-phalaṁ pradiṣṭam atyeti tat sarvam idaṁ viditvā yogī paraṁ sthānam upaiti chādyam || 28 ||
Words: vedeṣu (in the study of the Vedas), yajñeṣu (in performance of sacrifices), tapaḥsu (in austerities), cha (and), eva (certainly), dāneṣu (in giving charities), yat (which), puṇya-phalam (fruit of merit), pradiṣṭam (is gained), atyeti (surpasses), tat sarvam (all), idam (this), viditvā (having known), yogī (a yogi), param (Supreme), sthānam (Abode), upaiti (achieves), cha (and), ādyam (original)
Meaning: Having known this, the yogi transcends all the results of righteous deeds declared in the Vedas, sacrifices, austerities, and charities, and reaches the primordial, supreme state.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे अक्षरब्रह्मयोगो नाम अष्टमोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the eighth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Akṣara Brahma Yoga — the Yoga of the Imperishable Brahman.
