Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 11

अध्यायः ११ · विश्वरूपदर्शनयोगः — The Yoga of the Vision of the Cosmic Form

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 55 verses.

Arjuna asks to see Krishna’s divine form. Granted divine sight, he beholds the cosmic Viśvarūpa — infinite, dazzling, and terrible — and learns that Time itself is devouring the warriors. Awestruck, he begs to see again the gentle four-armed form; Krishna says this vision is rare, and that He is reached by single-minded devotion.

Meaning: And that You have been discourteously treated out of fun—while walking, while on a bed, while on a seat, while eating, in privacy, or, O Acyuta, even in public—for that, I beg pardon of You, the incomprehensible One.

Verse 47
