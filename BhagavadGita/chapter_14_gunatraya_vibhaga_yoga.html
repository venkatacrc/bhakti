Bhagavad Gītā — Chapter 14

अध्यायः १४ · गुणत्रयविभागयोगः — The Yoga of Distinguishing the Three Guṇas

Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with word-by-word and full meaning. 27 verses.

Krishna teaches the three guṇas — sattva, rajas, and tamas — how they bind the soul through knowledge, attachment, and ignorance, their signs in life and at death, and the marks of one who has transcended them. Crossing beyond the guṇas through unswerving devotion, the devotee becomes fit for Brahman.

The Twenty-Seven Verses

Verse 1
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
परं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि ज्ञानानां ज्ञानमुत्तमम् ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः ॥ १॥
పరం భూయః ప్రవక్ష్యామి జ్ఞానానాం జ్ఞానమ్ ఉత్తమమ్ ।
యజ్జ్ఞాత్వా మునయః సర్వే పరాం సిద్ధిమితో గతాః ॥ ౧॥
paraṁ bhūyaḥ pravakṣyāmi jñānānāṁ jñānam uttamam |
yaj jñātvā munayaḥ sarve parāṁ siddhim ito gatāḥ || 1 ||
Words: param (supreme), bhūyaḥ (again), pravakṣyāmi (I shall explain), jñānānām (of all knowledge), jñānam uttamam (the supreme wisdom), yat (which), jñātvā (knowing), munayaḥ (saints), sarve (all), parām (highest), siddhim (perfection), itaḥ (through this), gatāḥ (attained)
Meaning: I shall speak again of the supreme knowledge, the best of all knowledge, by realizing which all the contemplatives have reached the highest perfection from here."
Verse 2
इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रित्य मम साधर्म्यमागताः ।
सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च ॥ २॥
ఇదం జ్ఞానముపాశ్రిత్య మమ సాధర్మ్యమాగతాః ।
సర్గేఽపి నోపజాయన్తే ప్రలయే న వ్యథన్తి చ ॥ ౨॥
idaṁ jñānam upāśritya mama sādharmyam āgatāḥ |
sarge ’pi nopajāyante pralaye na vyathanti cha || 2 ||
Words: idam (this), jñānam (wisdom), upāśritya (take refuge in), mama (mine), sādharmyam (of similar nature), āgatāḥ (having attained), sarge (at the time of creation), api (even), na (not), upajāyante (are born), pralaye (at the time of dissolution), na-vyathanti (they will not experience misery), cha (and)
Meaning: Those who attain identity with Me by resorting to this Knowledge are not born even during creation, nor do they suffer pain during dissolution.
Verse 3
मम योनिर्महद्ब्रह्म तस्मिन् गर्भं दधाम्यहम् ।
संभवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत ॥ ३॥
మమ యోనిర్మహద్బ్రహ్మ తస్మిన్ గర్భం దధామ్యహమ్ ।
సంభవః సర్వభూతానాం తతో భవతి భారత ॥ ౩॥
mama yonir mahad brahma tasmin garbhaṁ dadhāmy aham |
sambhavaḥ sarva-bhūtānāṁ tato bhavati bhārata || 3 ||
Words: mama (my), yoniḥ (womb), mahat brahma (the total material substance, prakṛiti), tasmin (in that), garbham (womb), dadhāmi (impregnate), aham (I), sambhavaḥ (birth), sarva-bhūtānām (of all living beings), tataḥ (thereby), bhavati (becomes), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat)
Meaning: My womb is the great sustainer. In it, I place the seed. From that, O scion of the Bharata dynasty, all things are born.
Verse 4
सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः सम्भवन्ति याः ।
तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता ॥ ४॥
సర్వయోనిషు కౌన్తేయ మూర్తయః సమ్భవన్తి యాః ।
తాసాం బ్రహ్మ మహద్యోనిరహం బీజప్రదః పితా ॥ ౪॥
sarva-yoniṣu kaunteya mūrtayaḥ sambhavanti yāḥ |
tāsāṁ brahma mahad yonir ahaṁ bīja-pradaḥ pitā || 4 ||
Words: sarva (all), yoniṣu (species of life), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), mūrtayaḥ (forms), sambhavanti (are produced), yāḥ (which), tāsām (of all of them), brahma-mahat (great material nature), yoniḥ (womb), aham (I), bīja-pradaḥ (seed-giving), pitā (Father)
Meaning: O son of Kunti, whatever forms are born from all the wombs, of them the great sustainer is the womb; I am the father who deposits the seed.
Verse 5
सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसंभवाः ।
निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥ ५॥
సత్త్వం రజః తమ ఇతి గుణాః ప్రకృతిసంభవాః ।
నిబధ్నన్తి మహా-బాహో దేహే దేహినమవ్యయమ్ ॥ ౫॥
sattvaṁ rajas tama iti guṇāḥ prakṛti-sambhavāḥ |
nibadhnanti mahā-bāho dehe dehinam avyayam || 5 ||
Words: sattvam (mode of goodness), rajaḥ (mode of passion), tamaḥ (mode of ignorance), iti (thus), guṇāḥ (modes), prakṛti (material nature), sambhavāḥ (consists of), nibadhnanti (bind), mahā-bāho (mighty-armed one), dehe (in the body), dehinam (the embodied soul), avyayam (eternal)
Meaning: O mighty-armed one, the qualities, viz. sattva, rajas, and tamas, born of Nature, are the immutable embodiments belonging to the body.
Verse 6
तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् ।
सुखसङ्गेन बध्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ ॥ ६॥
తత్ర సత్త్వం నిర్మలత్వాత్ప్రకాశకమనామయమ్ ।
సుఖసఙ్గేన బధ్నాతి జ్ఞానసఙ్గేన చానఘ ॥ ౬॥
tatra sattvaṁ nirmalatvāt prakāśakam anāmayam |
sukha-saṅgena badhnāti jñāna-saṅgena chānagha || 6 ||
Words: tatra (amongst these), sattvam (mode of goodness), nirmalatvāt (being purest), prakāśakam (illuminating), anāmayam (healthy and full of well-being), sukha (happiness), saṅgena (attachment), badhnāti (binds), jñāna (knowledge), saṅgena (attachment), cha (also), anagha (Arjun, the sinless one)
Meaning: Among them, sattva, being pure and nirmala (pure-transparent, i.e., capable of resisting any form of ignorance, and hence an illuminator, i.e. a revealer of Consciousness), is harmless. O sinless one, it binds through attachment to happiness and attachment to knowledge.
Verse 7
रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् ।
तन्निबध्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम् ॥ ७॥
రజో రాగాత్మకం విద్ధి తృష్ణాసఙ్గసముద్భవమ్ ।
తన్నిబధ్నాతి కౌన్తేయ కర్మసఙ్గేన దేహినమ్ ॥ ౭॥
rajo rāgātmakaṁ viddhi tṛṣṇā-saṅga-samudbhavam |
tan nibadhnāti kaunteya karma-saṅgena dehinam || 7 ||
Words: rajaḥ (mode of passion), rāga-ātmakam (of the nature of passion), viddhi (know), tṛṣṇā (desires), saṅga (association), samudbhavam (arises from), tat (that), nibadhnāti (binds), kaunteya (Arjun, the son of Kunti), karma-saṅgena (through attachment to fruitive actions), dehinam (the embodied soul)
Meaning: Know rajas to be of the nature of passion, born of hankering and attachment. O son of Kunti, it binds the embodied one through attachment to action.
Verse 8
तमस्त्वज्ञानजं विद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम् ।
प्रमादालस्यनिद्राभिस्तन्निबध्नाति भारत ॥ ८॥
తమస్త్వజ్ఞానజం విద్ధి మోహనం సర్వదేహినామ్ ।
ప్రమాదాలస్యనిద్రాభిస్తన్నిబధ్నాతి భారత ॥ ౮॥
tamas tv ajñāna-jaṁ viddhi mohanaṁ sarva-dehinām |
pramādālasya-nidrābhis tan nibadhnāti bhārata || 8 ||
Words: tamaḥ (mode of ignorance), tu (but), ajñāna-jam (born of ignorance), viddhi (know), mohanam (illusion), sarva-dehinām (for all the embodied souls), pramāda (negligence), ālasya (laziness), nidrābhiḥ (and sleep), tat (that), nibadhnāti (binds), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat)
Meaning: On the other hand, know tamas, which deludes all embodied beings, to be born of ignorance. O scion of the Bharata dynasty, it binds through inadvertence, laziness, and sleep.
Verse 9
सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति रजः कर्मणि भारत ।
ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे सञ्जयत्युत ॥ ९॥
సత్త్వం సుఖే సఞ్జయతి రజః కర్మణి భారత ।
జ్ఞానమావృత్య తు తమః ప్రమాదే సఞ్జయత్యుత ॥ ౯॥
sattvaṁ sukhe sañjayati rajaḥ karmaṇi bhārata |
jñānam āvṛtya tu tamaḥ pramāde sañjayaty uta || 9 ||
Words: sattvam (mode of goodness), sukhe (to happiness), sañjayati (binds), rajaḥ (mode of passion), karmaṇi (toward actions), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat), jñānam (wisdom), āvṛtya (clouds), tu (but), tamaḥ (mode of ignorance), pramāde (to delusion), sañjayati (binds), uta (indeed)
Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, sattva attaches one to happiness, rajas to action, while tamas, obscuring knowledge, leads to inadvertence as well.
Verse 10
रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत ।
रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा ॥ १०॥
రజస్తమశ్చాభిభూయ సత్త్వం భవతి భారత ।
రజః సత్త్వం తమశ్చైవ తమః సత్త్వం రజస్తథా ॥ ౧౦॥
rajas tamaś chābhibhūya sattvaṁ bhavati bhārata |
rajaḥ sattvaṁ tamaś chaiva tamaḥ sattvaṁ rajas tathā || 10 ||
Words: rajaḥ (mode of passion), tamaḥ (mode of ignorance), cha (and), abhibhūya (prevails), sattvam (mode of goodness), bhavati (becomes), bhārata (Arjun, the son of Bharat), rajaḥ (mode of passion), sattvam (mode of goodness), tamaḥ (mode of ignorance), cha (and), eva (indeed), tamaḥ (mode of ignorance), sattvam (mode of goodness), rajaḥ (mode of passion), tathā (also)
Meaning: O scion of the Bharata dynasty, sattva increases by subduing rajas and tamas; rajas increases by overpowering sattva and tamas; and tamas increases by dominating over sattva and rajas.
Verse 11
सर्वद्वारेषु देहेऽस्मिन्प्रकाश उपजायते ।
ज्ञानं यदा तदा विद्याद्विवृद्धं सत्त्वमित्युत ॥ ११॥
సర్వద్వారేషు దేహేఽస్మిన్ప్రకాశ ఉపజాయతే ।
జ్ఞానం యదా తదా విద్యాద్వివృద్ధం సత్త్వమిత్యుత ॥ ౧౧॥
sarva-dvāreṣu dehe ’smin prakāśa upajāyate |
jñānaṁ yadā tadā vidyād vivṛddhaṁ sattvam ity uta || 11 ||
Words: sarva (all), dvāreṣu (through the gates), dehe (body), asmin (in this), prakāśaḥ (illumination), upajāyate (manifest), jñānam (knowledge), yadā (when), tadā (then), vidyāt (know), vivṛddham (predominates), sattvam (mode of goodness), iti (thus), uta (certainly)
Meaning: When the illumination of knowledge radiates through all the doors of the senses in this body, then one should know that sattva has greatly increased.
Verse 12
लोभः प्रवृत्तिरारम्भः कर्मणामशमः स्पृहा ।
रजस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे भरतर्षभ ॥ १२॥
లోభః ప్రవృత్తిరారమ్భః కర్మణామశమః స్పృహా ।
రజస్యేతాని జాయన్తే వివృద్ధే భరత-ఋషభ ॥ ౧౨॥
lobhaḥ pravṛttir ārambhaḥ karmaṇām aśamaḥ spṛhā |
rajasy etāni jāyante vivṛddhe bharatarṣabha || 12 ||
Words: lobhaḥ (greed), pravṛttiḥ (activity), ārambhaḥ (exertion), karmaṇām (for fruitive actions), aśamaḥ (restlessness), spṛhā (craving), rajasi (of the mode of passion), etāni (these), jāyante (develop), vivṛddhe (when predominates), bharata-ṛṣabha (the best of the Bharatas, Arjun)
Meaning: O best of the Bharata dynasty, when rajas becomes predominant, these come into being: avarice, restlessness, undertaking of actions, agitation, and craving.
Verse 13
अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च ।
तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे कुरुनन्दन ॥ १३॥
అప్రకాశోఽప్రవృత్తిశ్చ ప్రమాదో మోహ ఏవ చ ।
తమస్యేతాని జాయన్తే వివృద్ధే కురు-నన్దన ॥ ౧౩॥
aprakāśo ’pravṛttiś cha pramādo moha eva cha |
tamasy etāni jāyante vivṛddhe kuru-nandana || 13 ||
Words: aprakāśaḥ (nescience), apravṛttiḥ (inertia), cha (and), pramādaḥ (negligence), mohaḥ (delusion), eva (indeed), cha (also), tamasi (mode of ignorance), etāni (these), jāyante (manifest), vivṛddhe (when dominates), kuru-nandana (the joy of the Kurus, Arjun)
Meaning: O descendant of the Kuru dynasty, when tamas predominates, these come into being without exception: non-discrimination, inactivity, inadvertence, and delusion.
Verse 14
यदा सत्त्वे प्रवृद्धे तु प्रलयं याति देहभृत् ।
तदोत्तमविदां लोकानमलान्प्रतिपद्यते ॥ १४॥
యదా సత్త్వే ప్రవృద్ధే తు ప్రలయం యాతి దేహభృత్ ।
తదోత్తమవిదాం లోకానమలాన్ప్రతిపద్యతే ॥ ౧౪॥
yadā sattve pravṛddhe tu pralayaṁ yāti deha-bhṛt |
tadottama-vidāṁ lokān amalān pratipadyate || 14 ||
Words: yadā (when), sattve (in the mode of goodness), pravṛddhe (when premodinates), tu (indeed), pralayam (death), yāti (reach), deha-bhṛt (the embodied), tadā (then), uttama-vidām (of the learned), lokān (abodes), amalān (pure), pratipadyate (attains)
Meaning: When an embodied one undergoes death while sattva is exclusively predominant, then he attains the untainted worlds of those who know the highest (entities).
Verse 15
रजसि प्रलयं गत्वा कर्मसङ्गिषु जायते ।
तथा प्रलीनस्तमसि मूढयोनिषु जायते ॥ १५॥
రజసి ప్రలయం గత్వా కర్మసఙ్గిషు జాయతే ।
తథా ప్రలీనస్తమసి మూఢయోనిషు జాయతే ॥ ౧౫॥
rajasi pralayaṁ gatvā karma-saṅgiṣu jāyate |
tathā pralīnas tamasi mūḍha-yoniṣu jāyate || 15 ||
Words: rajasi (in the mode of passion), pralayam (death), gatvā (attaining), karma-saṅgiṣu (among people driven by work), jāyate (are born), tathā (likewise), pralīnaḥ (dying), tamasi (in the mode of ignorance), mūḍha-yoniṣu (in the animal kingdom), jāyate (takes birth)
Meaning: When one dies while rajas predominates, they are born among people attached to activity. Similarly, when one dies while tamas predominates, they take birth among the stupid species.
Verse 16
कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम् ।
रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम् ॥ १६॥
కర్మణః సుకృతస్యాహుః సాత్త్వికం నిర్మలం ఫలమ్ ।
రజసస్తు ఫలం దుఃఖమజ్ఞానం తమసః ఫలమ్ ॥ ౧౬॥
karmaṇaḥ sukṛtasyāhuḥ sāttvikaṁ nirmalaṁ phalam |
rajasas tu phalaṁ duḥkham ajñānaṁ tamasaḥ phalam || 16 ||
Words: karmaṇaḥ (of action), su-kṛtasya (pure), āhuḥ (is said), sāttvikam (mode of goodness), nirmalam (pure), phalam (result), rajasaḥ (mode of passion), tu (indeed), phalam (result), duḥkham (pain), ajñānam (ignorance), tamasaḥ (mode of ignorance), phalam (result)
Meaning: They say that the result of good work is pure and is born of Sattva. But the result of Rajas is sorrow; the result of Tamas is ignorance.
Verse 17
सत्त्वात्सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च ।
प्रमादमोहौ तमसो भवतोऽज्ञानमेव च ॥ १७॥
సత్త్వాత్సఞ్జాయతే జ్ఞానం రజసో లోభ ఏవ చ ।
ప్రమాదమోహౌ తమసో భవతోఽజ్ఞానమేవ చ ॥ ౧౭॥
sattvāt sañjāyate jñānaṁ rajaso lobha eva cha |
pramāda-mohau tamaso bhavato ’jñānam eva cha || 17 ||
Words: sattvāt (from the mode of goodness), sañjāyate (arises), jñānam (knowledge), rajasaḥ (from the mode of passion), lobhaḥ (greed), eva (indeed), cha (and), pramāda (negligence), mohau (delusion), tamasaḥ (from the mode of ignorance), bhavataḥ (arise), ajñānam (ignorance), eva (indeed), cha (and)
Meaning: From sattva is born knowledge, and from rajas, avarice indeed. From tamas are born inadvertence, delusion, and ignorance, to be sure.
Verse 18
ऊर्ध्वं गच्छन्ति सत्त्वस्था मध्ये तिष्ठन्ति राजसाः ।
जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अधो गच्छन्ति तामसाः ॥ १८॥
ఊర్ధ్వం గచ్ఛన్తి సత్త్వస్థా మధ్యే తిష్ఠన్తి రాజసాః ।
జఘన్యగుణవృత్తిస్థా అధో గచ్ఛన్తి తామసాః ॥ ౧౮॥
ūrdhvaṁ gachchhanti sattva-sthā madhye tiṣṭhanti rājasāḥ |
jaghanya-guṇa-vṛtti-sthā adho gachchhanti tāmasāḥ || 18 ||
Words: ūrdhvam (upward), gachchhanti (rise), sattva-sthāḥ (those situated in the mode of goodness), madhye (in the middle), tiṣṭhanti (stay), rājasāḥ (those in the mode of passion), jaghanya (abominable), guṇa (quality), vṛtti-sthāḥ (engaged in activities), adhaḥ (down), gachchhanti (go), tāmasāḥ (those in the mode of ignorance)
Meaning: People who conform to sattva go higher up; those who conform to rajas stay in the middle; those who conform to tamas, who conform to the actions of the lowest quality, go down.
Verse 19
नान्यं गुणेभ्यः कर्तारं यदा द्रष्टानुपश्यति ।
गुणेभ्यश्च परं वेत्ति मद्भावं सोऽधिगच्छति ॥ १९॥
నాన్యం గుణేభ్యః కర్తారం యదా ద్రష్టానుపశ్యతి ।
గుణేభ్యః చ పరం వేత్తి మద్భావం సోఽధిగచ్ఛతి ॥ ౧౯॥
nānyaṁ guṇebhyaḥ kartāraṁ yadā draṣṭānupaśyati |
guṇebhyaś cha paraṁ vetti mad-bhāvaṁ so ’dhigachchhati || 19 ||
Words: na (no), anyam (other), guṇebhyaḥ (of the guṇas), kartāram (agents of action), yadā (when), draṣṭā (the seer), anupaśyati (see), guṇebhyaḥ (to the modes of nature), cha (and), param (transcendental), vetti (know), mat-bhāvam (my divine nature), saḥ (they), adhigachchhati (attain)
Meaning: When the witness sees none other than the qualities as the agent, and knows that which is superior to the qualities, he attains My nature.
Verse 20
गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान् ।
जन्ममृत्युजरादुःखैर्विमुक्तोऽमृतमश्नुते ॥ २०॥
గుణానేతానతీత్య త్రీన్దేహీ దేహసముద్భవాన్ ।
జన్మమృత్యుజరాదుఃఖైర్విముక్తోఽమృతమశ్నుతే ॥ ౨౦॥
guṇān etān atītya trīn dehī deha-samudbhavān |
janma-mṛtyu-jarā-duḥkhair vimukto ’mṛtam aśnute || 20 ||
Words: guṇān (the three modes of material nature), etān (these), atītya (transcending), trīn (three), dehī (the embodied), deha (body), samudbhavān (produced of), janma (birth), mṛtyu (death), jarā (old age), duḥkhaiḥ (misery), vimuktaḥ (freed from), amṛtam (immortality), aśnute (attains)
Meaning: Having transcended these three qualities, which are the origin of the body, the embodied one becomes free from birth, death, old age, and sorrows, and experiences Immortality.
Verse 21
अर्जुन उवाच · arjuna uvāca
कैर्लिंगैस्त्रीन्गुणानेतानतीतो भवति प्रभो ।
किमाचारः कथं चैतांस्त्रीन्गुणानतिवर्तते ॥ २१॥
కైర్లింగైస్త్రీన్గుణానేతానతీతో భవతి ప్రభో ।
కిమాచారః కథం చైతాంస్త్రీన్గుణానతివర్తతే ॥ ౨౧॥
kair liṅgais trīn guṇān etān atīto bhavati prabho |
kim āchāraḥ kathaṁ chaitāns trīn guṇān ativartate || 21 ||
Words: kaiḥ (by what), liṅgaiḥ (symptoms), trīn (three), guṇān (modes of material nature), etān (these), atītaḥ (having transcended), bhavati (is), prabho (Lord), kim (what), āchāraḥ (conduct), katham (how), cha (and), etān (these), trīn (three), guṇān (modes of material nature), ativartate (transcend)
Meaning: O Lord, by what signs is one known who has gone beyond these three qualities? What is their behavior, and how do they transcend these three qualities?"
Verse 22
श्रीभगवानुवाच · śrī-bhagavān uvāca
प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव ।
न द्वेष्टि सम्प्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति ॥ २२॥
ప్రకాశం చ ప్రవృత్తిం చ మోహమేవ చ పాణ్డవ ।
న ద్వేష్టి సమ్ప్రవృత్తాని న నివృత్తాని కాఙ్క్షతి ॥ ౨౨॥
prakāśaṁ cha pravṛttiṁ cha moham eva cha pāṇḍava |
na dveṣṭi sampravṛttāni na nivṛttāni kāṅkṣati || 22 ||
Words: prakāśam (illumination), cha (and), pravṛttim (activity), cha (and), moham (delusion), eva (even), cha (and), pāṇḍava (Arjun, the son of Pandu), na dveṣṭi (do not hate), sampravṛttāni (when present), na (nor), nivṛttāni (when absent), kāṅkṣati (longs)
Meaning: O son of Pandu, he neither dislikes illumination (knowledge), activity, nor delusion when they appear, nor does he long for them when they disappear."
Verse 23
उदासीनवदासीनो गुणैर्यो न विचाल्यते ।
गुणा वर्तन्त इत्येव योऽवतिष्ठति नेङ्गते ॥ २३॥
ఉదాసీనవదాసీనో గుణైర్యో న విచాల్యతే ।
గుణా వర్తన్త ఇత్యేవ యోఽవతిష్ఠతి నేఙ్గతే ॥ ౨౩॥
udāsīna-vad āsīno guṇair yo na vichālyate |
guṇā vartanta ity evaṁ yo ’vatiṣṭhati neṅgate || 23 ||
Words: udāsīna-vat (neutral), āsīnaḥ (situated), guṇaiḥ (to the modes of material nature), yaḥ (who), na (not), vichālyate (are disturbed), guṇāḥ (modes of material nature), vartante (act), iti-evam (knowing it in this way), yaḥ (who), avatiṣṭhati (established in the self), na (not), iṅgate (wavering)
Meaning: He who, sitting like one indifferent, is not distracted by the three qualities; he who, thinking that the qualities alone act, remains firm and does not move surely;
Verse 24
समदुःखसुखः स्वस्थः समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः ।
तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः ॥ २४॥
సమదుఃఖసుఖః స్వస్థః సమలోష్టాశ్మకాఞ్చనః ।
తుల్యప్రియాప్రియో ధీరస్తుల్యనిన్దాత్మసంస్తుతిః ॥ ౨౪॥
sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ sva-sthaḥ sama-loṣṭāśma-kāñchanaḥ |
tulya-priyāpriyo dhīras tulya-nindātma-sanstutiḥ || 24 ||
Words: sama (alike), duḥkha (distress), sukhaḥ (happiness), sva-sthaḥ (established in the self), sama (equally), loṣṭa (a clod), aśma (stone), kāñchanaḥ (gold), tulya (of equal value), priya (pleasant), apriyaḥ (unpleasant), dhīraḥ (steady), tulya (the same), nindā (blame), ātma-sanstutiḥ (praise)
Meaning: He to whom sorrow and happiness are alike, who is established in his own Self, to whom a lump of earth, iron, and gold are the same, to whom the agreeable and the disagreeable are the same, who is wise, to whom censure and his own praise are the same;
Verse 25
मानापमानयोस्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते ॥ २५॥
మానాపమానయోస్తుల్యస్తుల్యో మిత్రారిపక్షయోః ।
సర్వారమ్భపరిత్యాగీ గుణ-అతీతః స ఉచ్యతే ॥ ౨౫॥
mānāpamānayos tulyas tulyo mitrāri-pakṣayoḥ |
sarvārambha-parityāgī guṇātītaḥ sa uchyate || 25 ||
Words: māna (honor), apamānayoḥ (dishonor), tulyaḥ (equal), tulyaḥ (equal), mitra (friend), ari (foe), pakṣayoḥ (to the parties), sarva (all), ārambha (enterprises), parityāgī (renouncer), guṇa-atītaḥ (risen above the three modes of material nature), saḥ (they), uchyate (are said to have)
Meaning: He who is the same under honor and dishonor, who is equitably disposed towards both friends and foes, who has renounced all undertakings—he is said to have gone beyond dualities.
Verse 26
मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते ।
स गुणान्समतीत्यैतान् ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ २६॥
మాం చ యోఽవ్యభిచారేణ భక్తియోగేన సేవతే ।
స గుణాన్సమతీత్యైతాన్ బ్రహ్మ-భూయాయ కల్పతే ॥ ౨౬॥
māṁ cha yo ’vyabhichāreṇa bhakti-yogena sevate |
sa guṇān samatītyaitān brahma-bhūyāya kalpate || 26 ||
Words: mām (me), cha (only), yaḥ (who), avyabhichāreṇa (unalloyed), bhakti-yogena (through devotion), sevate (serve), saḥ (they), guṇān (the three modes of material nature), samatītya (rise above), etān (these), brahma-bhūyāya (level of Brahman), kalpate (comes to)
Meaning: And he who serves Me through the unswerving Yoga of Devotion, he, having gone beyond these qualities, qualifies for becoming Brahman.
Verse 27
ब्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाऽहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च ।
शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्यैकान्तिकस्य च ॥ २७॥
బ్రహ్మణో హి ప్రతిష్ఠాఽహమమృతస్యావ్యయస్య చ ।
శాశ్వతస్య చ ధర్మస్య సుఖస్యైకాన్తికస్య చ ॥ ౨౭॥
brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāham amṛtasyāvyayasya cha |
śāśvatasya cha dharmasya sukhasyaikāntikasya cha || 27 ||
Words: brahmaṇaḥ (of Brahman), hi (only), pratiṣṭhā (the basis), aham (I), amṛtasya (of the immortal), avyayasya (of the imperishable), cha (and), śāśvatasya (of the eternal), cha (and), dharmasya (of the dharma), sukhasya (of bliss), aikāntikasya (unending), cha (and)
Meaning: For I am the Abode of Brahman—the indestructible, immutable, eternal, Dharma, and absolute Bliss.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे गुणत्रयविभागयोगो नाम चतुर्दशोऽध्यायः ॥
Thus ends the fourteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gītā, entitled Guṇatraya Vibhāga Yoga — the Yoga of Distinguishing the Three Guṇas.
