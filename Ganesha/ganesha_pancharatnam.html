Ganesha Pancharatnam

श्री गणेश पञ्चरत्नम् — Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with Meaning.

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya

The Five Gems

Verse 1
मुदाकरात्तमोदकं सदा विमुक्तिसाधकं कलाधरावतंसकं विलासिलोकरक्षकम् ।
अनायकैकनायकं विनाशितेभदैत्यकं नताशुभाशुनाशकं नमामि तं विनायकम् ॥ १॥
ముదాకరాత్తమోదకం సదా విముక్తిసాధకం కలాధరావతంసకం విలాసిలోకరక్షకమ్ ।
అనాయకైకనాయకం వినాశితేభదైత్యకం నతాశుభాశునాశకం నమామి తం వినాయకమ్ ॥ ౧॥
mudaakaraatta-modakaM sadaa vimukti-saadhakam
kalaadharaavataMsakaM vilaasi-loka-rakShakam
anaayakaika-naayakaM vinaashitebha-daityakaM
nataashubhaashu-naashakaM namaami taM vinaayakam || 1 ||
Words: mudaa (joy), karaatta-modakam (holding a laddu in hand), sadaa (always), vimukti-saadhakam (bestower of liberation), kalaadhara-avataMsakam (wearing the crescent moon), vilaasi-loka-rakShakam (protector of the playful world), anaayaka-eka-naayakam (sole leader of the leaderless), vinaashita-ibha-daityakam (destroyer of the elephant-demon Gajamukhasura), nata-ashubha-ashu-naashakam (swiftly destroys the ills of those who bow), vinaayakam (Vinayaka), namaami (I bow).
Meaning: I bow to Vinayaka, who joyfully holds a laddu, who is ever the giver of liberation, wears the crescent moon, protects the playful world, is the sole leader of the leaderless, destroyed the elephant-demon, and swiftly destroys the misfortunes of those who bow to Him.
Verse 2
नतेतरातिभीकरं नवोदितार्कभास्वरं नमत्सुरारिनिर्जरं नताधिकापदुद्धरम् ।
सुरेश्वरं निधीश्वरं गजेश्वरं गणेश्वरं महेश्वरं तमाश्रये परात्परं निरन्तरम् ॥ २॥
నతేతరాతిభీకరం నవోదితార్కభాస్వరం నమత్సురారినిర్జరం నతాధికాపదుద్ధరమ్ ।
సురేశ్వరం నిధీశ్వరం గజేశ్వరం గణేశ్వరం మహేశ్వరం తమాశ్రయే పరాత్పరం నిరంతరమ్ ॥ ౨॥
natetaraati-bhiikaraM navodit-aarka-bhaasvaraM
namat-suraari-nirjaraM nataadhikaapad-uddharam
sureshvaraM nidhiishvaraM gajeshvaraM gaNeshvaraM
maheshvaraM tam-aashraye paraatparaM nirantaram || 2 ||
Words: nata-itara-ati-bhiikaram (terrifying to the enemies of devotees), nava-udita-arka-bhaasvaram (radiant as the newly risen sun), namat-suraari-nirjaram (freeing devotees who bow from decay), nata-adhika-aapad-uddharam (removes even the greatest calamities of devotees), sura-ishvaram (lord of devas), nidhi-ishvaram (lord of treasures), gaja-ishvaram (elephant-faced lord), gaNa-ishvaram (lord of the ganas), mahaa-ishvaram (great lord), aashraye (I take refuge), paraat-param (higher than the highest), nirantaram (constantly).
Meaning: I ever take refuge in Him who is terrifying to the foes of devotees, radiant as the rising sun, who removes decay and calamity for those who bow, who is Lord of the devas, of treasures, of elephants, of the ganas, the great Lord, higher than the highest.
Verse 3
समस्तलोकशंकरं निरस्तदैत्यकुञ्जरं दरेतरोदरं वरं वरेभवक्त्रमक्षरम् ।
कृपाकरं क्षमाकरं मुदाकरं यशस्करं मनस्करं नमस्कृतां नमस्करोमि भास्वरम् ॥ ३॥
సమస్తలోకశంకరం నిరస్తదైత్యకుంజరం దరేతరోదరం వరం వరేభవక్త్రమక్షరమ్ ।
కృపాకరం క్షమాకరం ముదాకరం యశస్కరం మనస్కరం నమస్కృతాం నమస్కరోమి భాస్వరమ్ ॥ ౩॥
samasta-loka-sha~NkaraM nirasta-daitya-ku~njaraM
darEtarodaraM varaM varEbha-vaktram-akSharaM
kR^ipaakaraM kShamaakaraM mudaakaraM yashaskaraM
manaskaraM namaskR^itaaM namaskaromi bhaasvaram || 3 ||
Words: samasta-loka-sha~Nkaram (bestower of good on all worlds), nirasta-daitya-ku~njaram (who cast down the demon-elephant Gajamukhasura), darEtara-udaram (large belly, fearless in nature), vareBha-vaktram (best of elephant faces), akSharam (imperishable), kR^ipaakaram (compassionate), kShamaakaram (forgiving), mudaakaram (joy-giver), yashaskaram (glory-giver), manaskaram (delighter of the mind), bhaasvaram (radiant).
Meaning: I bow to the radiant One who blesses all worlds, who cast down the demon-elephant, whose form is imperishable with a large belly and elephant face, who is compassionate, forgiving, and grants joy, glory, and mental peace to those who bow to Him.
Verse 4
अकिञ्चनार्तिमार्जनं चिरन्तनोक्तिभाजनं पुरारिपूर्वनन्दनं सुरारिगर्वचर्वणम् ।
प्रपञ्चनाशभीषणं धनञ्जयादिभूषणं कपोलदानवारणं भजे पुराणवारणम् ॥ ४॥
అకించనార్తిమార్జనం చిరంతనోక్తిభాజనం పురారిపూర్వనందనం సురారిగర్వచర్వణమ్ ।
ప్రపంచనాశభీషణం ధనంజయాదిభూషణం కపోలదానవారణం భజే పురాణవారణమ్ ॥ ౪॥
aki~nchan-aarti-maarjanaM chirantanokti-bhaajanaM
puraari-puurva-nandanaM suraari-garva-charvaNam
prapa~ncha-naasha-bhiiShaNaM dhana~njay-aadi-bhuuShaNaM
kapola-daana-vaaraNaM bhaje puraaNa-vaaraNam || 4 ||
Words: aki~nchana-aarti-maarjanam (wipes away the misery of the destitute), chirantana-ukti-bhaajanam (receptacle of eternal Vedic sayings), puraari-puurva-nandanam (first-born son of Shiva, destroyer of Tripura), suraari-garva-charvaNam (chewer of the pride of the enemies of devas), prapa~ncha-naasha-bhiiShaNam (fearsome at the dissolution of the universe), dhana~njaya-aadi-bhuuShaNam (ornament/protector of Arjuna and other warriors), kapola-daana-vaaraNam (elephant whose cheeks flow with rut-fluid), puraaNa-vaaraNam (the ancient elephant-faced One), bhaje (I worship).
Meaning: I worship the ancient elephant-faced Lord who wipes away the misery of the destitute, who is the receptacle of eternal Vedic sayings, the first-born son of Shiva, who chews up the pride of the enemies of the devas, who is fearsome at the dissolution of the universe, the ornament of Arjuna and other warriors, whose cheeks flow with the rut-fluid of a mighty elephant.
Verse 5
नितान्तकान्तदन्तकान्तिमन्तकान्तकात्मजं अचिन्त्यरूपमन्तहीनमन्तरायकृन्तनम् ।
हृदन्तरे निरन्तरं वसन्तमेव योगिनां तमेकदन्तमेव तं विचिन्तयामि सन्ततम् ॥ ५॥
నితాంతకాంతదంతకాంతిమంతకాంతకాత్మజం అచింత్యరూపమంతహీనమంతరాయకృంతనమ్ ।
హృదంతరే నిరంతరం వసంతమేవ యోగినాం తమేకదంతమేవ తం విచింతయామి సంతతమ్ ॥ ౫॥
nitaanta-kaanta-danta-kaanti-manta-kaanta-kaatmajaM
achintya-ruupam-anta-hiinam-antaraaya-kR^intanam
hR^idantare nirantaraM vasantam-eva yoginaaM
tam-eka-dantam-eva taM vichintayaami santatam || 5 ||
Words: nitaanta-kaanta-danta-kaanti (of exceedingly lustrous tusk), anta-kaanta-kaatmajam (son of Shiva, the destroyer/ender), achintya-ruupam (of inconceivable form), anta-hiinam (without end, eternal), antaraaya-kR^intanam (remover of obstacles), hR^idantare (within the heart), nirantaram (constantly), vasantam (dwelling), yoginaam (of yogis), eka-dantam (single-tusked), vichintayaami (I contemplate), santatam (always).
Meaning: I constantly contemplate that single-tusked Lord, whose tusk gleams with exceeding lustre, who is son of Shiva, of inconceivable and eternal form, remover of obstacles, and who ever dwells within the hearts of yogis.

Phalashruti

Verse 6
महागणेशपञ्चरत्नमादरेण योऽन्वहं प्रजल्पति प्रभातके हृदि स्मरन् गणेश्वरम् ।
अरोगतामदोषतां सुसाहितीं सुपुत्रतां समीहितायुरष्टभूतिमभ्युपैति सोऽचिरात् ॥ ६॥
మహాగణేశపంచరత్నమాదరేణ యోఽన్వహం ప్రజల్పతి ప్రభాతకే హృది స్మరన్ గణేశ్వరమ్ ।
అరోగతామదోషతాం సుసాహితీం సుపుత్రతాం సమీహితాయురష్టభూతిమభ్యుపైతి సోఽచిరాత్ ॥ ౬॥
mahaa-gaNesha-pa~ncharatnam-aadareNa yo.anvahaM
prajalpati prabhaatake hR^idi smaran gaNeshvaram
arogataam-adoShataaM susaahitiiM suputrataaM
samiihit-aayur-aShTabhuutim-abhyupaiti so.achiraat || 6 ||
Words: mahaa-gaNesha-pa~ncharatnam (this Great Ganesha Pancharatnam), aadareNa (with reverence), anvaham (every day), prajalpati (recites), prabhaatake (at dawn), hR^idi smaran (remembering in the heart), gaNeshvaram (Lord of the ganas), arogataam (freedom from disease), adoShataam (freedom from faults), susaahitiim (good learning), suputrataam (good progeny), samiihita-aayuH (desired lifespan), aShTa-bhuutim (eightfold prosperity), abhyupaiti (attains), achiraat (soon).
Meaning: Whoever reverently recites this Ganesha Pancharatnam every dawn, remembering Ganeshvara in the heart, soon attains freedom from disease, freedom from faults, good learning, good progeny, the desired lifespan, and eightfold prosperity.
