Guru Paduka Stotram

श्री गुरु पादुका स्तोत्रम् — Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with Meaning.

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya, on beholding the sandals of his Guru, Sri Govinda Bhagavatpada

The Nine Verses

Verse 1
अनन्तसंसारसमुद्रतार-नौकायिताभ्यां गुरुभक्तिदाभ्याम् ।
वैराग्यसाम्राज्यदपूजनाभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ १॥
అనంత-సంసార-సముద్ర-తార- నౌకాయితాభ్యాం గురు-భక్తి-దాభ్యామ్ ।
వైరాగ్య-సామ్రాజ్యద-పూజనాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౧॥
ananta-saMsaara-samudra-taara-naukaayitaabhyaam guru-bhakti-daabhyaam
vairaagya-saamraajyada-puujanaabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 1 ||
Words: ananta-saMsaara-samudra-taara-naukaayitaabhyaam (to the two that serve as a boat to cross the boundless ocean of worldly existence), guru-bhakti-daabhyaam (that bestow devotion to the Guru), vairaagya-saamraajyada-puujanaabhyaam (by worship of which one attains the very sovereignty of dispassion), namo namaH (salutations, salutations), shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam (to the two sacred sandals of the Guru).
Meaning: Salutations, again and again, to the holy sandals of the Guru — which serve as a boat to ferry us across the boundless ocean of worldly existence, which bestow devotion to the Guru, and by worshipping which one attains the very sovereignty of dispassion.
Verse 2
कवित्ववाराशिनिशाकराभ्यां दौर्भाग्यदावाम्बुदमालिकाभ्याम् ।
दूरीकृतानम्रविपत्तिताभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ २॥
కవిత్వ-వారాశి-నిశాకరాభ్యాం దౌర్భాగ్య-దావాంబుద-మాలికాభ్యామ్ ।
దూరీకృతానమ్ర-విపత్తితాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౨॥
kavitva-vaaraashi-nishaakaraabhyaam daurbhaagya-daavaambuda-maalikaabhyaam
duuriikR^itaanamra-vipattitaabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 2 ||
Words: kavitva-vaaraashi-nishaakaraabhyaam (to the two that are like the moon raising the tide of the ocean of poetic genius), daurbhaagya-daavaambuda-maalikaabhyaam (that are like a bank of rain-clouds quenching the wildfire of misfortune), duuriikR^itaanamra-vipattitaabhyaam (that drive far away the calamities of those who bow to them), shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam (to the sandals of the Guru).
Meaning: Salutations to the Guru's sandals — which are as the moon to the ocean of poetic genius, which are as a bank of rain-clouds quenching the wildfire of misfortune, and which drive far away the calamities of those who bow before them.
Verse 3
नता ययोः श्रीपतितां समीयुः कदाचिदप्याशु दरिद्रवर्याः ।
मूकाश्च वाचस्पतितां हि ताभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ३॥
నతా యయోః శ్రీ-పతితాం సమీయుః కదాచిద్-అప్యాశు దరిద్ర-వర్యాః ।
మూకాశ్-చ వాచస్-పతితాం హి తాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౩॥
nataa yayoH shrii-patitaaM samiiyuH kadaachid-apy-aashu daridra-varyaaH
muukaash-cha vaachas-patitaaM hi taabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 3 ||
Words: nataa (those who bow), yayoH (by which two), shrii-patitaaM samiiyuH (attain the state of being lords of fortune), kadaachid api aashu (sometimes swiftly), daridra-varyaaH (even the greatest among the poor), muukaaH cha (and the mute), vaachas-patitaaM hi (indeed the eloquence of Brihaspati, lord of speech), taabhyaam (by those two).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — bowing to which, even the very poorest swiftly attain the state of lords of fortune, and even the mute attain the eloquence of Brihaspati, the lord of speech.
Verse 4
नालीकनीकाशपदाहृताभ्यां नानाविमोहादिनिवारिकाभ्याम् ।
नमज्जनाभीष्टततिप्रदाभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ४॥
నాలీక-నీకాశ-పదాహృతాభ్యాం నానా-విమోహాది-నివారికాభ్యామ్ ।
నమజ్జనాభీష్ట-తతి-ప్రదాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౪॥
naaliika-niikaasha-padaahR^itaabhyaam naanaa-vimohaadi-nivaarikaabhyaam
namajjanaabhiiShTa-tati-pradaabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 4 ||
Words: naaliika-niikaasha-padaahR^itaabhyaam (to the two, lotus-like, that draw the heart to the Guru's feet), naanaa-vimohaadi-nivaarikaabhyaam (that ward off manifold delusions and the like), namajjanaabhiiShTa-tati-pradaabhyaam (that fulfil the whole host of desires of those who bow), shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam (to the Guru's sandals).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — which are charming as lotuses and draw the heart to the Guru's feet, which ward off manifold delusions, and which fulfil all the desires of those who bow before them.
Verse 5
नृपालिमौलिव्रजरत्नकान्ति-सरिद्विराजज्झषकन्यकाभ्याम् ।
नृपत्वदाभ्यां नतलोकपङ्क्तेः नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ५॥
నృపాలి-మౌలి-వ్రజ-రత్న-కాంతి- సరిద్-విరాజజ్ఝష-కన్యకాభ్యామ్ ।
నృపత్వ-దాభ్యాం నత-లోక-పంక్తేః నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౫॥
nR^ipaali-mauli-vraja-ratna-kaanti-sarid-viraajaj-jhaSha-kanyakaabhyaam
nR^ipatva-daabhyaam nata-loka-pa~NkteH namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 5 ||
Words: nR^ipaali-mauli-vraja-ratna-kaanti (with the lustre of the gem-studded crowns of rows of bowing kings), sarid-viraajaj-jhaSha-kanyakaabhyaam (shining like the radiant maiden of a river), nR^ipatva-daabhyaam (that confer kingship), nata-loka-pa~NkteH (upon the rows of bowing devotees).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — which shine, lit by the lustre of the gem-studded crowns of rows of bowing kings, like the radiant maiden of a river, and which confer the very status of a king upon the rows of devotees who bow before them.
Verse 6
पापान्धकारार्कपरम्पराभ्यां तापत्रयाहीन्द्रखगेश्वराभ्याम् ।
जाड्याब्धिसंशोषणवाडवाभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ६॥
పాపాంధకారార్క-పరంపరాభ్యాం తాప-త్రయాహీంద్ర-ఖగేశ్వరాభ్యామ్ ।
జాడ్యాబ్ధి-సంశోషణ-వాడవాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౬॥
paapaandhakaararka-paramparaabhyaam taapa-trayaahiindra-khageshvaraabhyaam
jaaDyaabdhi-samshoshaNa-vaaDavaabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 6 ||
Words: paapaandhakaararka-paramparaabhyaam (to the two that are like a succession of suns dispelling the darkness of sin), taapa-trayaahiindra-khageshvaraabhyaam (that are like Garuda to the serpent-king of the three afflictions), jaaDyaabdhi-samshoshaNa-vaaDavaabhyaam (that are like the submarine fire drying up the ocean of dullness and ignorance).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — which are like an unbroken succession of suns dispelling the darkness of sin, which are as Garuda to the serpent-king of the three afflictions, and which are as the submarine fire that dries up the very ocean of dullness and ignorance.
Verse 7
शमादिषट्कप्रदवैभवाभ्यां समाधिदानव्रतदीक्षिताभ्याम् ।
रमाधवाङ्घ्रिस्थिरभक्तिदाभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ७॥
శమాది-షట్క-ప్రద-వైభవాభ్యాం సమాధి-దాన-వ్రత-దీక్షితాభ్యామ్ ।
రమా-ధవాంఘ్రి-స్థిర-భక్తి-దాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౭॥
shamaadi-ShaTka-prada-vaibhavaabhyaam samaadhi-daana-vrata-diikShitaabhyaam
ramaa-dhavaa~Nghri-sthira-bhakti-daabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 7 ||
Words: shamaadi-ShaTka-prada-vaibhavaabhyaam (whose glory bestows the sixfold virtues beginning with inner calm), samaadhi-daana-vrata-diikShitaabhyaam (that are consecrated to the vow of granting absorption in samadhi), ramaa-dhavaa~Nghri-sthira-bhakti-daabhyaam (that give unwavering devotion to the feet of Vishnu, the consort of Rama/Lakshmi).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — whose glory bestows the sixfold virtue beginning with inner calm, which are consecrated to the vow of granting deep absorption in samadhi, and which give unwavering devotion to the feet of Lord Vishnu.
Verse 8
स्वार्चापराणामखिलेष्टदाभ्यां स्वाहासहायाक्षधुरन्धराभ्याम् ।
स्वान्ताच्छभावप्रदपूजनाभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ८॥
స్వార్చా-పరాణామ్-అఖిలేష్ట-దాభ్యాం స్వాహా-సహాయాక్ష-ధురంధరాభ్యామ్ ।
స్వాంతాచ్ఛ-భావ-ప్రద-పూజనాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౮॥
svaarchaa-paraaNaam-akhileShTa-daabhyaam svaahaa-sahaayaaksha-dhurandharaabhyaam
svaantaachchha-bhaava-prada-puujanaabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 8 ||
Words: svaarchaa-paraaNaam akhileShTa-daabhyaam (that grant every desired blessing to those devoted to worshipping them), svaahaa-sahaayaaksha-dhurandharaabhyaam (that bear the burden of the world with the help of sacred offerings), svaantaachchha-bhaava-prada-puujanaabhyaam (the worship of which bestows a pure and serene heart).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — which grant every desired blessing to those devoted to their worship, which bear the burden of the world with the help of sacred oblation, and the worship of which bestows a pure and serene heart.
Verse 9
कामादिसर्पव्रजगारुडाभ्यां विवेकवैराग्यनिधिप्रदाभ्याम् ।
बोधप्रदाभ्यां दृतमोक्षदाभ्यां नमो नमः श्रीगुरुपादुकाभ्याम् ॥ ९॥
కామాది-సర్ప-వ్రజ-గారుడాభ్యాం వివేక-వైరాగ్య-నిధి-ప్రదాభ్యామ్ ।
బోధ-ప్రదాభ్యాం దృత-మోక్ష-దాభ్యాం నమో నమః శ్రీ-గురు-పాదుకాభ్యామ్ ॥ ౯॥
kaamaadi-sarpa-vraja-gaaruDaabhyaam viveka-vairaagya-nidhi-pradaabhyaam
bodha-pradaabhyaam dR^ita-moksha-daabhyaam namo namaH shrii-guru-paadukaabhyaam || 9 ||
Words: kaamaadi-sarpa-vraja-gaaruDaabhyaam (to the two that are like Garuda to the host of serpents of desire and the like), viveka-vairaagya-nidhi-pradaabhyaam (that grant the very treasure of discrimination and dispassion), bodha-pradaabhyaam (that bestow spiritual awakening), dR^ita-moksha-daabhyaam (that grant liberation swiftly).
Meaning: Salutations to the sandals — which are as Garuda to the host of serpents of desire and its kindred passions, which grant the treasure of discrimination and dispassion, which bestow spiritual awakening, and which grant liberation swiftly.

|| इति श्रीमद् आदि शङ्कराचार्य विरचितं श्री गुरु पादुका स्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम् ||
Thus ends the Guru Paduka Stotram composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya.
