Bhaja Govindam

भज गोविन्दम् (मोहमुद्गरः) — Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with Meaning.

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya (also known as Mohamudgaram, "The Hammer of Delusion")

Refrain

Verse 1
भज गोविन्दं भज गोविन्दं गोविन्दं भज मूढमते ।
सम्प्राप्ते सन्निहिते काले न हि न हि रक्षति डुकृञ्करणे ॥ १॥
భజ గోవిందం భజ గోవిందం గోవిందం భజ మూఢమతే ।
సంప్రాప్తే సన్నిహితే కాలే న హి న హి రక్షతి డుకృఞ్-కరణే ॥ ౧॥
bhaja govindaM bhaja govindaM govindaM bhaja muuDhamate
sampraapte sannihite kaale na hi na hi rakShati dukR^i~NkaraNe || 1 ||
Words: bhaja govindam (worship Govinda), muuDha-mate (O foolish mind), sampraapte sannihite kaale (when the appointed time draws near, i.e. death), na hi na hi rakShati (it will surely not protect you), dukR^i~NkaraNe (the grammatical rule/formula, a symbol of dry scholarship).
Meaning: Worship Govinda, worship Govinda, worship Govinda, O foolish mind! At the appointed time of death, rules of grammar (mere intellectual learning) will certainly not save you.

Dvadashamanjarika Stotram — "Bouquet of Twelve Verses"

Spoken directly by Adi Shankaracharya to an old scholar of grammar.

Verse 2
मूढ जहीहि धनागमतृष्णां कुरु सद्बुद्धिं मनसि वितृष्णाम् ।
यल्लभसे निजकर्मोपात्तं वित्तं तेन विनोदय चित्तम् ॥ २॥
మూఢ జహీహి ధనాగమ-తృష్ణాం కురు సద్-బుద్ధిం మనసి వితృష్ణామ్ ।
యల్లభసే నిజ-కర్మోపాత్తం విత్తం తేన వినోదయ చిత్తమ్ ॥ ౨॥
muuDha jahiihi dhanaagama-tR^iShNaaM kuru sad-buddhiM manasi vitR^iShNaam
yal-labhase nija-karmopaattaM vittaM tena vinodaya chittam || 2 ||
Words: jahiihi (give up), dhana-aagama-tR^iShNaam (the thirst for acquiring wealth), sad-buddhim (a good/wise mind), vitR^iShNaam (free of desire), yat labhase (whatever you obtain), nija-karma-upaattam (earned by your own past deeds), vinodaya chittam (occupy/content your mind).
Meaning: O fool, give up your thirst for amassing wealth; cultivate a wise, desireless mind. Be content with whatever wealth comes to you through the fruits of your own past deeds.
Verse 3
नारीस्तनभरनाभीदेशं दृष्ट्वा मा गा मोहावेशम् ।
एतन्मांसवसादिविकारं मनसि विचिन्तय वारं वारम् ॥ ३॥
నారీ-స్తన-భర-నాభీ-దేశం దృష్ట్వా మా గా మోహావేశమ్ ।
ఏతన్మాంస-వసాది-వికారం మనసి విచింతయ వారం వారమ్ ॥ ౩॥
naarii-stana-bhara-naabhii-desham dR^iShTvaa maa gaa mohaaveshaM
etan-maamsa-vasaadi-vikaaraM manasi vichintaya vaaraM vaaram || 3 ||
Words: naarii-stana-bhara-naabhii-desham (a woman's full bosom and navel region), dR^iShTvaa (having seen), maa gaa mohaaveshaM (do not fall into an infatuated state), etat maamsa-vasa-aadi-vikaaram (this is merely a modification of flesh, fat, and such), vichintaya vaaraM vaaram (reflect again and again).
Meaning: Seeing a woman's full bosom and navel, do not fall into a state of infatuation. This is merely a transformation of flesh and fat — reflect on this again and again in your mind.
Verse 4
नलिनीदलगतजलमतितरलं तद्वज्जीवितमतिशयचपलम् ।
विद्धि व्याध्यभिमानग्रस्तं लोकं शोकहतं च समस्तम् ॥ ४॥
నలినీ-దల-గత-జలమ్-అతితరలం తద్వజ్జీవితమ్-అతిశయ-చపలమ్ ।
విద్ధి వ్యాధ్యభిమాన-గ్రస్తం లోకం శోక-హతం చ సమస్తమ్ ॥ ౪॥
naliniidala-gata-jalam-atitaralaM tadvaj-jiivitam-atishaya-chapalam
viddhi vyaadhy-abhimaana-grastaM lokaM shoka-hataM cha samastam || 4 ||
Words: naliniidala-gata-jalam (a drop of water on a lotus petal), atitaralam (extremely unstable), tadvat jiivitam (life is likewise), atishaya-chapalam (exceedingly fickle), vyaadhi-abhimaana-grastam (seized by disease and pride), shoka-hatam (struck by grief), samastam (entirely).
Meaning: Life is as unstable as a drop of water trembling upon a lotus petal — exceedingly fickle. Know that the entire world is seized by disease and pride, and is struck through and through by grief.
Verse 5
यावद्वित्तोपार्जनसक्तस्तावन्निजपरिवारो रक्तः ।
पश्चाज्जीवति जर्जरदेहे वार्तां कोऽपि न पृच्छति गेहे ॥ ५॥
యావద్-విత్తోపార్జన-సక్తస్-తావన్నిజ-పరివారో రక్తః ।
పశ్చాజ్జీవతి జర్జర-దేహే వార్తాం కోఽపి న పృచ్ఛతి గేహే ॥ ౫॥
yaavad-vittopaarjana-saktas-taavan-nija-parivaaro raktaH
pashchaaj-jiivati jarjara-dehe vaartaaM ko.api na pR^ichchhati gehe || 5 ||
Words: yaavat (so long as), vitta-upaarjana-saktaH (capable of earning wealth), taavat nija-parivaaraH raktaH (so long one's family remains devoted/attached), pashchaat jiivati jarjara-dehe (afterward, when one lives on with a decrepit body), vaartaam ko.api na pR^ichchhati gehe (no one at home even asks about him).
Meaning: So long as a man is capable of earning wealth, his family remains devoted to him. But afterward, when he lives on with a decrepit body, no one at home even bothers to ask after him.
Verse 6
यावत्पवनो निवसति देहे तावत्पृच्छति कुशलं गेहे ।
गतवति वायौ देहापाये भार्या बिभ्यति तस्मिन्काये ॥ ६॥
యావత్-పవనో నివసతి దేహే తావత్-పృచ్ఛతి కుశలం గేహే ।
గతవతి వాయౌ దేహాపాయే భార్యా బిభ్యతి తస్మిన్-కాయే ॥ ౬॥
yaavat-pavano nivasati dehe taavat-pR^ichchhati kushalaM gehe
gatavati vaayau dehaapaaye bhaaryaa bibhyati tasmin-kaaye || 6 ||
Words: yaavat pavanaH nivasati dehe (as long as the breath dwells in the body), taavat pR^ichchhati kushalam gehe (that long the household inquires after one's welfare), gatavati vaayau deha-apaaye (once the breath departs and the body perishes), bhaaryaa bibhyati tasmin kaaye (even the wife becomes afraid of that same body).
Meaning: As long as the breath dwells in the body, the household kindly inquires after one's welfare. But once the breath departs and the body perishes, even one's own wife becomes afraid of that very body.
Verse 7
बालस्तावत्क्रीडासक्तः तरुणस्तावत्तरुणीसक्तः ।
वृद्धस्तावच्चिन्तासक्तः परमे ब्रह्मणि कोऽपि न सक्तः ॥ ७॥
బాలస్-తావత్-క్రీడా-సక్తః తరుణస్-తావత్-తరుణీ-సక్తః ।
వృద్ధస్-తావచ్చింతా-సక్తః పరమే బ్రహ్మణి కోఽపి న సక్తః ॥ ౭॥
baalas-taavat-kriiDaa-saktaH taruNas-taavat-taruNii-saktaH
vR^iddhas-taavach-chintaa-saktaH parame brahmaNi ko.api na saktaH || 7 ||
Words: baalaH taavat kriiDaa-saktaH (the child is absorbed in play), taruNaH taavat taruNii-saktaH (the youth is absorbed in women), vR^iddhaH taavat chintaa-saktaH (the aged is absorbed in worries), parame brahmaNi ko.api na saktaH (hardly anyone is absorbed in the Supreme Brahman).
Meaning: The child is absorbed in play, the youth is absorbed in the pursuit of women, and the aged is absorbed in worries — but hardly anyone becomes absorbed in the Supreme Brahman.
Verse 8
का ते कान्ता कस्ते पुत्रः संसारोऽयमतीव विचित्रः ।
कस्य त्वं कः कुत आयातस्तत्त्वं चिन्तय तदिह भ्रातः ॥ ८॥
కా తే కాంతా కస్-తే పుత్రః సంసారోఽయమ్-అతీవ విచిత్రః ।
కస్య త్వం కః కుత ఆయాతస్-తత్త్వం చింతయ తద్-ఇహ భ్రాతః ॥ ౮॥
kaa te kaantaa kas-te putraH saMsaaro.ayam-atiiva vichitraH
kasya tvaM kaH kuta aayaatas-tattvaM chintaya tad-iha bhraataH || 8 ||
Words: kaa te kaantaa (who is your wife), kaH te putraH (who is your son), saMsaaraH ayam atiiva vichitraH (this samsara is exceedingly strange), kasya tvam (whose are you), kaH kutaH aayaataH (who are you, where have you come from), tattvam chintaya bhraataH (ponder this truth, O brother).
Meaning: Who is your wife? Who is your son? This samsara is exceedingly strange. Whose are you? Who are you? Where have you come from? Ponder this truth, O brother, here and now.
Verse 9
सत्सङ्गत्वे निस्सङ्गत्वं निस्सङ्गत्वे निर्मोहत्वम् ।
निर्मोहत्वे निश्चलतत्त्वं निश्चलतत्त्वे जीवन्मुक्तिः ॥ ९॥
సత్-సంగత్వే నిస్-సంగత్వం నిస్-సంగత్వే నిర్మోహత్వమ్ ।
నిర్మోహత్వే నిశ్చల-తత్త్వం నిశ్చల-తత్త్వే జీవన్-ముక్తిః ॥ ౯॥
sat-sa~Ngatve nis-sa~NgatvaM nis-sa~Ngatve nirmohatvam
nirmohatve nishchala-tattvaM nishchala-tattve jiivan-muktiH || 9 ||
Words: sat-sa~Ngatve (from association with the good/wise), nis-sa~Ngatvam (comes non-attachment), nirmohatvam (freedom from delusion), nishchala-tattvam (steadfastness in the Truth), jiivan-muktiH (liberation while living).
Meaning: From good company comes non-attachment; from non-attachment comes freedom from delusion; from freedom from delusion comes steadfastness in the Truth; and from steadfastness in the Truth comes liberation while yet living.
Verse 10
वयसि गते कः कामविकारः शुष्के नीरे कः कासारः ।
क्षीणे वित्ते कः परिवारः ज्ञाते तत्त्वे कः संसारः ॥ १०॥
వయసి గతే కః కామ-వికారః శుష్కే నీరే కః కాసారః ।
క్షీణే విత్తే కః పరివారః జ్ఞాతే తత్త్వే కః సంసారః ॥ ౧౦॥
vayasi gate kaH kaama-vikaaraH shuShke niire kaH kaasaaraH
kShiiNe vitte kaH parivaaraH j~naate tattve kaH saMsaaraH || 10 ||
Words: vayasi gate (when youth has passed), kaH kaama-vikaaraH (what use is passion), shuShke niire kaH kaasaaraH (what is a lake when its water has dried up), kShiiNe vitte kaH parivaaraH (where are the relatives when wealth is exhausted), j~naate tattve kaH saMsaaraH (where is samsara when the Truth is known).
Meaning: When youth has passed, what use is passion? When its water has dried up, what is a lake? When wealth is exhausted, where are the relatives? And when the Truth is known, where is samsara at all?
Verse 11
मा कुरु धनजनयौवनगर्वं हरति निमेषात्कालः सर्वम् ।
मायामयमिदमखिलं हित्वा ब्रह्मपदं त्वं प्रविश विदित्वा ॥ ११॥
మా కురు ధన-జన-యౌవన-గర్వం హరతి నిమేషాత్-కాలః సర్వమ్ ।
మాయామయమ్-ఇదమ్-అఖిలం హిత్వా బ్రహ్మ-పదం త్వం ప్రవిశ విదిత్వా ॥ ౧౧॥
maa kuru dhana-jana-yauvana-garvaM harati nimeShaat-kaalaH sarvam
maayaamayam-idam-akhilaM hitvaa brahma-padaM tvaM pravisha viditvaa || 11 ||
Words: maa kuru garvam (do not take pride), dhana-jana-yauvana (in wealth, followers, and youth), harati nimeShaat kaalaH sarvam (time takes all of it away in an instant), maayaamayam idam akhilam hitvaa (giving up this entirely illusory world), brahma-padam tvaM pravisha viditvaa (enter the state of Brahman, having known it).
Meaning: Do not take pride in wealth, followers, and youth — time snatches all of it away in an instant. Giving up this entirely illusory world, enter the state of Brahman having truly known it.
Verse 12
दिनयामिन्यौ सायं प्रातः शिशिरवसन्तौ पुनरायातः ।
कालः क्रीडति गच्छत्यायुस्तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशावायुः ॥ १२॥
దిన-యామిన్యౌ సాయం ప్రాతః శిశిర-వసంతౌ పునర్-ఆయాతః ।
కాలః క్రీడతి గచ్ఛత్యాయుస్-తద్-అపి న ముంచత్యాశా-వాయుః ॥ ౧౨॥
dina-yaaminyau saayaM praataH shishira-vasantau punaraayaataH
kaalaH kriiDati gachchhaty-aayus-tad-api na mu~nchaty-aashaa-vaayuH || 12 ||
Words: dina-yaaminyau (day and night), saayam praataH (dusk and dawn), shishira-vasantau (winter and spring), punar aayaataH (come again and again), kaalaH kriiDati (time plays on), gachchhati aayuH (life ebbs away), aashaa-vaayuH na mu~nchati (yet the wind of desire never departs).
Meaning: Day and night, dusk and dawn, winter and spring — all come and go again and again. Time plays on and life ebbs away, yet the storm of desire never leaves a person.
Verse 13
का ते कान्ता धनगतचिन्ता वातुल किं तव नास्ति नियन्ता ।
त्रिजगति सज्जनसङ्गतिरेका भवति भवार्णवतरणे नौका ॥ १३॥
కా తే కాంతా ధన-గత-చింతా వాతుల కిం తవ నాస్తి నియంతా ।
త్రి-జగతి సజ్-జన-సంగతిర్-ఏకా భవతి భవార్ణవ-తరణే నౌకా ॥ ౧౩॥
kaa te kaantaa dhana-gata-chintaa vaatula kiM tava naasti niyantaa
tri-jagati saj-jana-sa~Ngatir-ekaa bhavati bhavaarNava-taraNe naukaa || 13 ||
Words: kaa te kaantaa (who is your beloved), dhana-gata-chintaa (this preoccupation with wealth), vaatula (O madman), kim tava naasti niyantaa (have you no one to restrain you), tri-jagati (in the three worlds), saj-jana-sa~Ngatir ekaa (only the company of the good/wise), bhavaarNava-taraNe naukaa (is the boat to cross the ocean of samsara).
Meaning: Who is this wife of yours, O madman, whose preoccupation with wealth consumes you? Have you no one to restrain you? In all three worlds, only the company of the good and wise is the boat that carries one across the ocean of samsara.

Chaturdashamanjarika Stotram — "Bouquet of Fourteen Verses"

Traditionally attributed to fourteen disciples of Sri Shankaracharya.

Verse 14
जटिलो मुण्डी लुञ्छितकेशः काषायाम्बरबहुकृतवेषः ।
पश्यन्नपि च न पश्यति मूढो ह्युदरनिमित्तं बहुकृतवेषः ॥ १४॥
జటిలో ముండీ లుంచిత-కేశః కాషాయాంబర-బహు-కృత-వేషః ।
పశ్యన్న్-అపి చ న పశ్యతి మూఢో హ్యుదర-నిమిత్తం బహు-కృత-వేషః ॥ ౧౪॥
jaTilo muNDii lu~nchita-keshaH kaaShaayaambara-bahu-kR^ita-veShaH
pashyann-api cha na pashyati muuDho hy-udara-nimittaM bahu-kR^ita-veShaH || 14 ||
Words: jaTilaH (one with matted locks), muNDii (a shaven-headed one), lu~nchita-keshaH (one with plucked-out hair), kaaShaaya-ambara-bahu-kR^ita-veShaH (many disguised in saffron robes), pashyann api na pashyati (even while seeing, does not truly see), udara-nimittam (merely for the sake of the belly, livelihood).
Meaning: Many wear matted locks, many are shaven-headed, many have their hair plucked out, and many don saffron robes — all merely as disguises for livelihood. Though seeing the truth before them, the foolish do not truly perceive it.
Verse 15
अङ्गं गलितं पलितं मुण्डं दशनविहीनं जातं तुण्डम् ।
वृद्धो याति गृहीत्वा दण्डं तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशापिण्डम् ॥ १५॥
అంగం గలితం పలితం ముండం దశన-విహీనం జాతం తుండమ్ ।
వృద్ధో యాతి గృహీత్వా దండం తద్-అపి న ముంచత్యాశా-పిండమ్ ॥ ౧౫॥
a~NgaM galitaM palitaM muNDaM dashana-vihiinaM jaataM tuNDam
vR^iddho yaati gR^ihiitvaa daNDaM tad-api na mu~nchaty-aashaa-piNDam || 15 ||
Words: a~Ngam galitam (the body has decayed), palitam muNDam (the head has turned grey/bald), dashana-vihiinam jaataM tuNDam (the mouth has become toothless), vR^iddhaH yaati gR^ihiitvaa daNDam (the old man walks leaning on a staff), tad api na mu~nchati aashaa-piNDam (still he does not let go of the bundle of desires).
Meaning: The body has decayed, the head has grown grey and bald, the mouth has become toothless, and the old man walks leaning upon a staff — yet even then he does not let go of his bundle of desires.
Verse 16
अग्रे वह्निः पृष्ठे भानुः रात्रौ चुबुकसमर्पितजानुः ।
करतलभिक्षस्तरुतलवासस्तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशापाशः ॥ १६॥
అగ్రే వహ్నిః పృష్ఠే భానుః రాత్రౌ చుబుక-సమర్పిత-జానుః ।
కర-తల-భిక్షస్-తరు-తల-వాసస్-తద్-అపి న ముంచత్యాశా-పాశః ॥ ౧౬॥
agre vahniH pR^iShThe bhaanuH raatrau chubuka-samarpita-jaanuH
kara-tala-bhikShas-taru-tala-vaasas-tad-api na mu~nchaty-aashaa-paashaH || 16 ||
Words: agre vahniH (fire in front), pR^iShThe bhaanuH (the sun behind), raatrau chubuka-samarpita-jaanuH (at night, chin resting on knee), kara-tala-bhikShaH (begging food into the palm of the hand), taru-tala-vaasaH (dwelling beneath a tree), aashaa-paashaH na mu~nchati (still does not release the noose of desire).
Meaning: With fire in front and the sun behind, at night curling up with chin resting on knee, eating alms from the palm of his hand, and dwelling beneath a tree — even such a one does not release the noose of desire.
Verse 17
कुरुते गङ्गासागरगमनं व्रतपरिपालनमथवा दानम् ।
ज्ञानविहीनः सर्वमतेन मुक्तिं न भजति जन्मशतेन ॥ १७॥
కురుతే గంగా-సాగర-గమనం వ్రత-పరిపాలనమ్-అథవా దానమ్ ।
జ్ఞాన-విహీనః సర్వమతేన ముక్తిం న భజతి జన్మ-శతేన ॥ ౧౭॥
kurute ga~Ngaa-saagara-gamanaM vrata-paripaalanam-athavaa daanam
j~naana-vihiinaH sarva-matena muktiM na bhajati janma-shatena || 17 ||
Words: kurute ga~Ngaa-saagara-gamanam (one may travel to Gangasagar), vrata-paripaalanam (observe vows), athavaa daanam (or give charity), j~naana-vihiinaH (devoid of true knowledge), sarva-matena (by all accounts), muktim na bhajati janma-shatena (does not attain liberation even in a hundred births).
Meaning: One may travel to Gangasagar, observe vows, or give charity — yet, devoid of true knowledge, by every account, liberation is not attained even in a hundred births.
Verse 18
सुरमन्दिरतरुमूलनिवासः शय्या भूतलमजिनं वासः ।
सर्वपरिग्रहभोगत्यागः कस्य सुखं न करोति विरागः ॥ १८॥
సుర-మందిర-తరు-మూల-నివాసః శయ్యా భూతలమ్-అజినం వాసః ।
సర్వ-పరిగ్రహ-భోగ-త్యాగః కస్య సుఖం న కరోతి విరాగః ॥ ౧౮॥
sura-mandira-taru-muula-nivaasaH shayyaa bhuutalam-ajinaM vaasaH
sarva-parigraha-bhoga-tyaagaH kasya sukhaM na karoti viraagaH || 18 ||
Words: sura-mandira-taru-muula-nivaasaH (dwelling in a temple or at the foot of a tree), shayyaa bhuutalam (the bare earth as one's bed), ajinam vaasaH (a deerskin as one's garment), sarva-parigraha-bhoga-tyaagaH (renouncing all possessions and pleasures), kasya sukham na karoti viraagaH (for whom does dispassion not bring happiness).
Meaning: Dwelling in a temple or at the foot of a tree, with the bare earth as one's bed and a deerskin as one's garment, renouncing all possessions and pleasures — for whom does such dispassion not bring happiness?
Verse 19
योगरतो वा भोगरतो वा सङ्गरतो वा सङ्गविहीनः ।
यस्य ब्रह्मणि रमते चित्तं नन्दति नन्दति नन्दत्येव ॥ १९॥
యోగ-రతో వా భోగ-రతో వా సంగ-రతో వా సంగ-విహీనః ।
యస్య బ్రహ్మణి రమతే చిత్తం నందతి నందతి నందత్యేవ ॥ ౧౯॥
yoga-rato vaa bhoga-rato vaa sa~Nga-rato vaa sa~Nga-vihiinaH
yasya brahmaNi ramate chittaM nandati nandati nandaty-eva || 19 ||
Words: yoga-ratah vaa (whether devoted to yoga), bhoga-rataH vaa (or devoted to enjoyment), sa~Nga-rataH vaa sa~Nga-vihiinaH (whether attached or free of attachment), yasya brahmaNi ramate chittam (whoever's mind delights in Brahman), nandati nandati nandaty-eva (rejoices, rejoices, truly rejoices).
Meaning: Whether devoted to yoga or to enjoyment, whether attached or free of attachment — only he whose mind delights in Brahman truly rejoices, rejoices indeed.
Verse 20
भगवद्गीता किञ्चिदधीता गङ्गाजललवकणिका पीता ।
सकृदपि येन मुरारिसमर्चा क्रियते तस्य यमेन न चर्चा ॥ २०॥
భగవద్-గీతా కించిద్-అధీతా గంగా-జల-లవ-కణికా పీతా ।
సకృద్-అపి యేన మురారి-సమర్చా క్రియతే తస్య యమేన న చర్చా ॥ ౨౦॥
bhagavad-giitaa ki~nchid-adhiitaa ga~Ngaa-jala-lava-kaNikaa piitaa
sakR^id-api yena muraari-samarchaa kriyate tasya yamena na charchaa || 20 ||
Words: bhagavad-giitaa ki~nchid-adhiitaa (having studied even a little of the Bhagavad Gita), ga~Ngaa-jala-lava-kaNikaa piitaa (having drunk even a tiny drop of Ganga water), sakR^id api (even once), muraari-samarchaa kriyate (worship of Murari is performed), tasya yamena na charchaa (for him there is no reckoning with Yama).
Meaning: For one who has studied even a little of the Bhagavad Gita, drunk even a tiny drop of Ganga water, and worshipped Murari even once — for him there is no reckoning to fear from Yama, the lord of death.
Verse 21
पुनरपि जननं पुनरपि मरणं पुनरपि जननीजठरे शयनम् ।
इह संसारे बहुदुस्तारे कृपयाऽपारे पाहि मुरारे ॥ २१॥
పునర్-అపి జననం పునర్-అపి మరణం పునర్-అపి జననీ-జఠరే శయనమ్ ।
ఇహ సంసారే బహు-దుస్తారే కృపయాఽపారే పాహి మురారే ॥ ౨౧॥
punarapi jananaM punarapi maraNaM punarapi jananii-jaThare shayanam
iha saMsaare bahu-dustaare kR^ipayaa.apaare paahi muraare || 21 ||
Words: punarapi jananam (birth again), punarapi maraNam (death again), punarapi jananii-jaThare shayanam (lying again in a mother's womb), iha saMsaare bahu-dustaare (in this samsara so hard to cross), apaare (boundless), kR^ipayaa paahi muraare (protect me by Your grace, O Murari).
Meaning: Birth again, death again, and again lying within a mother's womb — this boundless samsara is so hard to cross. O Murari, protect me through Your mercy.
Verse 22
रथ्याचर्पटविरचितकन्थः पुण्यापुण्यविवर्जितपन्थः ।
योगी योगनियोजितचित्तो रमते बालोन्मत्तवदेव ॥ २२॥
రథ్యా-చర్పట-విరచిత-కంథః పుణ్యాపుణ్య-వివర్జిత-పంథః ।
యోగీ యోగ-నియోజిత-చిత్తో రమతే బాలోన్మత్త-వద్-ఏవ ॥ ౨౨॥
rathyaa-charpaTa-virachita-kanthaH puNya-apuNya-vivarjita-panthaH
yogii yoga-niyojita-chitto ramate baalonmattavad-eva || 22 ||
Words: rathyaa-charpaTa-virachita-kanthaH (one whose patchwork garment is made of rags picked off the road), puNya-apuNya-vivarjita-panthaH (whose path is free from merit and sin), yogii yoga-niyojita-chittaH (the yogi whose mind is fixed in yoga), ramate baalonmattavad-eva (revels just like a child or an intoxicated person).
Meaning: Clothed in a patchwork garment of rags picked off the road, walking a path free of merit and sin, the yogi whose mind is fixed in yoga revels in bliss, just like a child or one who is intoxicated.
Verse 23
कस्त्वं कोऽहं कुत आयातः का मे जननी को मे तातः ।
इति परिभावय सर्वमसारं विश्वं त्यक्त्वा स्वप्नविचारम् ॥ २३॥
కస్-త్వం కోఽహం కుత ఆయాతః కా మే జననీ కో మే తాతః ।
ఇతి పరిభావయ సర్వమ్-అసారం విశ్వం త్యక్త్వా స్వప్న-విచారమ్ ॥ ౨౩॥
kas-tvaM ko.ahaM kuta aayaataH kaa me jananii ko me taataH
iti paribhaavaya sarvam-asaaraM vishvaM tyaktvaa svapna-vichaaram || 23 ||
Words: kaH tvam (who are you), ko.aham (who am I), kuta aayaataH (where have I come from), kaa me jananii (who is my mother), ko me taataH (who is my father), iti paribhaavaya (reflect thus), sarvam asaaram (all is essence-less), vishvam tyaktvaa svapna-vichaaram (give up this world as one gives up dream-thoughts).
Meaning: Who are you? Who am I? From where have I come? Who is my mother, who is my father? Reflect thus, that all this is without essence, and let go of this world like the idle thoughts of a dream.
Verse 24
त्वयि मयि चान्यत्रैको विष्णुर्व्यर्थं कुप्यसि मय्यसहिष्णुः ।
भव समचित्तः सर्वत्र त्वं वाञ्छस्यचिराद्यदि विष्णुत्वम् ॥ २४॥
త్వయి మయి చాన్యత్రైకో విష్ణుర్-వ్యర్థం కుప్యసి మయ్యసహిష్ణుః ।
భవ సమ-చిత్తః సర్వత్ర త్వం వాంఛస్యచిరాద్-యది విష్ణుత్వమ్ ॥ ౨౪॥
tvayi mayi chaanyatraiko viShNur-vyarthaM kupyasi mayy-asahiShNuH
bhava sama-chittaH sarvatra tvaM vaa~nchhasy-achiraad-yadi viShNutvam || 24 ||
Words: tvayi mayi cha anyatra ekaH viShNuH (in you, in me, and everywhere is the one Vishnu), vyartham kupyasi mayi asahiShNuH (you needlessly grow angry and impatient with me), bhava sama-chittaH sarvatra (be even-minded everywhere), vaa~nchhasi achiraat yadi viShNutvam (if you wish soon to attain the state of Vishnu).
Meaning: In you, in me, and everywhere, dwells the one Vishnu alone — your anger and impatience with me are needless. Be even-minded everywhere, if you wish to attain the state of Vishnu without delay.
Verse 25
शत्रौ मित्रे पुत्रे बन्धौ मा कुरु यत्नं विग्रहसन्धौ ।
सर्वस्मिन्नपि पश्यात्मानं सर्वत्रोत्सृज भेदाज्ञानम् ॥ २५॥
శత్రౌ మిత్రే పుత్రే బంధౌ మా కురు యత్నం విగ్రహ-సంధౌ ।
సర్వస్మిన్న్-అపి పశ్యాత్మానం సర్వత్రోత్సృజ భేదాజ్ఞానమ్ ॥ ౨౫॥
shatrau mitre putre bandhau maa kuru yatnaM vigraha-sandhau
sarvasminn-api pashyaatmaanaM sarvatrotsR^ija bhedaaj~naanam || 25 ||
Words: shatrau mitre putre bandhau (toward an enemy, a friend, a son, a relative), maa kuru yatnam vigraha-sandhau (make no effort in conflict or reconciliation), sarvasmin api pashya aatmaanam (see the Self in everyone), sarvatra utsR^ija bhedaaj~naanam (abandon everywhere the notion of difference).
Meaning: Toward an enemy, a friend, a son, or a relative, make no special effort in conflict or reconciliation. See the Self in everyone, and abandon everywhere the notion of duality.
Verse 26
कामं क्रोधं लोभं मोहं त्यक्त्वाऽऽत्मानं भावय कोऽहम् ।
आत्मज्ञानविहीना मूढास्ते पच्यन्ते नरकनिगूढाः ॥ २६॥
కామం క్రోధం లోభం మోహం త్యక్త్వాఽఽత్మానం భావయ కోఽహమ్ ।
ఆత్మ-జ్ఞాన-విహీనా మూఢాస్-తే పచ్యంతే నరక-నిగూఢాః ॥ ౨౬॥
kaamaM krodhaM lobhaM mohaM tyaktvaa.aatmaanaM bhaavaya ko.aham
aatma-j~naana-vihiinaa muuDhaas-te pachyante naraka-niguuDhaaH || 26 ||
Words: kaamam krodham lobham moham tyaktvaa (having given up lust, anger, greed, and delusion), aatmaanam bhaavaya ko.aham (contemplate the truth of who am I), aatma-j~naana-vihiinaa muuDhaaH (those fools devoid of Self-knowledge), pachyante naraka-niguuDhaaH (are cast into hell and suffer there).
Meaning: Give up lust, anger, greed, and delusion, and contemplate the truth of who you really are. Those foolish ones devoid of Self-knowledge are cast into hell and suffer there endlessly.
Verse 27
गेयं गीतानामसहस्रं ध्येयं श्रीपतिरूपमजस्रम् ।
नेयं सज्जनसङ्गे चित्तं देयं दीनजनाय च वित्तम् ॥ २७॥
గేయం గీతా-నామ-సహస్రం ధ్యేయం శ్రీపతి-రూపమ్-అజస్రమ్ ।
నేయం సజ్-జన-సంగే చిత్తం దేయం దీన-జనాయ చ విత్తమ్ ॥ ౨౭॥
geyaM giitaa-naama-sahasraM dhyeyaM shriipati-ruupam-ajasram
neyaM saj-jana-sa~Nge chittaM deyaM diina-janaaya cha vittam || 27 ||
Words: geyam giitaa-naama-sahasram (recite the Gita and the thousand names of Vishnu), dhyeyam shriipati-ruupam ajasram (meditate constantly on the form of Vishnu), neyam saj-jana-sa~Nge chittam (lead the mind into the company of the good), deyam diina-janaaya cha vittam (give wealth to the poor and needy).
Meaning: Recite the Gita and the thousand names of the Lord; meditate constantly on the form of Vishnu; lead your mind ever into the company of the good; and give of your wealth to the poor and needy.

Shankara's Concluding Verses

Verse 28
सुखतः क्रियते रामाभोगः पश्चाद्धन्त शरीरे रोगः ।
यद्यपि लोके मरणं शरणं तदपि न मुञ्चति पापाचरणम् ॥ २८॥
సుఖతః క్రియతే రామా-భోగః పశ్చాద్-ధంత శరీరే రోగః ।
యద్యపి లోకే మరణం శరణం తద్-అపి న ముంచతి పాపాచరణమ్ ॥ ౨౮॥
sukhataH kriyate raamaa-bhogaH pashchaad-dhanta shariire rogaH
yadyapi loke maraNaM sharaNaM tad-api na mu~nchati paapaacharaNam || 28 ||
Words: sukhataH kriyate raamaa-bhogaH (for the sake of pleasure one indulges in sensual enjoyment), pashchaat dhanta shariire rogaH (alas, afterward the body falls prey to disease), yadyapi loke maraNam sharaNam (though in this world death is the final refuge), tad api na mu~nchati paapaacharaNam (still one does not give up sinful conduct).
Meaning: For the sake of pleasure one indulges in sensual enjoyment, but afterward, alas, the body falls prey to disease. Although death is the final refuge in this world, still one does not give up sinful conduct.
Verse 29
अर्थमनर्थं भावय नित्यं नास्तिततः सुखलेशः सत्यम् ।
पुत्रादपि धनभाजां भीतिः सर्वत्रैषा विहिता रीतिः ॥ २९॥
అర్థమ్-అనర్థం భావయ నిత్యం నాస్తి-తతః సుఖ-లేశః సత్యమ్ ।
పుత్రాద్-అపి ధన-భాజాం భీతిః సర్వత్రైషా విహితా రీతిః ॥ ౨౯॥
artham-anarthaM bhaavaya nityaM naastitataH sukha-leshaH satyam
putraad-api dhana-bhaajaaM bhiitiH sarvatraiShaa vihitaa riitiH || 29 ||
Words: artham anartham bhaavaya nityam (constantly reflect that wealth is a source of trouble), na asti tataH sukha-leshaH satyam (truly, from it there is not the least bit of happiness), putraat api dhana-bhaajaam bhiitiH (the wealthy fear even their own sons), sarvatra eShaa vihitaa riitiH (such is the way everywhere).
Meaning: Constantly reflect that wealth is truly a source of trouble, not happiness — from it there is not the least bit of real joy. The wealthy fear even their own sons; such is the way of the world everywhere.
Verse 30
प्राणायामं प्रत्याहारं नित्यानित्यविवेकविचारम् ।
जाप्यसमेतसमाधिविधानं कुर्ववधानं महदवधानम् ॥ ३०॥
ప్రాణాయామం ప్రత్యాహారం నిత్యానిత్య-వివేక-విచారమ్ ।
జాప్య-సమేత-సమాధి-విధానం కుర్వవధానం మహద్-అవధానమ్ ॥ ౩౦॥
praaNaayaamaM pratyaahaaraM nityaanitya-viveka-vichaaram
jaapya-sameta-samaadhi-vidhaanaM kurv-avadhaanaM mahad-avadhaanam || 30 ||
Words: praaNaayaamam (regulation of the breath), pratyaahaaram (withdrawal of the senses), nityaanitya-viveka-vichaaram (discernment between the eternal and the transient), jaapya-sameta-samaadhi-vidhaanam (the discipline of meditation together with repetition of the divine name), kuru avadhaanam mahat avadhaanam (do this with great, careful attention).
Meaning: Practice regulation of the breath, withdrawal of the senses, and the discernment between the eternal and the transient. Undertake the discipline of meditation together with repetition of the divine name — and do all this with the utmost, careful attention.
Verse 31
गुरुचरणाम्बुजनिर्भरभक्तः संसारादचिराद्भव मुक्तः ।
सेन्द्रियमानसनियमादेवं द्रक्ष्यसि निजहृदयस्थं देवम् ॥ ३१॥
గురు-చరణాంబుజ-నిర్భర-భక్తః సంసారాద్-అచిరాద్-భవ ముక్తః ।
సేంద్రియ-మానస-నియమాద్-ఏవం ద్రక్ష్యసి నిజ-హృదయస్థం దేవమ్ ॥ ౩౧॥
guru-charaNaambuja-nirbhara-bhaktaH saMsaaraad-achiraad-bhava muktaH
sendriya-maanasa-niyamaad-evaM drakShyasi nija-hR^idayasthaM devam || 31 ||
Words: guru-charaNa-ambuja-nirbhara-bhaktaH (O devoted one, wholly devoted to the lotus feet of the Guru), saMsaaraat achiraat bhava muktaH (become free from samsara without delay), sendriya-maanasa-niyamaat evam (through such discipline of the senses and the mind), drakShyasi nija-hR^idayastham devam (you shall behold the Lord dwelling within your own heart).
Meaning: O devotee wholly devoted to the lotus feet of the Guru, become free from samsara without delay. Through such discipline of the senses and the mind, you shall behold the Lord who dwells within your own heart.

|| इति श्रीमद् आदि शङ्कराचार्य विरचितं मोहमुद्गरः (भज गोविन्दम्) सम्पूर्णम् ||
Thus ends the Mohamudgaram (Bhaja Govindam) composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya.
