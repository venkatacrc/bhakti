Kanakadhara Stotram

कनकधारा स्तोत्रम् — Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with Meaning.

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya

The Twenty-One Verses

Verse 1
अङ्गं हरेः पुलकभूषणमाश्रयन्ती भृङ्गाङ्गनेव मुकुलाभरणं तमालम् ।
अङ्गीकृताखिलविभूतिरपाङ्गलीला माङ्गल्यदाऽस्तु मम मङ्गलदेवतायाः ॥ १॥
అంగం హరేః పులక-భూషణమ్-ఆశ్రయంతీ భృంగాంగనేవ ముకులాభరణం తమాలమ్ ।
అంగీకృతాఖిల-విభూతిర్-అపాంగ-లీలా మాంగల్యదాఽస్తు మమ మంగల-దేవతాయాః ॥ ౧॥
a~NgaM hareH pulaka-bhuuShaNam-aashrayantii
bhR^i~Ngaa~Nganeva mukulaabharaNaM tamaalam
a~Ngiikrita-akhila-vibhuutir-apaa~Nga-liilaa
maa~Ngalyad-aastu mama ma~Ngala-devataayaaH || 1 ||
Words: a~Ngam hareH (Hari's body), pulaka-bhuuShaNam (thrilled with goosebumps), aashrayantii (resting upon, like), bhR^i~Ngaa~Nganaa-iva (like a female bee), mukula-aabharaNam tamaalam (the budding tamala tree), a~NgiikR^ita-akhila-vibhuuti (which has taken in all opulence), apaa~Nga-liilaa (the playful side-glance), maa~Ngalyadaa (bestower of auspiciousness), ma~Ngala-devataayaaH (of the Goddess of Fortune).
Meaning: May the playful side-glance of the Goddess of Fortune — which rests thrilled upon Hari's body like a female bee upon a budding tamala tree, and which has taken in all opulence — ever bestow auspiciousness upon me.
Verse 2
मुग्धा मुहुर्विदधती वदने मुरारेः प्रेमत्रपाप्रणिहितानि गतागतानि ।
माला दृशोर्मधुकरीव महोत्पले या सा मे श्रियं दिशतु सागरसम्भवायाः ॥ २॥
ముగ్ధా ముహుర్-విదధతీ వదనే మురారేః ప్రేమ-త్రపా-ప్రణిహితాని గతాగతాని ।
మాలా దృశోర్-మధుకరీవ మహోత్పలే యా సా మే శ్రియం దిశతు సాగర-సంభవాయాః ॥ ౨॥
mugdhaa muhur-vidadhatii vadane muraareH
prema-trapaa-praNihitaani gataagataani
maalaa dR^ishor-madhukariiva mahotpale yaa
saa me shriyaM dishatu saagara-sambhavaayaaH || 2 ||
Words: mugdhaa (innocent), muhuH vidadhatii (repeatedly casting), vadane muraareH (upon Murari's face), prema-trapaa-praNihitaani (sent forth with love and shyness), gataagataani (moving to and fro), maalaa dR^ishoH madhukarii-iva (like a garland/swarm of bees of the eyes), mahotpale (upon the great lotus), saagara-sambhavaayaaH (of the Ocean-born One).
Meaning: May the Ocean-born Goddess grant me prosperity through that innocent gaze — moving to and fro upon Murari's face out of love mingled with shyness — resembling a swarm of bees hovering over a great lotus.
Verse 3
विश्वामरेन्द्रपदविभ्रमदानदक्षं आनन्दहेतुरधिकं मुरविद्विषोऽपि ।
ईषन्निषीदतु मयि क्षणमीक्षणार्धम् इन्दीवरोदरसहोदरमिन्दिरायाः ॥ ३॥
విశ్వామరేంద్ర-పద-విభ్రమ-దాన-దక్షం ఆనంద-హేతుర్-అధికం ముర-విద్విషోఽపి ।
ఈషన్నిషీదతు మయి క్షణమ్-ఈక్షణార్ధమ్ ఇందీవరోదర-సహోదరమ్-ఇందిరాయాః ॥ ౩॥
vishvaamarendra-pada-vibhrama-daana-dakShaM
aananda-hetur-adhikaM mura-vidviSho.api
iiShan-niShiidatu mayi kShaNam-iikShaNaardhaM
indiivarodara-sahodaram-indiraayaaH || 3 ||
Words: vishva-amarendra-pada (the rank of Indra over all celestials), vibhrama-daana-dakSham (capable of granting), aananda-hetuH adhikam (the supreme cause of joy), mura-vidviShaH api (even to Vishnu, foe of Mura), indiivara-udara-sahodaram (akin to the inner hue of a blue lotus), indiraayaaH (of Indira/Lakshmi).
Meaning: May that half-glance of Indira — capable of granting even the rank of Indra to the celestials, the very source of supreme joy even for Vishnu, and resembling the inner hue of a blue lotus — rest upon me, if only for a moment.
Verse 4
आमीलिताक्षमधिगम्य मुदा मुकुन्दं आनन्दकन्दमनिमेषमनङ्गतन्त्रम् ।
आकेकरस्थितकनीनिकपक्ष्मनेत्रं भूत्यै भवेन्मम भुजङ्गशयाङ्गनायाः ॥ ४॥
ఆమీలితాక్షమ్-అధిగమ్య ముదా ముకుందం ఆనంద-కందమ్-అనిమేషమ్-అనంగ-తంత్రమ్ ।
ఆకేకర-స్థిత-కనీనిక-పక్ష్మనేత్రం భూత్యై భవేన్మమ భుజంగ-శయాంగనాయాః ॥ ౪॥
aamiilitaaksham-adhigamya mudaa mukundaM
aananda-kandam-animeSham-ana~Nga-tantram
aakekara-sthita-kaniinika-pakShma-netraM
bhuutyai bhaven-mama bhuja~Nga-shayaa~Nganaayaah || 4 ||
Words: aamiilita-aksham (with half-closed eyes), adhigamya mudaa (gazing joyfully), mukundam (upon Mukunda), aananda-kandam (the very root of bliss), animeSham (unblinking), ana~Nga-tantram (the essence of love), aakekara-sthita-kaniinika-pakShma-netram (eyes with lashes curved at the corners), bhujaMga-shayaa-a~Nganaayaah (of the Consort of Him who reclines on the serpent).
Meaning: May that glance of the Consort of the Serpent-reclining Lord — gazing joyfully with half-closed, unblinking eyes upon Mukunda, the very root of bliss and essence of love, with lashes curved at the corners — become the cause of my prosperity.
Verse 5
बाह्वन्तरे मधुजितः श्रितकौस्तुभे या हारावलीव हरिनीलमयी विभाति ।
कामप्रदा भगवतोऽपि कटाक्षमाला कल्याणमावहतु मे कमलालयायाः ॥ ५॥
బాహ్వంతరే మధుజితః శ్రిత-కౌస్తుభే యా హారావలీవ హరి-నీలమయీ విభాతి ।
కామ-ప్రదా భగవతోఽపి కటాక్ష-మాలా కల్యాణమ్-ఆవహతు మే కమలాలయాయాః ॥ ౫॥
baahvantare madhujitaH shrita-kaustubhe yaa
haaraavaliiva hari-niila-mayii vibhaati
kaama-pradaa bhagavato.api kaTaaksha-maalaa
kalyaaNam-aavahatu me kamalaalayaayaaH || 5 ||
Words: baahvantare (between the arms), madhujitaH (of the slayer of Madhu), shrita-kaustubhe (near the Kaustubha gem), haaraavalii-iva (like a row of garlands), hari-niila-mayii (made of sapphires), kaama-pradaa (fulfiller of wishes), bhagavataH api (even of the Lord Himself), kaTaaksha-maalaa (the garland of glances), kamalaalayaayaaH (of Her who dwells in the lotus).
Meaning: Her row of glances, shining between the arms of Vishnu near the Kaustubha gem like a garland of blue sapphires — that garland of glances, which fulfils even the Lord's own wishes — may it, belonging to Lakshmi who dwells in the lotus, bring auspiciousness to me.
Verse 6
कालाम्बुदालिललितोरसि कैटभारेः धाराधरे स्फुरति या तडिदङ्गनेव ।
मातुस्समस्तजगतां महनीयमूर्तिः भद्राणि मे दिशतु भार्गवनन्दनायाः ॥ ६॥
కాలాంబుదాలి-లలితోరసి కైటభారేః ధారాధరే స్ఫురతి యా తడిద్-అంగనేవ ।
మాతుస్-సమస్త-జగతాం మహనీయ-మూర్తిః భద్రాణి మే దిశతు భార్గవ-నందనాయాః ॥ ౬॥
kaalaambudaali-lalitorasi kaiTabhaareH
dhaaraadhare sphurati yaa taDid-a~Nganeva
maatus-samasta-jagataaM mahaniiya-muurtiH
bhadraaNi me dishatu bhaargava-nandanaayaaH || 6 ||
Words: kaala-ambuda-aali-lalita-urasi (upon the dark, cloud-like lovely chest), kaiTabha-areH (of the foe of Kaitabha, Vishnu), dhaaraadhare (upon that raincloud-like chest), sphurati (flashes), taDid-a~Nganaa-iva (like a streak of lightning), maatuH samasta-jagataam (mother of all worlds), mahaniiya-muurtiH (venerable form), bhaargava-nandanaayaaH (of the daughter of Bhrigu).
Meaning: Upon the lovely, dark cloud-like chest of Vishnu, the foe of Kaitabha, she flashes like a streak of lightning. May that venerable form, mother of all worlds — Bhrigu's daughter Lakshmi — bestow all good fortune upon me.
Verse 7
प्राप्तं पदं प्रथमतः खलु यत्प्रभावात् माङ्गल्यभाजि मधुमाथिनि मन्मथेन ।
मय्यापतेत्तदिह मन्थरमीक्षणार्धं मन्दालसं च मकरालयकन्यकायाः ॥ ७॥
ప్రాప్తం పదం ప్రథమతః ఖలు యత్-ప్రభావాత్ మాంగల్య-భాజి మధు-మాథిని మన్మథేన ।
మయ్యాపతేత్-తద్-ఇహ మంథర-మీక్షణార్ధం మందాలసం చ మకరాలయ-కన్యకాయాః ॥ ౭॥
praaptaM padaM prathamataH khalu yat-prabhaavaat
maa~Ngalya-bhaaji madhu-maathini manmathena
mayyaapatet-tad-iha manthara-miikShaNaardhaM
mandaalasaM cha makaraalaya-kanyakaayaaH || 7 ||
Words: praaptam padam prathamataH (the position first attained), yat-prabhaavaat (by whose grace), maa~Ngalya-bhaaji madhu-maathini (in the auspicious heart of the slayer of Madhu), manmathena (by Manmatha, the god of love), manthara-iikShaNaardham (slow, gentle half-glance), mandaalasam (languid), makaraalaya-kanyakaayaaH (of the maiden born of the ocean).
Meaning: By whose grace Manmatha first attained his cherished place in the auspicious heart of Madhusudana — may that same slow, gentle, languid half-glance of the Ocean-born maiden fall upon me here.
Verse 8
दद्याद्दयानुपवनो द्रविणाम्बुधारां अस्मिन्नकिञ्चनविहङ्गशिशौ विषण्णे ।
दुष्कर्मघर्ममपनीय चिराय दूरं नारायणप्रणयिनीनयनाम्बुवाहः ॥ ८॥
దద్యాద్-దయానుపవనో ద్రవిణాంబు-ధారాం అస్మిన్న్-అకించన-విహంగ-శిశౌ విషణ్ణే ।
దుష్కర్మ-ఘర్మమ్-అపనీయ చిరాయ దూరం నారాయణ-ప్రణయినీ-నయనాంబు-వాహః ॥ ౮॥
dadyaad-dayaanupavano draviNaambu-dhaaraaM
asminn-aki~nchana-viha~Nga-shishau viShaNNe
duShkarma-gharmam-apaniiya chiraaya duuraM
naaraayaNa-praNayinii-nayanaambu-vaahaH || 8 ||
Words: dadyaat (may give), dayaa-anupavanaH (the gentle breeze of compassion), draviNa-ambu-dhaaraam (a shower of golden water/wealth), aki~nchana-viha~Nga-shishau (upon this poor, helpless fledgling), viShaNNe (distressed), duShkarma-gharmam (the heat of past misdeeds), apaniiya (removing), naaraayaNa-praNayinii-nayana-ambu-vaahaH (the raincloud that is the glance of Narayana's beloved).
Meaning: May the raincloud that is the glance of Narayana's beloved — the gentle breeze of compassion itself — bestow a shower of golden wealth upon this distressed, helpless fledgling, driving far away, for long, the scorching heat of past misdeeds.
Verse 9
इष्टाविशिष्टमतयोऽपि यया दयार्द्र- दृष्ट्या त्रिविष्टपपदं सुलभं लभन्ते ।
दृष्टिः प्रहृष्टकमलोदरदीप्तिरिष्टां पुष्टिं कृषीष्ट मम पुष्करविष्टरायाः ॥ ९॥
ఇష్టా-విశిష్ట-మతయోఽపి యయా దయార్ద్ర- దృష్ట్యా త్రి-విష్టప-పదం సులభం లభంతే ।
దృష్టిః ప్రహృష్ట-కమలోదర-దీప్తిర్-ఇష్టాం పుష్టిం కృషీష్ట మమ పుష్కర-విష్టరాయాః ॥ ౯॥
iShTaa vishiShTa-matayo.api yayaa dayaardra-
dR^iShTyaa triviShTapa-padaM sulabhaM labhante
dR^iShTiH prahR^iShTa-kamalodara-diiptir-iShTaaM
puShTiM kR^iShiiShTa mama puShkara-viShTaraayaaH || 9 ||
Words: vishiShTa-matayaH api (even the most learned/exalted), dayaardra-dR^iShTyaa (by whose compassion-laden glance), triviShTapa-padam (the state of heaven), sulabham labhante (easily attain), prahR^iShTa-kamalodara-diiptiH (radiant as the glow within a blooming lotus), puShkara-viShTaraayaaH (of Her who is seated on the lotus throne).
Meaning: By whose compassion-laden glance even the most exalted easily attain the state of heaven — may that glance, radiant as the glow within a blooming lotus, belonging to Lakshmi seated on the lotus throne, grant me the desired nourishment and prosperity.
Verse 10
गीर्देवतेति गरुडध्वजसुन्दरीति शाकम्भरीति शशिशेखरवल्लभेति ।
सृष्टिस्थितिप्रलयकेलिषु संस्थितायै तस्यै नमस्त्रिभुवनैकगुरोस्तरुण्यै ॥ १०॥
గీర్-దేవతేతి గరుడ-ధ్వజ-సుందరీతి శాకంభరీతి శశి-శేఖర-వల్లభేతి ।
సృష్టి-స్థితి-ప్రలయ-కేలిషు సంస్థితాయై తస్యై నమస్-త్రిభువనైక-గురోస్-తరుణ్యై ॥ ౧౦॥
giir-devateti garuDa-dhvaja-sundariiti
shaakambhariiti shashi-shekhara-vallabheti
sR^iShTi-sthiti-pralaya-keliShu saMsthitaayai
tasyai namas-tribhuvanaika-guros-taruNyai || 10 ||
Words: giir-devataa-iti (as the goddess of speech), garuDa-dhvaja-sundarii-iti (as the beauty beside the Garuda-bannered Vishnu), shaakambharii-iti (as Shakambhari), shashi-shekhara-vallabhaa-iti (as the beloved of the moon-crested Shiva), sR^iShTi-sthiti-pralaya-keliShu (in the play of creation, sustenance, and dissolution), tribhuvanaika-guroH taruNyai (to the eternally youthful Shakti of the sole Guru of the three worlds).
Meaning: Known variously as the goddess of speech, the beauty beside Garuda-bannered Vishnu, as Shakambhari, and as the beloved of moon-crested Shiva — She who abides through the divine play of creation, sustenance, and dissolution — salutations to that eternally youthful Shakti of the one sole Guru of the three worlds.
Verse 11
श्रुत्यै नमोऽस्तु शुभकर्मफलप्रसूत्यै रत्यै नमोऽस्तु रमणीयगुणार्णवायै ।
शक्त्यै नमोऽस्तु शतपत्रनिकेतनायै पुष्ट्यै नमोऽस्तु पुरुषोत्तमवल्लभायै ॥ ११॥
శ్రుత్యై నమోఽస్తు శుభ-కర్మ-ఫల-ప్రసూత్యై రత్యై నమోఽస్తు రమణీయ-గుణార్ణవాయై ।
శక్త్యై నమోఽస్తు శత-పత్ర-నికేతనాయై పుష్ట్యై నమోఽస్తు పురుషోత్తమ-వల్లభాయై ॥ ౧౧॥
shrutyai namo.astu shubha-karma-phala-prasuutyai
ratyai namo.astu ramaNiiya-guNaarNavaayai
shaktyai namo.astu shata-patra-niketanaayai
puShTyai namo.astu puruShottama-vallabhaayai || 11 ||
Words: shrutyai (to Her as Shruti/Vedic speech), shubha-karma-phala-prasuutyai (bestower of fruits of righteous deeds), ratyai (as Rati), ramaNiiya-guNa-arNavaayai (ocean of lovely virtues), shaktyai (as Shakti), shata-patra-niketanaayai (dwelling in the hundred-petalled lotus), puShTyai (as Pushti), puruShottama-vallabhaayai (beloved of Purushottama).
Meaning: Salutations to Her as Shruti, who bestows the fruits of righteous deeds; salutations to Her as Rati, the ocean of lovely virtues; salutations to Her as Shakti, dwelling in the hundred-petalled lotus; salutations to Her as Pushti, the beloved of Purushottama.
Verse 12
नमोऽस्तु नालीकनिभाननायै नमोऽस्तु दुग्धोदधिजन्मभूम्यै ।
नमोऽस्तु सोमामृतसोदरायै नमोऽस्तु नारायणवल्लभायै ॥ १२॥
నమోఽస్తు నాలీక-నిభాననాయై నమోఽస్తు దుగ్ధోదధి-జన్మ-భూమ్యై ।
నమోఽస్తు సోమామృత-సోదరాయై నమోఽస్తు నారాయణ-వల్లభాయై ॥ ౧౨॥
namo.astu naaliika-nibhaananaayai
namo.astu dugdhodadhi-janma-bhuumyai
namo.astu somaamR^ita-sodaraayai
namo.astu naaraayaNa-vallabhaayai || 12 ||
Words: naaliika-nibha-aananaayai (whose face resembles a lotus), dugdha-udadhi-janma-bhuumyai (whose birthplace is the ocean of milk), soma-amR^ita-sodaraayai (sister of the moon and nectar), naaraayaNa-vallabhaayai (beloved of Narayana).
Meaning: Salutations to Her whose face resembles a lotus; salutations to Her whose birthplace is the ocean of milk; salutations to Her who is sister to the moon and to nectar, both born together from that churning; salutations to Her, the beloved of Narayana.
Verse 13
सम्पत्कराणि सकलेन्द्रियनन्दनानि साम्राज्यदानविभवानि सरोरुहाक्षि ।
त्वद्वन्दनानि दुरिताहरणोद्यतानि मामेव मातरनिशं कलयन्तु मान्ये ॥ १३॥
సంపత్కరాణి సకలేంద్రియ-నందనాని సామ్రాజ్య-దాన-విభవాని సరోరుహాక్షి ।
త్వద్-వందనాని దురితాహరణోద్యతాని మామ్-ఏవ మాతర్-అనిశం కలయంతు మాన్యే ॥ ౧౩॥
sampatkaraaNi sakalendriya-nandanaani
saamraajya-daana-vibhavaani saroruhaakShi
tvad-vandanaani duritaaharaNodyataani
maam-eva maatar-anishaM kalayantu maanye || 13 ||
Words: sampatkaraaNi (bestowers of prosperity), sakala-indriya-nandanaani (delighting every sense), saamraajya-daana-vibhavaani (bestowing the splendour of an empire), saroruha-akShi (O lotus-eyed one), tvad-vandanaani (salutations to You), durita-aharaNa-udyataani (ever ready to remove sin), maatar (O Mother), anisham kalayantu (may they keep always).
Meaning: O lotus-eyed Mother, may these salutations to You — which bring prosperity, delight every sense, bestow the splendour of an empire, and are ever ready to remove sin — keep me always within Your gracious remembrance, O venerable one.
Verse 14
यत्कटाक्षसमुपासनाविधिः सेवकस्य सकलार्थसम्पदः ।
सन्तनोति वचनाङ्गमानसैः त्वां मुरारिहृदयेश्वरीं भजे ॥ १४॥
యత్-కటాక్ష-సముపాసనా-విధిః సేవకస్య సకలార్థ-సంపదః ।
సంతనోతి వచనాంగ-మానసైః త్వాం మురారి-హృదయేశ్వరీం భజే ॥ ౧౪॥
yat-kaTaaksha-samupaasanaa-vidhiH
sevakasya sakala-artha-sampadaH
santanoti vachanaa~Nga-maanasaiH
tvaaM muraari-hR^idayeshvariiM bhaje || 14 ||
Words: yat-kaTaaksha-samupaasanaa-vidhiH (the discipline of worshipping whose sidelong glance), sevakasya (of the devotee), sakala-artha-sampadaH (all manner of wealth and fulfilment), santanoti (bestows), vachana-a~Nga-maanasaiH (in thought, word, and deed), muraari-hR^idaya-iishvariiM (the Queen who reigns over Murari's heart), bhaje (I worship).
Meaning: Her sidelong glance, when worshipped with devotion in thought, word, and deed, bestows upon the devotee every kind of wealth and fulfilment. I worship You, the Queen who reigns over Murari's heart.
Verse 15
सरसिजनिलये सरोजहस्ते धवलतमांशुकगन्धमाल्यशोभे ।
भगवति हरिवल्लभे मनोज्ञे त्रिभुवनभूतिकरि प्रसीद मह्यम् ॥ १५॥
సరసిజ-నిలయే సరోజ-హస్తే ధవలతమాంశుక-గంధ-మాల్య-శోభే ।
భగవతి హరి-వల్లభే మనోజ్ఞే త్రిభువన-భూతికరి ప్రసీద మహ్యమ్ ॥ ౧౫॥
sarasija-nilaye saroja-haste
dhavalatam-aaMshuka-gandha-maalya-shobhe
bhagavati hari-vallabhe manoj~ne
tribhuvana-bhuutikari prasiida mahyam || 15 ||
Words: sarasija-nilaye (dwelling upon the lotus), saroja-haste (holding a lotus in hand), dhavalatama-aaMshuka-gandha-maalya-shobhe (resplendent with pure white garments, fragrance, and garlands), hari-vallabhe (beloved of Hari), manoj~ne (enchanting), tribhuvana-bhuutikari (bestower of prosperity on the three worlds), prasiida mahyam (be gracious unto me).
Meaning: O Goddess dwelling upon the lotus, holding a lotus in hand, resplendent with pure white garments, fragrance, and garlands, most enchanting, beloved of Hari, bestower of prosperity upon the three worlds — be gracious unto me.
Verse 16
दिग्घस्तिभिः कनककुम्भमुखावसृष्ट- स्वर्वाहिनीविमलचारुजलप्लुताङ्गीम् ।
प्रातर्नमामि जगतां जननीमशेष- लोकाधिनाथगृहिणीममृताब्धिपुत्रीम् ॥ १६॥
దిగ్-ఘస్తిభిః కనక-కుంభ-ముఖావసృష్ట- స్వర్వాహినీ-విమల-చారు-జల-ప్లుతాంగీమ్ ।
ప్రాతర్-నమామి జగతాం జననీమ్-అశేష- లోకాధినాథ-గృహిణీమ్-అమృతాబ్ధి-పుత్రీమ్ ॥ ౧౬॥
dig-ghastibhiH kanaka-kumbha-mukhaavasR^iShTa-
svarvaahinii-vimala-chaaru-jala-plutaa~Ngiim
praatar-namaami jagataaM jananiim-ashesha-
lokaadhinaatha-gR^ihiNiim-amR^itaabdhi-putriim || 16 ||
Words: dig-ghastibhiH (by the guardian elephants of the directions), kanaka-kumbha-mukha-avasR^iShTa (poured from the mouths of golden pitchers), svarvaahinii-vimala-chaaru-jala-plutaa~Ngiim (whose body is bathed in the pure, lovely waters of the celestial Ganga), jananiim jagataam (mother of the worlds), ashesha-lokaadhinaatha-gR^ihiNiim (consort of the Lord of all worlds), amR^ita-abdhi-putriim (daughter of the ocean of nectar).
Meaning: Each morning I bow to the Mother of the worlds — whose body is bathed by the guardian elephants of the directions with the pure, lovely waters of the celestial Ganga poured from golden pitchers — consort of the Lord of all worlds, daughter of the ocean of nectar.
Verse 17
कमले कमलाक्षवल्लभे त्वं करुणापूरतरङ्गितैरपाङ्गैः ।
अवलोकय मामकिञ्चनानां प्रथमं पात्रमकृत्रिमं दयायाः ॥ १७॥
కమలే కమలాక్ష-వల్లభే త్వం కరుణా-పూర-తరంగితైర్-అపాంగైః ।
అవలోకయ మామ్-అకించనానాం ప్రథమం పాత్రమ్-అకృత్రిమం దయాయాః ॥ ౧౭॥
kamale kamalaaksha-vallabhe tvaM
karuNaa-puura-tara~NgitaiH-apaa~NgaiH
avalokaya maam-aki~nchanaanaaM
prathamaM paatram-akR^itrimaM dayaayaaH || 17 ||
Words: kamale (O Kamala/Lakshmi), kamalaaksha-vallabhe (beloved of the lotus-eyed Vishnu), karuNaa-puura-tara~NgitaiH apaa~NgaiH (with side-glances surging with waves of compassion), avalokaya maam (look upon me), aki~nchanaanaam prathamam paatram (the foremost recipient among the destitute), akR^itrimam dayaayaaH (of Your natural, unfeigned mercy).
Meaning: O Kamala, beloved of the lotus-eyed Vishnu, look upon me with Your side-glances surging with waves of compassion — regard me as the very first, most deserving recipient of Your natural, unfeigned mercy toward the destitute.
Verse 18
स्तुवन्ति ये स्तुतिभिरमीभिरन्वहं त्रयीमयीं त्रिभुवनमातरं रमाम् ।
गुणाधिका गुरुतरभाग्यभागिनो भवन्ति ते भुवि बुधभाविताशयाः ॥ १८॥
స్తువంతి యే స్తుతిభిర్-అమీభిర్-అన్వహం త్రయీ-మయీం త్రిభువన-మాతరం రమామ్ ।
గుణాధికా గురుతర-భాగ్య-భాగినో భవంతి తే భువి బుధ-భావితాశయాః ॥ ౧౮॥
stuvanti ye stutibhir-amiibhir-anvahaM
trayii-mayiiM tribhuvana-maataraM ramaam
guNaadhikaa gurutara-bhaagya-bhaagino
bhavanti te bhuvi budha-bhaavitaashayaaH || 18 ||
Words: stuvanti ye (those who praise), stutibhiH amiibhiH anvaham (daily with these very hymns), trayii-mayiim (of the essence of the three Vedas), tribhuvana-maataram ramaam (Rama, mother of the three worlds), guNa-adhikaa (superior in virtue), gurutara-bhaagya-bhaaginaH (partakers of the greatest fortune), budha-bhaavita-aashayaaH (whose minds are cherished by the wise).
Meaning: Those who daily praise, with these very hymns, Rama (Lakshmi) — who is the essence of the three Vedas and mother of the three worlds — become superior in virtue and partake of the greatest fortune on this earth, their minds ever cherished by the wise.
Verse 19
नमोऽस्तु हेमाम्बुजपीठिकायै नमोऽस्तु भूमण्डलनायिकायै ।
नमोऽस्तु देवादिदयापरायै नमोऽस्तु शार्ङ्गायुधवल्लभायै ॥ १९॥
నమోఽస్తు హేమాంబుజ-పీఠికాయై నమోఽస్తు భూమండల-నాయికాయై ।
నమోఽస్తు దేవాది-దయా-పరాయై నమోఽస్తు శార్ంగాయుధ-వల్లభాయై ॥ ౧౯॥
namo.astu hemaambuja-piiThikaayai
namo.astu bhuumaNDala-naayikaayai
namo.astu devaadi-dayaa-paraayai
namo.astu shaarNgaayudha-vallabhaayai || 19 ||
Words: hema-ambuja-piiThikaayai (seated on a throne of golden lotus), bhuumaNDala-naayikaayai (mistress of the whole earth), deva-aadi-dayaa-paraayai (ever compassionate toward the gods and all beings), shaarNga-aayudha-vallabhaayai (beloved of the wielder of the Sharnga bow).
Meaning: Salutations to Her who is seated on a throne of golden lotus; salutations to Her who is mistress of the whole earth; salutations to Her who is ever compassionate toward the gods and all beings; salutations to Her, the beloved of the wielder of the Sharnga bow.
Verse 20
नमोऽस्तु देव्यै भृगुनन्दनायै नमोऽस्तु विष्णोरुरसि स्थितायै ।
नमोऽस्तु लक्ष्म्यै कमलालयायै नमोऽस्तु दामोदरवल्लभायै ॥ २०॥
నమోఽస్తు దేవ్యై భృగు-నందనాయై నమోఽస్తు విష్ణోర్-ఉరసి స్థితాయై ।
నమోఽస్తు లక్ష్మ్యై కమలాలయాయై నమోఽస్తు దామోదర-వల్లభాయై ॥ ౨౦॥
namo.astu devyai bhR^igu-nandanaayai
namo.astu viShNor-urasi sthitaayai
namo.astu lakShmyai kamalaalayaayai
namo.astu daamodara-vallabhaayai || 20 ||
Words: devyai bhR^igu-nandanaayai (Goddess born in the line of sage Bhrigu), viShNoH urasi sthitaayai (residing on Vishnu's chest), lakShmyai kamalaalayaayai (Lakshmi, dwelling in the lotus), daamodara-vallabhaayai (beloved of Damodara).
Meaning: Salutations to the Goddess born in the line of sage Bhrigu; salutations to Her who ever resides upon the chest of Vishnu; salutations to Lakshmi, who dwells in the lotus; salutations to Her, the beloved of Damodara.
Verse 21
नमोऽस्तु कान्त्यै कमलेक्षणायै नमोऽस्तु भूत्यै भुवनप्रसूत्यै ।
नमोऽस्तु देवादिभिरर्चितायै नमोऽस्तु नन्दात्मजवल्लभायै ॥ २१॥
నమోఽస్తు కాంత్యై కమలేక్షణాయై నమోఽస్తు భూత్యై భువన-ప్రసూత్యై ।
నమోఽస్తు దేవాదిభిర్-అర్చితాయై నమోఽస్తు నందాత్మజ-వల్లభాయై ॥ ౨౧॥
namo.astu kaantyai kamalekShaNaayai
namo.astu bhuutyai bhuvana-prasuutyai
namo.astu devaadibhir-architaayai
namo.astu nandaatmaja-vallabhaayai || 21 ||
Words: kaantyai kamala-iikShaNaayai (as Beauty itself, lotus-eyed), bhuutyai bhuvana-prasuutyai (as Opulence, mother of all worlds), deva-aadibhiH architaayai (worshipped by all the gods), nanda-aatmaja-vallabhaayai (beloved of Nanda's son, Krishna).
Meaning: Salutations to Her who is Beauty itself, lotus-eyed; salutations to Her who is Opulence, the mother of all worlds; salutations to Her who is worshipped by all the gods; salutations to Her, the beloved of Nanda's son, Krishna.
