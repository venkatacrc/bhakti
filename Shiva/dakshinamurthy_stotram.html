Sri Dakshinamurthy Stotram

श्री दक्षिणामूर्ति स्तोत्रम् — Complete in Sanskrit, Telugu, English with Meaning.

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya

Dhyanam

ॐ नमः प्रणवार्थाय शुद्धज्ञानैकमूर्तये ।
निर्मलाय प्रशान्ताय दक्षिणामूर्तये नमः ॥
ఓం నమః ప్రణవార్థాయ శుద్ధజ్ఞానైకమూర్తయే ।
నిర్మలాయ ప్రశాంతాయ దక్షిణామూర్తయే నమః ॥
oM namaH praNavaarthaaya shuddhaj~naanaika-muurtaye
nirmalaaya prashaantaaya dakShiNaamuurtaye namaH
Words: praNava-arthaaya (to the meaning of Om), shuddha-j~naana-eka-muurtaye (sole embodiment of pure knowledge), nirmalaaya (spotless), prashaantaaya (utterly tranquil), dakShiNaamuurtaye (to Dakshinamurthy), namaH (salutations).
Meaning: Salutations to the meaning of the syllable Om, the sole embodiment of pure knowledge, spotless and utterly tranquil — salutations to Sri Dakshinamurthy.

The Ten Verses

Verse 1
विश्वं दर्पणदृश्यमाननगरीतुल्यं निजान्तर्गतं पश्यन्नात्मनि मायया बहिरिवोद्भूतं यथा निद्रया ।
यः साक्षात्कुरुते प्रबोधसमये स्वात्मानमेवाद्वयं तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ १॥
విశ్వం దర్పణదృశ్యమాననగరీతుల్యం నిజాంతర్గతం పశ్యన్నాత్మని మాయయా బహిరివోద్భూతం యథా నిద్రయా ।
యః సాక్షాత్కురుతే ప్రబోధసమయే స్వాత్మానమేవాద్వయం తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౧॥
vishvaM darpaNa-dR^ishyamaana-nagarii-tulyaM nijaantar-gataM
pashyann-aatmani maayayaa bahir-ivodbhuutaM yathaa nidrayaa
yaH saakShaat-kurute prabodha-samaye svaatmaanam-evaadvayaM
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 1 ||
Words: vishvam (the universe), darpaNa-dR^ishyamaana-nagarii-tulyam (like a city seen in a mirror), nijaantar-gatam (existing within one's own Self), pashyan (seeing), aatmani (in the Self), maayayaa (by maya), bahiH-iva-udbhutam (as if arisen outside), yathaa nidrayaa (as in a dream), saakShaat-kurute (directly realizes), prabodha-samaye (at the time of awakening), svaatmaanam-eva-advayam (one's own non-dual Self), shrii-guru-muurtaye (to the form of the divine Guru), namaH (salutations).
Meaning: To Him who sees the universe — like a city reflected in a mirror — as existing within His own Self, yet appearing external through maya, as in a dream; who at the moment of awakening directly realizes His own non-dual Self — to that Guru-formed Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 2
बीजस्यान्तरिवाङ्कुरो जगदिदं प्राङ्निर्विकल्पं पुनः मायाकल्पितदेशकालकलनावैचित्र्यचित्रीकृतम् ।
मायावीव विजृम्भयत्यपि महायोगीव यः स्वेच्छया तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ २॥
బీజస్యాంతరివాంకురో జగదిదం ప్రాఙ్నిర్వికల్పం పునః మాయాకల్పితదేశకాలకలనావైచిత్ర్యచిత్రీకృతమ్ ।
మాయావీవ విజృంభయత్యపి మహాయోగీవ యః స్వేచ్ఛయా తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౨॥
biijasyaantar-ivaa~nkuro jagad-idaM praa~N-nirvikalpaM punaH
maayaa-kalpita-desha-kaala-kalanaa-vaichitrya-chitriikR^itam
maayaaviiva vijR^imbhayaty-api mahaayogiiva yaH svechchhayaa
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 2 ||
Words: biijasya antar-iva a~nkuraH (like a sprout hidden within a seed), jagad-idam (this universe), praak-nirvikalpam (formerly undifferentiated), punaH (again), maayaa-kalpita-desha-kaala-kalanaa-vaichitrya-chitriikR^itam (rendered wondrously varied by the maya-conceived play of space, time and causation), maayaavii-iva (like a magician), mahaa-yogii-iva (like a great yogi), svechchhayaa (by His own free will), vijR^imbhayati (unfolds/manifests).
Meaning: To Him who, like a sprout hidden within a seed, unfolds this universe — formerly undifferentiated — into the wondrous variety fashioned by maya's play of space, time, and causation, manifesting it of His own free will as would a magician or a great yogi — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 3
यस्यैव स्फुरणं सदात्मकमसत्कल्पार्थगं भासते साक्षात्तत्त्वमसीति वेदवचसा यो बोधयत्याश्रितान् ।
यत्साक्षात्करणाद्भवेन्न पुनरावृत्तिर्भवाम्भोनिधौ तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ३॥
యస్యైవ స్ఫురణం సదాత్మకమసత్కల్పార్థగం భాసతే సాక్షాత్తత్త్వమసీతి వేదవచసా యో బోధయత్యాశ్రితాన్ ।
యత్సాక్షాత్కరణాద్భవేన్న పునరావృత్తిర్భవాంభోనిధౌ తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౩॥
yasyaiva sphuraNaM sadaatmakam-asat-kalpaartha-gaM bhaasate
saakShaat-tattvam-asiiti veda-vachasaa yo bodhayaty-aashritaan
yat-saakShaat-karaNaad-bhaven-na punaraavR^ittir-bhavaambhonidhau
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 3 ||
Words: yasya sphuraNam (whose very self-luminous existence), sat-aatmakam (real in nature), asat-kalpa-artha-gam (giving apparent reality to the unreal), bhaasate (shines forth), saakShaat-tattvam-asi-iti (directly as "That thou art"), veda-vachasaa (through the Vedic dictum), aashritaan (surrendered devotees), bodhayati (instructs), yat-saakShaat-karaNaat (by whose direct realization), na punaH-aavR^ittiH (no more return), bhava-ambhonidhau (in the ocean of samsara).
Meaning: To Him whose very existence, real in nature, lends apparent reality to the unreal world; who, through the Vedic pronouncement "Tat Tvam Asi" (That thou art), directly instructs His surrendered devotees; by whose direct realization there is no more return to the ocean of samsara — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 4
नानाच्छिद्रघटोदरस्थितमहादीपप्रभाभास्वरं ज्ञानं यस्य तु चक्षुरादिकरणद्वारा बहिः स्पन्दते ।
जानामीति तमेव भान्तमनुभात्येतत्समस्तं जगत् तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ४॥
నానాచ్ఛిద్రఘటోదరస్థితమహాదీపప్రభాభాస్వరం జ్ఞానం యస్య తు చక్షురాదికరణద్వారా బహిః స్పందతే ।
జానామీతి తమేవ భాంతమనుభాత్యేతత్సమస్తం జగత్ తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౪॥
naanaa-cchidra-ghaTodara-sthita-mahaa-diipa-prabhaa-bhaasvaraM
j~naanaM yasya tu chakShur-aadi-karaNa-dvaaraa bahiH spandate
jaanaamiiti tam-eva bhaantam-anubhaaty-etat-samastaM jagat
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 4 ||
Words: naanaa-chhidra-ghaTa-udara-sthita-mahaa-diipa-prabhaa-bhaasvaram (radiant like a great lamp placed inside a pot with many holes), j~naanam (knowledge/awareness), chakShuH-aadi-karaNa-dvaaraa (through the gateways of the eye and other senses), bahiH spandate (vibrates/radiates outward), jaanaami-iti (as "I know"), tam-eva bhaantam (that very shining One), anubhaati (shines after/reflects), etat samastam jagat (this entire universe).
Meaning: To Him whose awareness, radiant like a great lamp placed within a many-holed pot, radiates outward through the gateways of the eye and other senses as "I know"; by whose shining alone this entire universe appears to shine — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 5
देहं प्राणमपीन्द्रियाण्यपि चलां बुद्धिं च शून्यं विदुः स्त्रीबालान्धजडोपमास्त्वहमिति भ्रान्ता भृशं वादिनः ।
मायाशक्तिविलासकल्पितमहाव्यामोहसंहारिणे तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ५॥
దేహం ప్రాణమపీంద్రియాణ్యపి చలాం బుద్ధిం చ శూన్యం విదుః స్త్రీబాలాంధజడోపమాస్త్వహమితి భ్రాంతా భృశం వాదినః ।
మాయాశక్తివిలాసకల్పితమహావ్యామోహసంహారిణే తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౫॥
dehaM praaNam-apiindriyaaNy-api chalaaM buddhiM cha shuunyaM viduH
strii-baalaandha-jaDopamaas-tv-aham-iti bhraantaa bhR^ishaM vaadinaH
maayaa-shakti-vilaasa-kalpita-mahaa-vyaamoha-saMhaariNe
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 5 ||
Words: deham (body), praaNam (vital breath), indriyaaNi (senses), chalaam buddhim (the wavering intellect), shuunyam (a void), viduH (they consider), strii-baala-andha-jaDa-upamaaH (comparable to women, children, the blind, the dull), aham-iti (as "I"), bhraantaa (deluded), bhR^isham vaadinaH (vehement disputers), maayaa-shakti-vilaasa-kalpita-mahaa-vyaamoha (the great delusion fashioned by the sport of maya's power), saMhaariNe (to the destroyer of).
Meaning: To Him who destroys the great delusion — fashioned by the sport of maya's power — of those deluded disputers who, comparable to women, children, the blind, and the dull, mistakenly claim the body, breath, senses, wavering intellect, or a mere void to be "I" — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 6
राहुग्रस्तदिवाकरेन्दुसदृशो मायासमाच्छादनात् सन्मात्रः करणोपसंहरणतो योऽभूत्सुषुप्तः पुमान् ।
प्रागस्वाप्समिति प्रबोधसमये यः प्रत्यभिज्ञायते तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ६॥
రాహుగ్రస్తదివాకరేందుసదృశో మాయాసమాచ్ఛాదనాత్ సన్మాత్రః కరణోపసంహరణతో యోఽభూత్సుషుప్తః పుమాన్ ।
ప్రాగస్వాప్సమితి ప్రబోధసమయే యః ప్రత్యభిజ్ఞాయతే తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౬॥
raahu-grasta-divaakarendu-sadR^isho maayaa-samaachchhaadanaat
sanmaatraH karaNopasaMharaNato yo.abhuut-suShuptaH pumaan
praag-asvaapsam-iti prabodha-samaye yaH pratyabhij~naayate
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 6 ||
Words: raahu-grasta-divaakara-indu-sadR^ishaH (like the sun or moon eclipsed by Rahu), maayaa-samaachchhaadanaat (due to the covering of maya), sanmaatraH (pure existence alone), karaNa-upasaMharaNataH (by the withdrawal of the senses), suShuptaH pumaan (the person in deep sleep), praak-asvaapsam-iti (as "I was asleep before"), prabodha-samaye (at the time of waking), pratyabhij~naayate (is recognized again).
Meaning: To Him who, like the sun or moon eclipsed by Rahu, becomes covered by maya and, with the senses withdrawn, remains as pure existence alone during deep sleep, and is recognized again upon waking as "I was asleep" — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 7
बाल्यादिष्वपि जाग्रदादिषु तथा सर्वास्ववस्थास्वपि व्यावृत्तास्वनुवर्तमानमहमित्यन्तः स्फुरन्तं सदा ।
स्वात्मानं प्रकटीकरोति भजतां यो मुद्रया भद्रया तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ७॥
బాల్యాదిష్వపి జాగ్రదాదిషు తథా సర్వాస్వవస్థాస్వపి వ్యావృత్తాస్వనువర్తమానమహమిత్యంతః స్ఫురంతం సదా ।
స్వాత్మానం ప్రకటీకరోతి భజతాం యో ముద్రయా భద్రయా తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౭॥
baalyaadiShv-api jaagrad-aadiShu tathaa sarvaasv-avasthaasv-api
vyaavR^ittaasv-anuvartamaanam-aham-ity-antaH sphurantaM sadaa
svaatmaanaM prakaTiikaroti bhajataaM yo mudrayaa bhadrayaa
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 7 ||
Words: baalya-aadiShu (in childhood and other stages of life), jaagrat-aadiShu (in waking and other states), sarva-avasthaasu-api (in all states whatsoever), vyaavR^ittaasu (though they change), anuvartamaanam (persisting continuously), aham-iti (as "I"), antaH sphurantam (shining within), svaatmaanam (one's own Self), prakaTii-karoti (reveals), bhajataam (to those who worship), mudrayaa bhadrayaa (through the auspicious gesture, chinmudra).
Meaning: To Him who, through the auspicious chinmudra gesture, reveals to worshippers their own Self — which shines within continuously as "I" through childhood and every stage of life, through waking, dream, and deep sleep, unbroken across all the changing states — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 8
विश्वं पश्यति कार्यकारणतया स्वस्वामिसम्बन्धतः शिष्याचार्यतया तथैव पितृपुत्राद्यात्मना भेदतः ।
स्वप्ने जाग्रति वा य एष पुरुषो मायापरिभ्रामितः तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ८॥
విశ్వం పశ్యతి కార్యకారణతయా స్వస్వామిసంబంధతః శిష్యాచార్యతయా తథైవ పితృపుత్రాద్యాత్మనా భేదతః ।
స్వప్నే జాగ్రతి వా య ఏష పురుషో మాయాపరిభ్రామితః తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౮॥
vishvaM pashyati kaarya-kaaraNatayaa sva-svaami-sambandhataH
shiShyaachaaryatayaa tathaiva pitR^i-putraady-aatmanaa bhedataH
svapne jaagrati vaa ya eSha puruSho maayaa-paribhraamitaH
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 8 ||
Words: vishvam pashyati (the world is seen), kaarya-kaaraNatayaa (as cause and effect), sva-svaami-sambandhataH (in the relation of servant and master), shiShya-aachaaryatayaa (as disciple and teacher), pitR^i-putra-aadi-aatmanaa (as father, son and so on), bhedataH (as differences), svapne jaagrati vaa (whether in dream or in waking), eSha puruShaH (this person), maayaa-paribhraamitaH (deluded/confused by maya).
Meaning: To Him owing to whom this person, confused by maya whether in dream or in waking, sees the world in terms of cause and effect, master and servant, teacher and disciple, father and son, and other such distinctions — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 9
भूरम्भांस्यनलोऽनिलोऽम्बरमहर्नाथो हिमांशुः पुमान् इत्याभाति चराचरात्मकमिदं यस्यैव मूर्त्यष्टकम् ।
नान्यत्किञ्चन विद्यते विमृशतां यस्मात्परस्माद्विभोः तस्मै श्रीगुरुमूर्तये नम इदं श्रीदक्षिणामूर्तये ॥ ९॥
భూరంభాంస్యనలోఽనిలోఽంబరమహర్నాథో హిమాంశుః పుమాన్ ఇత్యాభాతి చరాచరాత్మకమిదం యస్యైవ మూర్త్యష్టకమ్ ।
నాన్యత్కించన విద్యతే విమృశతాం యస్మాత్పరస్మాద్విభోః తస్మై శ్రీగురుమూర్తయే నమ ఇదం శ్రీదక్షిణామూర్తయే ॥ ౯॥
bhuur-ambhaaMsy-analo.anilo.ambaram-ahar-naatho himaaMshuH pumaan
ity-aabhaati charaachar-aatmakam-idaM yasyaiva muurty-aShTakam
na-anyat-ki~nchana vidyate vimR^ishataaM yasmaat-parasmaad-vibhoH
tasmai shrii-guru-muurtaye nama idaM shrii-dakShiNaamuurtaye || 9 ||
Words: bhuuH (earth), ambhaaMsi (water), analaH (fire), anilaH (air), ambaram (sky/ether), ahar-naathaH (lord of the day, the sun), himaaMshuH (the moon), pumaan (the individual soul), muurti-aShTakam (the eightfold form), charaachara-aatmakam (comprising the moving and unmoving universe), na-anyat-ki~nchana vidyate (nothing else exists), vimR^ishataam (for those who reflect deeply), parasmaad-vibhoH (than that Supreme Lord).
Meaning: To Him whose eightfold form — earth, water, fire, air, ether, sun, moon, and the individual soul — thus appears as this entire universe of the moving and unmoving; for those who reflect deeply, nothing else exists apart from that Supreme, all-pervading Lord — to that Dakshinamurthy, this salutation.
Verse 10
सर्वात्मत्वमिति स्फुटीकृतमिदं यस्मादमुष्मिन् स्तवे तेनास्य श्रवणात्तदर्थमननाद्ध्यानाच्च सङ्कीर्तनात् ।
सर्वात्मत्वमहाविभूतिसहितं स्यादीश्वरत्वं स्वतः सिद्ध्येत्तत्पुनरष्टधा परिणतं चैश्वर्यमव्याहतम् ॥ १०॥
సర్వాత్మత్వమితి స్ఫుటీకృతమిదం యస్మాదముష్మిన్ స్తవే తేనాస్య శ్రవణాత్తదర్థమననాద్ధ్యానాచ్చ సంకీర్తనాత్ ।
సర్వాత్మత్వమహావిభూతిసహితం స్యాదీశ్వరత్వం స్వతః సిద్ధ్యేత్తత్పునరష్టధా పరిణతం చైశ్వర్యమవ్యాహతమ్ ॥ ౧౦॥
sarvaatmatvam-iti sphuTiikR^itam-idaM yasmaad-amuShmin stave
tenaasya shravaNaat-tad-artha-mananaad-dhyaanaach-cha sa~Nkiirtanaat
sarvaatmatva-mahaa-vibhuuti-sahitaM syaad-iishvaratvaM svataH
siddhyet-tat-punar-aShTadhaa pariNataM ch-aishvaryam-avyaahatam || 10 ||
Words: sarvaatmatvam (the truth of being the Self of all), sphuTii-kR^itam (clearly made evident), amuShmin stave (in this hymn), asya shravaNaat (by hearing it), tad-artha-mananaat (by reflecting on its meaning), dhyaanaat (by meditating), sa~Nkiirtanaat (by chanting), mahaa-vibhuuti-sahitam (together with the great glory), iishvaratvam (lordliness/divine sovereignty), svataH siddhyet (arises spontaneously), aShTadhaa pariNatam (transformed eightfold), aishvaryam avyaahatam (unimpeded sovereignty).
Meaning: Since the truth of being the Self of all is clearly revealed in this hymn, by hearing it, reflecting on its meaning, meditating upon it, and chanting it, the state of divine sovereignty together with its great glory arises spontaneously, and further, the eightfold, unimpeded lordly powers (ashta-siddhis) are attained.

Thus ends the Sri Dakshinamurthy Stotram composed by Sri Shankaracharya.
